Food in the Prophetic Sunnah

How the Prophet ﷺ ate and drank, the foods he loved, and what was reported about healing with certain foods. Next to each hadith is its source and its grade according to hadith scholars, and at the end of the page are famous sayings that are not authentic.

Etiquette of eating

«Young man, say Allah's name, eat with your right hand, and eat from what is nearest to you»

Agreed upon (Bukhari and Muslim) Sahih

He said it to Umar ibn Abi Salamah when he was a child.

«When one of you eats, let him mention Allah's name; if he forgets to mention it at the beginning, let him say: In the name of Allah at its beginning and its end»

Narrated by Abu Dawud and Tirmidhi; graded sahih by al-Albani Sahih

«The Prophet ﷺ never criticised any food: if he liked it he ate it, and if he disliked it he left it»

Agreed upon (Bukhari and Muslim) Sahih

«I do not eat while reclining»

Narrated by Bukhari Sahih

He sat to eat in a humble posture.

The Prophet ﷺ ate with three fingers and licked his hand before wiping it

Narrated by Muslim Sahih

«If a morsel falls from one of you, let him remove any dirt from it and eat it, and not leave it for Satan»

Narrated by Muslim Sahih

A basis for not wasting food.

«Food for two is enough for three, and food for three is enough for four»

Agreed upon (Bukhari and Muslim) Sahih

«Gather together over your food and mention Allah's name over it, and it will be blessed for you»

Narrated by Abu Dawud and Ibn Majah Disputed

Graded hasan by al-Albani, and weak by some hadith scholars because some narrators are unknown.

«No human fills a vessel worse than his stomach. A few morsels to keep his back straight are enough for the son of Adam; if he must, then a third for his food, a third for his drink and a third for his breath»

Narrated by Tirmidhi and Ibn Majah; graded sahih by al-Albani Sahih

The basis of moderation in eating.

«The believer eats in one intestine, and the disbeliever eats in seven intestines»

Agreed upon (Bukhari and Muslim) Sahih

Scholars understood it as encouragement towards contentment and eating little.

«Allah is pleased with the servant who eats a meal and praises Him for it, or drinks a drink and praises Him for it»

Narrated by Muslim Sahih

«Whoever eats food and then says: Praise be to Allah who fed me this and provided it for me without any power or strength on my part, his past sins are forgiven»

Narrated by Abu Dawud, Tirmidhi and Ibn Majah; graded hasan by Tirmidhi and al-Albani Hasan

«Whoever goes to sleep with grease on his hand and has not washed it, and something harms him, let him blame no one but himself»

Narrated by Abu Dawud and Tirmidhi; graded sahih by al-Albani Sahih

The grease meant is the fat and smell of meat. It is a basis for washing the hands after eating.

«Whoever eats garlic or onion, let him keep away from us, or keep away from our mosque»

Agreed upon (Bukhari and Muslim) Sahih

It does not forbid them, and Umar said: whoever eats them, let him kill their smell by cooking (narrated by Muslim).

Etiquette of drinking

«When one of you drinks, let him not breathe into the vessel»

Agreed upon (Bukhari and Muslim) Sahih

The Prophet ﷺ forbade breathing into the vessel or blowing into it

Narrated by Abu Dawud and Tirmidhi; graded sahih by Tirmidhi Sahih

The Prophet ﷺ would breathe three times while drinking and say: «It is more thirst-quenching, healthier and more wholesome»

Narrated by Muslim Sahih

That is, he drank in three sips, moving the vessel away from his mouth between them.

The Prophet ﷺ forbade a man to drink standing

Narrated by Muslim Sahih

It is established that he ﷺ drank Zamzam standing (agreed upon), so most scholars took the prohibition as dislike, not prohibition.

«Cover the vessel and tie up the waterskin»

Narrated by Muslim Sahih

That is, cover food containers and tie water skins, which protects from insects and contamination.

«Do not drink from vessels of gold and silver, and do not eat from their plates»

Agreed upon (Bukhari and Muslim) Sahih

«The drink the Messenger of Allah ﷺ loved most was the sweet and cold»

Narrated by Tirmidhi Disputed

Graded sahih by al-Albani, while Tirmidhi preferred that it is mursal (from al-Zuhri, without the Companion).

Raisins or dates were soaked in water for him, and he drank it that day, the next and the day after, then ordered it poured away before it changed

Narrated by Muslim Sahih

This is the permitted «nabidh»: a sweet, non-intoxicating infusion. Whatever ferments and intoxicates is forbidden.

Food the Prophet ﷺ loved

The Prophet ﷺ would pick out the gourd (pumpkin) from around the dish

Agreed upon (Bukhari and Muslim) Sahih

Anas said: I have loved gourd ever since that day.

«The Messenger of Allah ﷺ loved sweets and honey»

Agreed upon (Bukhari and Muslim) Sahih

He liked the foreleg (shoulder meat)

Agreed upon (Bukhari and Muslim) Sahih

«The superiority of Aisha over other women is like the superiority of tharid over other food»

Agreed upon (Bukhari and Muslim) Sahih

Tharid: bread broken into meat broth, one of the favourite dishes of the Arabs.

«What an excellent condiment vinegar is»

Narrated by Muslim Sahih

He said it when he found nothing at home but bread and vinegar, showing contentment with little.

The Prophet ﷺ ate cucumber with fresh dates

Agreed upon (Bukhari and Muslim) Sahih

He ate watermelon with fresh dates and said: «We break the heat of this with the coolness of that, and the coolness of that with the heat of this»

Narrated by Abu Dawud and Tirmidhi; graded sahih by al-Albani Sahih

Butter and dates were served to him, and he loved butter and dates

Narrated by Abu Dawud and Ibn Majah; strengthened by Ibn Hajar and al-Albani Hasan

«Eat olive oil and anoint yourselves with it, for it is from a blessed tree»

Narrated by Tirmidhi and Ibn Majah Disputed

Graded sahih by al-Albani through its combined chains, while Tirmidhi pointed out an inconsistency in its chain.

«And whoever is given milk to drink by Allah, let him say: O Allah, bless it for us and give us more of it, for nothing suffices for food and drink except milk»

Narrated by Abu Dawud, Tirmidhi and Ibn Majah; graded hasan by Tirmidhi and al-Albani Hasan

I saw the Messenger of Allah ﷺ eating chicken

Agreed upon, from Abu Musa Sahih

The Prophet ﷺ said of the sea: «Its water is pure and its dead animals are lawful»

Narrated by Abu Dawud, Tirmidhi and Nasa'i; graded sahih by Tirmidhi Sahih

He ﷺ also ate from a huge whale that the sea cast ashore for the Companions (agreed upon).

The simplicity of his life

Aisha said: we would see the new moon, then the new moon, then the new moon, three new moons in two months, and no fire was lit in the houses of the Messenger of Allah ﷺ; their food was the two black things: dates and water

Agreed upon (Bukhari and Muslim) Sahih

«The family of Muhammad ﷺ never ate their fill of barley bread for two days in a row until he passed away»

Narrated by Muslim Sahih

«The Prophet ﷺ never ate at a raised table, nor from small side dishes, nor was thin bread baked for him»

Narrated by Bukhari Sahih

The raised table (khiwan) was a high dining table, the small dishes (sukurrujah) were for appetisers, and the thin bread (muraqqaq) was fine white bread.

On the day of the Trench, the Prophet ﷺ had a stone tied to his stomach from hunger

Narrated by Bukhari Sahih

A roasted lizard (dabb) was brought to him and he did not eat it, saying: «It was not found in the land of my people, so I find I dislike it», and Khalid ibn al-Walid ate it while he watched

Agreed upon (Bukhari and Muslim) Sahih

Evidence that what he left out of personal taste is not forbidden.

Foods for healing in the Sunnah

﴿From their bellies comes a drink of varying colours, in which there is healing for people﴾ [An-Nahl: 69]

The Holy Quran Verse of the Quran

A man came and said: my brother has diarrhoea. The Prophet ﷺ said: «Give him honey to drink», and he gave it to him until he recovered

Agreed upon (Bukhari and Muslim) Sahih

«In the black seed there is healing for every disease except death»

Agreed upon (Bukhari and Muslim) Sahih

As-sam means death. Most scholars understood that it benefits many diseases, not that it replaces all treatment.

«Talbina soothes the heart of the sick person and takes away some of the sadness»

Agreed upon (Bukhari and Muslim) Sahih

Talbina is a soup of barley flour with milk, and «soothes» means relieves and refreshes.

«Whoever eats seven Ajwa dates every morning will not be harmed by poison or magic that day»

Agreed upon (Bukhari and Muslim) Sahih

«Truffles are a kind of manna, and their water is a healing for the eye»

Agreed upon (Bukhari and Muslim) Sahih

Do not put anything unsterile into the eye; it may cause a serious infection.

The Prophet ﷺ said of Zamzam: «It is blessed; it is food that nourishes»

Narrated by Muslim Sahih

«Zamzam water is for whatever it is drunk for»

Narrated by Ahmad and Ibn Majah Disputed

Some hadith scholars graded it sahih and others weak.

«The cure for sciatica is the tail fat of a desert sheep, melted and divided into three parts, then one part drunk each day on an empty stomach»

Narrated by Ibn Majah; graded sahih by al-Albani Sahih

Tail fat is pure fat, so it does not suit heart or cholesterol patients without consulting a doctor.

«Seek treatment, servants of Allah, for Allah has not created a disease without creating a cure for it, except one disease: old age»

Narrated by Abu Dawud, Tirmidhi and Ibn Majah; graded sahih by Tirmidhi Sahih

A basis for seeking medical treatment and seeing a doctor.

Fasting and breaking the fast

«Take suhoor, for there is blessing in suhoor»

Agreed upon (Bukhari and Muslim) Sahih

The Prophet ﷺ would break his fast with fresh dates before praying; if there were none, then dried dates; if there were none, he took a few sips of water

Narrated by Abu Dawud and Tirmidhi; graded hasan by Tirmidhi and sahih by al-Albani Sahih

«People will remain in goodness as long as they hasten to break the fast»

Agreed upon (Bukhari and Muslim) Sahih

The Prophet ﷺ made a point of fasting on Mondays and Thursdays

Narrated by Tirmidhi and Nasa'i; graded sahih by al-Albani Sahih

Famous but not authentic

«We are a people who do not eat until we are hungry, and when we eat we do not eat our fill»

Has no basis in the books of hadith Not authentic

Its meaning is close to the authentic hadith of the thirds, but this wording is not a hadith.

«The stomach is the house of disease, and dieting is the head of medicine»

Not a hadith; it is attributed to al-Harith ibn Kalada, the physician of the Arabs Not authentic

«Fast and you will be healthy»

Narrated by al-Tabarani; graded weak by al-Iraqi and al-Albani Weak

«The blessing of food is washing before it and washing after it»

Narrated by Abu Dawud and Tirmidhi; graded weak by Abu Dawud Weak

Washing the hands before and after eating is useful hygiene even though the hadith is weak.

«The master of your condiments is salt»

Narrated by Ibn Majah; graded weak by al-Albani Weak

«The master of the food of the people of this world and the next is meat»

Narrated by Ibn Majah; graded weak by al-Albani Weak

«Honour bread»

Scholars counted it among fabricated hadiths Not authentic

«Take it, Talha, for it refreshes the heart» about quince

Narrated by Ibn Majah; graded weak by al-Albani Weak

«If my nation knew what is in fenugreek, they would buy it for its weight in gold»

Has no basis Not authentic

«Aubergine is for whatever it is eaten for»

Fabricated Not authentic

«Eat lentils, for they were blessed by the tongues of seventy prophets»

Fabricated Not authentic

«Eat endive, for there is no day but drops from Paradise fall on it»

Fabricated Not authentic

«There is no pomegranate without a seed from Paradise in it»

Not authentically attributed to the Prophet ﷺ Not authentic

«Watermelon before food washes the stomach and uproots disease»

Fabricated Not authentic

Custom and legislation

Scholars distinguish between what the Prophet ﷺ did as religious law and what he did following the custom and environment of his people. His love of gourd and tharid, and his leaving the lizard because he was not used to it, are matters of taste that do not bind us. And he ﷺ said about pollinating date palms: «You know best the affairs of your world» (narrated by Muslim).

Ibn Khaldun held in the «Muqaddimah» that the medicine reported in hadiths was the medicine of the Arabs of that time, and that using it for blessing and with sincere faith is beneficial. Others such as Ibn al-Qayyim disagreed and saw it as prophetic medicine. What is agreed is that the Sunnah commanded seeking treatment, that the authentic foods here are beneficial, and that they do not replace the doctor.

What does science say today about these habits?

  • Eating slowly and stopping before fullness: Agrees with what we know about fullness signals reaching the brain late, and reduces weight gain.
  • Breathing outside the vessel, not blowing into it, and covering food: Hygiene rules that reduce the spread of germs.
  • Breaking the fast with dates and water: Quick sugar and fluids after fasting, then prayer, then the meal.
  • Washing grease off the hands before sleep: Hygiene, and keeps insects away.
  • Simple food: Barley bread, dates, vinegar, olive oil, gourd and cucumber are minimally processed foods, close to what modern dietary guidelines recommend.
  • However: An authentic hadith about the virtue of a food does not mean it treats a specific disease, and people with diabetes or kidney disease count dates and honey within their allowance.

How do we know the grade of a hadith?

Sahih

Narrated by trustworthy narrators with a connected chain and no hidden defect. «Agreed upon» above means narrated by both Bukhari and Muslim.

Hasan

Like sahih, but the memory of some narrators is slightly weaker; it is still accepted as evidence.

Disputed

Some hadith scholars graded it sahih and others weak.

Weak

Its chain has a break or a weak narrator, so it cannot be firmly attributed to the Prophet ﷺ.

Fabricated or with no basis

Falsely attributed to the Prophet ﷺ, or not found with a chain in the books of hadith; it must not be spread in his name.

We relied on the rulings of major hadith scholars, including al-Albani among later scholars. You can check any hadith that reaches you in the Hadith Encyclopedia on the Dorar website.
