Privacy

What «Maryam» knows about you, and what it does not.

In short

«Maryam» asks for no registration, name, email or phone number, uses no tracking or advertising cookies, and does not sell or share any data with advertisers; there are no adverts at all.

Visit statistics

We use GoatCounter to count visits without tracking cookies. It knows which page you visited, your approximate country, your device type, browser and its language, and the site you came from, but not who you are. We use these numbers to learn which pages of the guide help people.

Anonymous rating

When you press «Yes» or «No» under «Was this helpful?», the rating and its reason are recorded in the same visit statistics, with no information about you.

The «Ask Maryam» assistant

Your question is sent to our server and then to an AI service (Claude by Anthropic) to compose an answer from the guide's content. We may keep the text of questions the assistant could not answer, with no information about who asked, to add what the guide is missing. So do not write your name, number or identifying details in your question.

Voice questions

If you use the microphone, the recording is sent to be turned into text and then handled like a written question; we do not keep the recording. In some browsers, the browser itself converts speech to text with its own service.

WhatsApp

When you send us a note or question on WhatsApp, we receive your number and WhatsApp name like any ordinary message, subject to WhatsApp's policy. We use the message only to reply to you, and we do not share your number with anyone.

Stored only on your device

Your favourites, font size, home medicine kit list, the latest news you read, and the pages you rated are stored in your browser on your device and never reach us. The site also stores a copy of its pages on your device so it works offline. You can clear all of this in your browser settings.

Ambulance number

We read the time zone set on your device to show the right ambulance number for your country; this happens only on your device and is not sent anywhere.

Third-party services

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and fonts load from Google Fonts; both services see your device's IP address, as with any site loaded from them, and each has its own privacy policy. Links to scientific sources take you to other sites with their own policies.

Children

The guide is for general education, and we collect no personal data from anyone, young or old.

Contact and changes

For any privacy question, contact us through the «Contact us» page. If anything in this policy changes, we will note it here with the update date.
