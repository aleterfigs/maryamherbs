Storage and safety

  • Keep the box high up and locked, away from children; iron tablets, painkillers and essential oils can be deadly for them.
  • In a cool, dry place — not in the bathroom or by the kitchen cooker — because damp and heat spoil medicines and herbs.
  • Check expiry dates every 6 months, and return expired medicines to the pharmacy for disposal.
  • Dried herbs stay good for about a year, and ground spices 6–12 months; write the purchase date on the container.
  • Keep medicines in their original packs with the leaflet.
  • Never give adult medicines to children, and never share antibiotics.
