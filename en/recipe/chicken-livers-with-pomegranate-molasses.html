The Levant

Chicken livers with pomegranate molasses

A Levantine dish among the richest foods in iron, B12 and vitamin A.

Relatively strong evidenceSafe for most peopleInherited folk custom
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Liver (Relatively strong).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Inherited folk custom.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.

Ingredients (For two people)

  • Liver: A quarter kilo of cleaned chicken livers
  • Garlic and chilli to taste
  • Pomegranate and its peel: One tablespoon of pomegranate molasses
  • Olives and olive oil: A spoon of olive oil

Method

  1. Fry the garlic in the oil, then add the livers and stir over a high heat until fully cooked.
  2. Add the pomegranate molasses, salt and pepper.
  3. Serve with bread and lemon.
How much and when? Once a week.
Pregnant women should not eat much liver, as it is rich in vitamin A. Cook it well.
