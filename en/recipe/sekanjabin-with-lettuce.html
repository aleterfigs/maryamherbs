Sekanjabin with lettuce

An Iranian summer custom: lettuce leaves dipped in vinegar-honey syrup with mint.

Relatively strong evidenceSafe for most peopleInherited folk custom
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Mint (Relatively strong).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Inherited folk custom.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.

Ingredients (For one cup)

  • Honey: Two tablespoons of honey
  • One tablespoon of vinegar
  • Mint: Mint leaves
  • Fresh lettuce leaves

Method

  1. Mix the honey and vinegar with two spoons of warm water and the chopped mint.
  2. Dip the lettuce leaves in the syrup and eat.
How much and when? One cup once or twice a day; can be continued 4–6 weeks, then a week's break.
Acidic: caution with stomach ulcers and reflux.
