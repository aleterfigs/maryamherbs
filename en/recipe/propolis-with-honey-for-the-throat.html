Propolis with honey for the throat

Propolis with honey for an irritated throat and mouth ulcers.

Moderate evidenceSafe for most peopleModern home recipe
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Honey (Moderate).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Modern home recipe.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.

Ingredients (For one cup)

  • Propolis (bee glue): A few drops of pharmacy propolis extract
  • Honey: One teaspoon of honey

Method

  1. Mix the drops with the honey.
  2. Let it dissolve slowly in the mouth twice a day.
How much and when? One cup 2–3 times a day until the symptoms go, for a week at most; if no better, see a doctor.
Avoid with allergy to bees or honey, and with asthma.
