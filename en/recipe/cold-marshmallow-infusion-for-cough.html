Cold marshmallow infusion for cough

Marshmallow is one of Arab medicine's remedies for the chest.

Moderate evidenceSafe for most peopleClassical medical books
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Marshmallow (Moderate).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Classical medical books.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.

Ingredients (For one cup)

  • Marshmallow (khatmi): One teaspoon of dried marshmallow root
  • A cup of cold water
  • Honey: One teaspoon of honey

Method

  1. Soak the root in cold water for two hours, stirring now and then.
  2. Strain and warm slightly.
  3. Add the honey and drink in sips.
How much and when? One cup 2–3 times a day until the symptoms go, for a week at most; if no better, see a doctor.
Keep an hour apart from medicines.
