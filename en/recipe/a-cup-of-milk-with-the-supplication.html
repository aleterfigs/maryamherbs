A cup of milk with the supplication

A cup of milk daily for calcium and protein, with the supplication from the Sunnah.

Moderate evidenceSafe for most peopleGood (hasan) hadith
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Milk (Moderate).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Good (hasan) hadith.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
Good (hasan) hadith«When one of you is given milk to drink, let him say: O God, bless it for us and give us more of it, for nothing suffices as both food and drink except milk” (Abu Dawud and Tirmidhi, who graded it good).

Ingredients (For one cup)

  • Milk: A cup of pasteurised milk

Method

  1. Drink the milk sitting down, in three breaths.
  2. Say: «O God, bless it for us and give us more of it».
How much and when? One cup once or twice a day; can be continued 4–6 weeks, then a week's break.
For lactose intolerance: yoghurt or lactose-free milk. Keep it apart from some antibiotics and iron.
