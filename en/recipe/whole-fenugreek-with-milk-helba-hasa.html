Whole fenugreek with milk (helba hasa)

A warm Egyptian winter drink.

Moderate evidenceNeeds caution for some groupsInherited folk custom
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Fenugreek (Moderate).
  • Safety: Fenugreek: not suitable for some situations (pregnancy, breastfeeding, illnesses or medicines). Use «Suggest a blend» to find out whether it suits you.
  • Source: Inherited folk custom.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.

Ingredients (For one cup)

  • Fenugreek: One teaspoon of whole fenugreek seeds
  • A cup of milk
  • Honey: One teaspoon of honey

Method

  1. Boil the fenugreek in half a cup of water for 5 minutes.
  2. Add the milk and let it heat.
  3. Strain and sweeten with honey.
How much and when? One cup once or twice a day; can be continued 4–6 weeks, then a week's break.
Avoid in pregnancy.
