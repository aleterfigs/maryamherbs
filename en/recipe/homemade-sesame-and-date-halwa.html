Homemade sesame and date halwa

Balls of tahini, dates and honey — an energy-rich snack.

Limited evidenceSafe for most peopleInherited folk custom
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Dates and fresh dates (Limited).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Inherited folk custom.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.

Ingredients (For twenty balls)

  • Dates and fresh dates (ajwa): A cup of pitted dates
  • A quarter cup of tahini
  • Honey: A spoon of honey
  • Toasted sesame for coating

Method

  1. Mash the dates with the tahini and honey into a paste.
  2. Roll into small balls and coat in sesame.
  3. Keeps in the fridge for a week.
How much and when? Two to three balls a day between meals.
High in sugar. Avoid with sesame allergy. No honey under one year.

🌿 From traditional medicine; most of its recipes have not been tested in large studies. Choose organic or clean local ingredients from a trusted source where you can, and if you do not improve, see a doctor.
