India (Ayurveda)

golden milk

A warm evening drink; the turmeric is absorbed better with pepper and fat.

Moderate evidenceNeeds caution for some groupsInherited folk custom
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Turmeric (Moderate).
  • Safety: Turmeric: not suitable for some situations (pregnancy, breastfeeding, illnesses or medicines). Use «Suggest a blend» to find out whether it suits you.
  • Source: Inherited folk custom.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.

Ingredients (For one cup)

  • Turmeric: One teaspoon of ground turmeric
  • Ginger: A quarter teaspoon of ground ginger
  • Cinnamon: A pinch of cinnamon
  • A pinch of black pepper
  • A cup of milk or plant milk

Method

  1. Warm the milk over a low heat.
  2. Add the spices, stirring for 3–5 minutes without letting it boil hard.
  3. Sweeten with honey if you like and drink warm.
How much and when? One cup in the evening; can be continued with breaks.
People on blood thinners or with gallstones should ask their doctor before having it often.
