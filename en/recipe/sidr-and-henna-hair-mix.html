Sidr and henna hair mix

Combines sidr's cleansing with henna's strengthening — a common recipe in Gulf homes.

Limited evidenceSafe for most peopleInherited folk custom
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Sidr (Limited).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Inherited folk custom.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.

Ingredients (For one cup)

  • Sidr (Christ's thorn jujube): 3 tablespoons of ground sidr
  • Henna: Two tablespoons of pure henna
  • Warm water
  • Olives and olive oil: A spoon of olive oil

Method

  1. Mix the sidr and henna with warm water into a paste, and add the oil.
  2. Spread over the scalp and hair and leave for an hour.
  3. Rinse well with water.
How much and when? Once every two weeks.
Gives light hair a slight copper tint. Test on the skin first.
