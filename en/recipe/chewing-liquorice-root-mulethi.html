India (Ayurveda)

Chewing liquorice root (mulethi)

An Indian habit to soothe the throat and a hoarse voice, for a few days only.

Moderate evidenceShort-term or supervised useInherited folk custom
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Liquorice (Moderate).
  • Safety: Liquorice: not for long-term use; best under medical supervision.
  • Source: Inherited folk custom.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.

Ingredients (For one cup)

  • Liquorice: A small piece of liquorice root

Method

  1. Chew the piece slowly and swallow the juice.
  2. Once or twice a day, for no more than 3 days.
How much and when? Once or twice a day, for no more than 3 days.
Forbidden with high blood pressure, heart and kidney disease, and in pregnancy.
