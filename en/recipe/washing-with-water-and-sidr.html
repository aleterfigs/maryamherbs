Washing with water and sidr

A known Sunnah in washing, and a gentle natural body cleanser.

Limited evidenceSafe for most peopleAuthentic (sahih) hadith
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Sidr (Limited).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Authentic (sahih) hadith.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
Authentic (sahih) hadithThe Prophet ﷺ said about the pilgrim: “Wash him with water and sidr” (Bukhari and Muslim).

Ingredients (For one cup)

  • Sidr (Christ's thorn jujube): 4 tablespoons of ground sidr
  • A litre of warm water

Method

  1. Soak the sidr in the warm water for 10 minutes and whisk until it foams.
  2. Wash the wet body with it, then rinse with water.
How much and when? When needed.
Keep away from the eyes. For dry skin: follow with a moisturiser.
