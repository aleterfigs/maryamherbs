Salt and bicarbonate mouth rinse for mouth sores

The rinse recommended by many cancer centres to clean the mouth and ease sores.

Traditional, no studiesSafe for most peopleModern home recipe
  • Evidence: There are no studies on its ingredients for this problem; it relies on traditional use.
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Modern home recipe.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.

Ingredients (For one cup)

  • A quarter teaspoon of salt
  • A quarter teaspoon of sodium bicarbonate
  • A cup of warm water (boiled then cooled)

Method

  1. Dissolve the salt and bicarbonate in the water.
  2. Rinse gently for 30 seconds, then spit out.
  3. Make a fresh batch every day.
How much and when? After meals and before bed, 4–6 times a day.
Do not use mouthwashes containing alcohol. Use a very soft toothbrush.
