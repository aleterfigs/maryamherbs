Honey, lemon and ginger jar

A winter mix made in advance; a spoonful is dissolved in warm water.

Moderate evidenceSafe for most peopleModern home recipe
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Honey (Moderate).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Modern home recipe.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.

Ingredients (For one jar)

  • Honey: A cup of honey
  • Ginger: A finger-sized piece of ginger, sliced
  • A lemon, thinly sliced

Method

  1. Layer the lemon and ginger in a clean, dry jar.
  2. Pour in honey to cover, close and keep in the fridge.
  3. After a day: one tablespoon in a cup of warm water.
How much and when? One teaspoon to one tablespoon 2–3 times a day, for a few days.
Use within two weeks. No honey under one year.
