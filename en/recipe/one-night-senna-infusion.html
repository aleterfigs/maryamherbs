The Gulf

One-night senna infusion

For occasional constipation only, and for a very short time.

Traditional, no studiesShort-term or supervised useInherited folk custom
  • Evidence: There are no studies on its ingredients for this problem; it relies on traditional use.
  • Safety: Senna of Mecca: not for long-term use; best under medical supervision.
  • Source: Inherited folk custom.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.

Ingredients (For one cup)

  • Senna of Mecca (sanamaki, ushriq): A ready-made senna tea bag (at the amount stated on the pack)
  • A cup of hot water

Method

  1. Steep the bag as per the pack (usually 10 minutes).
  2. Drink at bedtime; it works after 6–12 hours.
  3. Do not repeat it for more than a few days.
How much and when? Once before bed; no more than 3–5 days in a row, and never more than a week.
A strong laxative: not for more than a week, not with unexplained abdominal pain, and not for pregnant or breastfeeding women or children. Drink plenty of water.

🌿 From traditional medicine; most of its recipes have not been tested in large studies. Choose organic or clean local ingredients from a trusted source where you can, and if you do not improve, see a doctor.
