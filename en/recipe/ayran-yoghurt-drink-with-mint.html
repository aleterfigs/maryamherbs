The Levant

Ayran (yoghurt drink with mint)

Yoghurt whisked with water, salt and mint — the drink of the Levant and Turkey with meals.

Relatively strong evidenceSafe for most peopleInherited folk custom
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Mint (Relatively strong).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Inherited folk custom.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.

Ingredients (For one cup)

  • A cup of yoghurt with live cultures
  • Half a cup of cold water
  • Mint: A pinch of dried mint
  • A pinch of salt

Method

  1. Whisk the yoghurt with the water and salt until runny and frothy.
  2. Sprinkle with mint and serve cold.
How much and when? One cup a day with food.
People with high blood pressure should use less salt. Keep it two to three hours apart from the antibiotic dose, especially doxycycline and ciprofloxacin, because milk reduces their absorption.
