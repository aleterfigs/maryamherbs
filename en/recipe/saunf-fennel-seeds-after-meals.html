Saunf (fennel seeds after meals)

An Indian after-meal habit to freshen the mouth and ease digestion.

Moderate evidenceSafe for most peopleInherited folk custom
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Fennel (Moderate).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Inherited folk custom.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.

Ingredients (For one cup)

  • Fennel: Half a teaspoon of fennel seeds (lightly toasted)
  • Rock-sugar crystals (optional)

Method

  1. Lightly toast the seeds in a dry pan.
  2. Chew one teaspoon after eating.
How much and when? One teaspoon after the main meals.
For children: a very small amount, chewed well.
