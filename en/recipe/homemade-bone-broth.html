Homemade bone broth

A warm broth of bones and vegetables, drunk in cold weather, after childbirth and after illness.

Limited evidenceSafe for most peopleInherited folk custom
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Bone and trotter broth (Limited).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Inherited folk custom.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.

Ingredients (For one cup)

  • Bone and trotter broth: A kilo of meat or chicken bones with the joints
  • An onion, a carrot and a celery stick
  • A spoonful of vinegar
  • Water to cover the bones, and a little salt

Method

  1. Wash the bones, cover them with water and the vinegar, and leave for half an hour.
  2. Add the vegetables, bring to the boil, then simmer for 3–6 hours, skimming off the foam.
  3. Strain it, cool it, and lift off the layer of fat from the top.
How much and when? A warm cup daily when needed.
Keeps 3 days in the fridge or can be frozen. People with high blood pressure should use less salt, and people with gout should limit it.
