Six dates in the last month of pregnancy

Trials found that about 6 dates a day in the last four weeks may ease labour.

Limited evidenceSafe for most peopleVerse of the Quran
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Dates and fresh dates (Limited).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Verse of the Quran.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
Verse of the QuranGod said to Maryam during labour: “Shake the trunk of the palm tree toward you; it will drop on you ripe, fresh dates” [Quran 19:25].

Ingredients (For one cup)

  • Dates and fresh dates (ajwa): 6 dates (about 60–80 grams)

Method

  1. Eat them spread through the day with water or yoghurt.
  2. Start from about week 36 of pregnancy.
How much and when? 6 dates daily from week 36 until birth.
Ask your doctor first; with gestational diabetes, do not start without her approval.
