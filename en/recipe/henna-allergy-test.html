Henna allergy test

A small step that protects against an allergy that can be severe.

Limited evidenceSafe for most peopleModern home recipe
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Henna (Limited).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Modern home recipe.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.

Ingredients (For one cup)

  • Henna: A little of the henna paste you will use

Method

  1. Put a little behind the ear or inside the elbow.
  2. Leave it for an hour, then wash it off.
  3. Watch the spot for 48 hours; if redness, itching or swelling appears, do not use it.
How much and when? Before each use of a new kind.
Do the test with every new kind or pack.

🌿 From traditional medicine; most of its recipes have not been tested in large studies. Choose organic or clean local ingredients from a trusted source where you can, and if you do not improve, see a doctor.
