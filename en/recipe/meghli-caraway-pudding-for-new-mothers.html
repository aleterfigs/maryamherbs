Meghli (caraway pudding for new mothers)

The Levantine caraway pudding served to new mothers and their visitors.

Moderate evidenceSafe for most peopleInherited folk custom
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Caraway (Moderate).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Inherited folk custom.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.

Ingredients (For six bowls)

  • Caraway: One tablespoon of ground caraway
  • Cinnamon: One teaspoon of cinnamon
  • Half a cup of rice flour
  • 4 cups of water and a little sugar
  • Nuts and coconut to decorate

Method

  1. Mix the rice flour, caraway and cinnamon with the cold water.
  2. Cook, stirring, until it thickens (20 minutes), and add the sugar.
  3. Pour into bowls and decorate with nuts.
How much and when? As part of food, several times a week.
A sweet high in sugar.
