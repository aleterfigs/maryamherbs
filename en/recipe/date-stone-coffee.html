Date-stone coffee

Roasted, ground date stones drunk like coffee, without caffeine.

Limited evidenceSafe for most peopleInherited folk custom
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Dates and fresh dates (Limited).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Inherited folk custom.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.

Ingredients (For one cup)

  • Dates and fresh dates (ajwa): One tablespoon of roasted, ground date stones
  • Cardamom: A pinch of cardamom
  • One and a half cups of water

Method

  1. Wash and dry the stones, then roast them in the oven or a pan until dark brown, and grind finely.
  2. Boil the powder with the cardamom in the water for 5–7 minutes.
  3. Let it settle, then pour.
How much and when? One to two small cups a day.
Data on its benefits are few, but it is a caffeine-free alternative.

🌿 From traditional medicine; most of its recipes have not been tested in large studies. Choose organic or clean local ingredients from a trusted source where you can, and if you do not improve, see a doctor.
