Calcium yoghurt with dates and sesame

According to studies, calcium is one of the things that most eases premenstrual symptoms.

Limited evidenceSafe for most peopleModern home recipe
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Dates and fresh dates (Limited).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Modern home recipe.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.

Ingredients (For one cup)

  • A pot of yoghurt or labneh
  • Dates and fresh dates (ajwa): Two chopped dates
  • One teaspoon of toasted sesame

Method

  1. Mix the ingredients.
  2. Eat as a snack.
How much and when? Daily, especially in the two weeks before the period.
Avoid with sesame or milk allergy.
