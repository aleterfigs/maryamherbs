Miswak

Cleaning the teeth with an arak stick.

Moderate evidenceSafe for most peopleGood (hasan) hadith
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Miswak (Moderate).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Good (hasan) hadith.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
Good (hasan) hadith«The miswak purifies the mouth and pleases the Lord” (Nasa'i and Ahmad; mentioned by Bukhari without a chain).

Ingredients (For one cup)

  • Miswak (arak): A fresh arak stick

Method

  1. Peel about one centimetre from the tip of the stick.
  2. Chew it until the fibres spread.
  3. Clean the teeth with a gentle motion, and cut off the used tip every day.
How much and when? On waking, before prayers and after eating.
Best together with a brush and fluoride toothpaste to prevent decay.
