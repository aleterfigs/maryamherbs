Evening sleep infusion

To relax before sleep and ease mild stress.

Moderate evidenceSafe for most peopleModern home recipe
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Chamomile (Moderate).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Modern home recipe.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.

Ingredients (For one cup)

  • Chamomile: One teaspoon of chamomile
  • Lemon balm (melissa): One teaspoon of lemon balm
  • Lavender: A small pinch of lavender flowers

Method

  1. Steep the ingredients in a covered cup of hot (not boiling) water.
  2. Leave for 8–10 minutes, then strain.
  3. Drink it half an hour to an hour before bed.
How much and when? One cup in the evening an hour before bed; can be drunk daily with a week's break each month.
May increase drowsiness when combined with sedatives or sleeping pills.

🌿 From traditional medicine; most of its recipes have not been tested in large studies. Choose organic or clean local ingredients from a trusted source where you can, and if you do not improve, see a doctor.
