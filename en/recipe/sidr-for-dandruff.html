Sidr for dandruff

A traditional wash for a scalp with mild dandruff.

Limited evidenceSafe for most peopleInherited folk custom
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Sidr (Limited).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Inherited folk custom.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.

Ingredients (For one cup)

  • Sidr (Christ's thorn jujube): 3 tablespoons of ground sidr
  • Warm water
  • One teaspoon of apple cider vinegar

Method

  1. Mix the sidr with water until it foams, and add the vinegar.
  2. Massage into the scalp for 5 minutes and leave for 10 minutes.
  3. Rinse well.
How much and when? Twice a week for a month.
Severe dandruff, or dandruff with redness and strong itching, needs a dermatologist.
