Sugaring (halawa) for hair removal

The traditional method in the Gulf, Egypt and the Levant, using only sugar, lemon and water.

Traditional, no studiesSafe for most peopleInherited folk custom
  • Evidence: There are no studies on its ingredients for this problem; it relies on traditional use.
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Inherited folk custom.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.

Ingredients (For one cup)

  • 1 cup white sugar
  • ¼ cup lemon juice
  • ¼ cup water

Method

  1. Put the ingredients in a pan over medium heat without stirring much.
  2. Let it boil until it turns a golden honey colour (about 10–15 minutes).
  3. Take it off the heat and let it cool until warm, not hot; test the temperature on the inside of your wrist.
  4. Spread a piece on the skin against the direction of hair growth, then pull it off quickly in the direction of growth while holding the skin taut.
  5. Apply aloe gel or a cold compress afterwards.
How much and when? One cup once or twice a day; can be continued 4–6 weeks, then a week's break.
Hot sugar paste causes serious burns; always test the temperature. Don't use on broken or inflamed skin or with retinoid creams. Wash lemon off the skin before going into the sun.

🌿 From traditional medicine; most of its recipes have not been tested in large studies. Choose organic or clean local ingredients from a trusted source where you can, and if you do not improve, see a doctor.
