A salt-reduction plan using spices

Cutting salt is one of the strongest ways to lower blood pressure. Spices and lemon make up for the taste.

Moderate evidenceSafe for most peopleModern home recipe
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Garlic (Moderate).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Modern home recipe.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.

Ingredients (For one cup)

  • Sumac: Sumac
  • Cumin: Cumin
  • Garlic: Garlic
  • Lemon, black pepper and fresh herbs

Method

  1. Remove the salt shaker from the table, and cut salt in cooking gradually over two weeks — your taste adapts.
  2. Use lemon, sumac, garlic and cumin instead of salt.
  3. Cut down on pickles, salty cheeses, tinned foods, crisps and stock cubes.
  4. Read labels: the goal is under 5 grams of salt a day (one teaspoon) from all sources.
How much and when? Daily, as a way of life.
Do not use salt substitutes with kidney disease or potassium-sparing BP medicines without asking the doctor.

🌿 From traditional medicine; most of its recipes have not been tested in large studies. Choose organic or clean local ingredients from a trusted source where you can, and if you do not improve, see a doctor.
