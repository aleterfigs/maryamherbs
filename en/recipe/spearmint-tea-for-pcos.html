Spearmint tea for PCOS

Two small trials found that two cups a day for a month lowered excess male hormones and slightly reduced unwanted hair.

Traditional, no studiesSafe for most peopleModern home recipe
  • Evidence: There are no studies on its ingredients for this problem; it relies on traditional use.
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Modern home recipe.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.

Ingredients (For one cup)

  • Mint: 1 tsp dried spearmint, or a handful of fresh leaves

Method

  1. Put the mint in a cup.
  2. Pour over boiling water and cover for 5–10 minutes.
  3. Strain and drink one cup in the morning and one in the evening.
How much and when? One cup once or twice a day; can be continued 4–6 weeks, then a week's break.
Spearmint is not peppermint. Avoid with severe reflux, and it does not replace follow-up with your doctor.
