Hasa luban (rosemary) with honey

This is what the people of al-Andalus and the Maghreb called rosemary, and they drank it for a «cold stomach».

Moderate evidenceNeeds caution for some groupsClassical medical books
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Honey (Moderate).
  • Safety: Rosemary: not suitable for some situations (pregnancy, breastfeeding, illnesses or medicines). Use «Suggest a blend» to find out whether it suits you.
  • Source: Classical medical books.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.

Ingredients (For one cup)

  • Rosemary: A sprig of rosemary
  • Honey: One teaspoon of honey
  • A cup of boiling water

Method

  1. Steep the sprig for 7 minutes.
  2. Strain and add the honey.
How much and when? One cup once or twice a day; can be continued 4–6 weeks, then a week's break.
Avoid with epilepsy, and medicinal amounts in pregnancy.
