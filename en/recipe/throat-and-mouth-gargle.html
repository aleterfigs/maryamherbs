Throat and mouth gargle

For a mild sore throat and irritated gums.

Moderate evidenceNeeds caution for some groupsModern home recipe
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Sage (Moderate).
  • Safety: Sage: not suitable for some situations (pregnancy, breastfeeding, illnesses or medicines). Use «Suggest a blend» to find out whether it suits you.
  • Source: Modern home recipe.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.

Ingredients (For one cup)

  • Sage: One teaspoon of sage
  • Thyme: One teaspoon of thyme
  • A quarter teaspoon of salt

Method

  1. Steep the herbs in a cup of boiling water for 10 minutes, then strain.
  2. Dissolve the salt and let it cool to lukewarm.
  3. Gargle 2–3 times a day and do not swallow.
How much and when? 2–3 times a day without swallowing, for a few days until it improves.
For short-term use. See a doctor if the pain gets worse, swallowing becomes hard or a high fever appears.

🌿 From traditional medicine; most of its recipes have not been tested in large studies. Choose organic or clean local ingredients from a trusted source where you can, and if you do not improve, see a doctor.
