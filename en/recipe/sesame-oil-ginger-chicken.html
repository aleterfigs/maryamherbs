Sesame-oil ginger chicken

A traditional Chinese dish for the «sitting month» after childbirth.

Moderate evidenceSafe for most peopleInherited folk custom
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Ginger (Relatively strong).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Inherited folk custom.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.

Ingredients (For four people)

  • Chicken and chicken broth: Chicken pieces
  • Ginger: A large piece of ginger, sliced
  • Two spoons of toasted sesame oil
  • Water and salt

Method

  1. Fry the ginger in the sesame oil until golden.
  2. Add the chicken and stir, then the water.
  3. Cook for 30 minutes over a low heat.
How much and when? As part of food, several times a week.
The original recipe contains rice wine; we have left it out.
