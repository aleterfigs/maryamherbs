Homemade date ma'moul

Semolina cookies filled with dates, the festive sweet of the Levant, Iraq and the Gulf.

Limited evidenceSafe for most peopleInherited folk custom
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Dates and fresh dates (Limited).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Inherited folk custom.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.

Ingredients (For about 30 pieces)

  • Dates and fresh dates (ajwa): A cup of date paste
  • Two cups of semolina and a cup of flour
  • Half a cup of ghee
  • Orange blossom and orange-blossom water: A spoon of orange-blossom water
  • Damask rose and rose water: A spoon of rose water
  • Cinnamon: A pinch of cinnamon

Method

  1. Rub the semolina and flour with the ghee and leave for two hours, then knead with the orange-blossom and rose water.
  2. Mix the date paste with the cinnamon and roll into small balls.
  3. Fill the dough with the dates, shape in the mould, and bake at 180 °C for 15–20 minutes.
How much and when? One or two pieces on special occasions.
A sweet high in sugar and fat.
