The Levant

A bowl of salad before the meal

Starting with vegetables dressed in vinegar and oil fills the stomach with few calories and softens the rise in blood sugar.

Relatively strong evidenceSafe for most peopleInherited folk custom
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Olives and olive oil (Relatively strong).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Inherited folk custom.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.

Ingredients (For one dish)

  • Leafy greens, cucumber and tomato
  • Apple cider vinegar: A spoon of vinegar
  • Olives and olive oil: One teaspoon of olive oil
  • Sumac: A pinch of sumac

Method

  1. Make a large bowl of salad.
  2. Eat it first, ten minutes before the main dish.
How much and when? As part of food, several times a week.
A safe dish.
