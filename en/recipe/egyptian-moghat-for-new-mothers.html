Egyptian moghat for new mothers

The traditional drink served to new mothers and their visitors in Egypt.

Limited evidenceSafe for most peopleInherited folk custom
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Moghat (Limited).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Inherited folk custom.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.

Ingredients (For one cup)

  • Moghat: Two tablespoons of moghat powder
  • A cup of milk
  • A spoon of ghee
  • Nuts and coconut to decorate

Method

  1. Lightly toast the moghat in the ghee.
  2. Add the milk gradually, stirring until it thickens.
  3. Decorate with nuts.
How much and when? One cup a day in the postpartum weeks.
High in calories. There is no scientific evidence that it increases milk.
