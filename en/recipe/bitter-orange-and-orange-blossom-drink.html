Bitter-orange and orange-blossom drink

The bitter orange came to al-Andalus with the Arabs, and its syrup and blossom water were among the refreshing summer drinks.

Moderate evidenceSafe for most peopleClassical medical books
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Honey (Moderate).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Classical medical books.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.

Ingredients (For one cup)

  • Juice of a bitter orange or an orange
  • Orange blossom and orange-blossom water: One teaspoon of orange-blossom water
  • Honey: One teaspoon of honey
  • A cup of cold water

Method

  1. Mix the juice with the orange-blossom water and honey.
  2. Add the cold water.
How much and when? One cup once or twice a day; can be continued 4–6 weeks, then a week's break.
Bitter-orange juice may interact with some medicines, like grapefruit; replace it with orange if you take long-term medicines.
