Rosemary infusion after meals

For a heavy stomach and indigestion after fatty food.

Limited evidenceNeeds caution for some groupsModern home recipe
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Rosemary (Limited).
  • Safety: Rosemary: not suitable for some situations (pregnancy, breastfeeding, illnesses or medicines). Use «Suggest a blend» to find out whether it suits you.
  • Source: Modern home recipe.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.

Ingredients (For one cup)

  • Rosemary: One teaspoon of dried rosemary or a fresh sprig

Method

  1. Steep the rosemary in a cup of boiling water for 7 minutes.
  2. Strain.
  3. Drink once a day after lunch.
How much and when? One cup once or twice a day; can be continued 4–6 weeks, then a week's break.
Not in large amounts in pregnancy; avoid with epilepsy.
