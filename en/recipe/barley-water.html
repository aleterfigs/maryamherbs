Barley water

One of the oldest drinks in Arab and Greek medicine, for the sick, the feverish and the thirsty.

Moderate evidenceNeeds caution for some groupsClassical medical books
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Barley (Moderate).
  • Safety: Barley: not suitable for some situations (pregnancy, breastfeeding, illnesses or medicines). Use «Suggest a blend» to find out whether it suits you.
  • Source: Classical medical books.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.

Ingredients (For three cups)

  • Barley: A quarter cup of barley grains
  • 4 cups of water
  • Juice of one lemon (optional)

Method

  1. Rinse the barley and simmer in the water for 30–40 minutes.
  2. Strain and add the lemon.
  3. Drink lukewarm or cold through the day.
How much and when? One cup once or twice a day; can be continued 4–6 weeks, then a week's break.
Contains gluten: not suitable for people with coeliac disease.
