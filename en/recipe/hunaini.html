Hunaini

A Najdi winter dish of dates, brown bread and ghee.

Limited evidenceSafe for most peopleInherited folk custom
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Dates and fresh dates (Limited).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Inherited folk custom.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.

Ingredients (For one bowl)

  • Dates and fresh dates (ajwa): A cup of soft dates
  • Two loaves of brown bread, crumbled
  • Two spoons of ghee
  • Cardamom: A pinch of cardamom
  • Saffron: Saffron threads (optional)

Method

  1. Warm the ghee and add the dates until soft.
  2. Add the crumbled bread and cardamom and mash them together.
  3. Serve warm.
How much and when? As part of food, several times a week.
High in calories.
