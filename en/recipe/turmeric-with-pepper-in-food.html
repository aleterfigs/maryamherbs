Turmeric with pepper in food

An easy daily way to take turmeric for people with joint pain.

Moderate evidenceNeeds caution for some groupsModern home recipe
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Turmeric (Moderate).
  • Safety: Turmeric: not suitable for some situations (pregnancy, breastfeeding, illnesses or medicines). Use «Suggest a blend» to find out whether it suits you.
  • Source: Modern home recipe.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.

Ingredients (For one cup)

  • Turmeric: Half a teaspoon of turmeric
  • A pinch of black pepper
  • A spoon of olive oil

Method

  1. Mix the turmeric and pepper into the oil.
  2. Add it to rice, soup or eggs.
  3. Daily with a meal.
How much and when? As part of food, several times a week.
Avoid with gallstones; caution with blood thinners.
