Tharid

Bread broken into meat and vegetable broth — a warm, easy food for the sick, the weak and older people.

Moderate evidenceSafe for most peopleAuthentic (sahih) hadith
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Lamb and mutton (Moderate).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Authentic (sahih) hadith.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
Authentic (sahih) hadithThe Prophet ﷺ said: “The superiority of Aisha over other women is like the superiority of tharid over all other food” (Bukhari and Muslim).

Ingredients (For two people)

  • Two loaves of bread (brown or thin flatbread preferred)
  • Two cups of meat or chicken broth with vegetable pieces
  • Lamb and mutton: Pieces of cooked meat or chicken

Method

  1. Break the bread into a deep dish.
  2. Pour the hot broth over it until it soaks in.
  3. Top with the meat and vegetables and serve warm.
How much and when? One cup once or twice a day; can be continued 4–6 weeks, then a week's break.
People with high blood pressure should use less salt in the broth.
