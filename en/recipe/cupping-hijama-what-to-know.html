Cupping (hijama): what to know

A well-known Sunnah; small trials suggest benefit for neck and lower-back pain, and what matters most is doing it safely.

Limited evidenceShort-term or supervised useAuthentic (sahih) hadith
  • Evidence: Small clinical trials on neck and lower-back pain, of limited quality.
  • Safety: Cupping is a procedure that needs a licensed therapist: not for long-term use; best under medical supervision.
  • Source: Authentic (sahih) hadith.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
Authentic (sahih) hadith«Healing is in three: a cut of the cupping blade, a drink of honey, or cautery with fire — and I forbid my community from cautery” (Bukhari), and «The best of what you treat yourselves with is cupping and sea costus» (Bukhari and Muslim).

Ingredients (For one cup)

  • A therapist licensed by the health authorities
  • Sterile single-use cups and tools

Method

  1. Choose a licensed centre, and make sure the tools are opened in front of you and thrown away after use.
  2. Tell the therapist about your medicines and illnesses, especially blood thinners, diabetes and anaemia.
  3. After the session: keep the small cuts clean, drink water, and rest that day.
How much and when? At wide intervals, under the therapist's supervision.
Not to be done with blood thinners, anaemia, low platelets, pregnancy, uncontrolled diabetes or weak immunity, nor for children. Do not stop your medicines because of it. The Prophet ﷺ forbade his community from cauterising, so children must never be branded with fire (wasm), as happens in some homes; it causes burns and disfigurement.

🌿 From traditional medicine; most of its recipes have not been tested in large studies. Choose organic or clean local ingredients from a trusted source where you can, and if you do not improve, see a doctor.
