Drumsticks in sambar

The green pods cooked in Indian lentil stew.

Moderate evidenceNeeds caution for some groupsInherited folk custom
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Turmeric (Moderate).
  • Safety: Moringa, Turmeric: not suitable for some situations (pregnancy, breastfeeding, illnesses or medicines). Use «Suggest a blend» to find out whether it suits you.
  • Source: Inherited folk custom.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.

Ingredients (For four people)

  • Moringa (ban, shu', yusr): 3 Green moringa pods, cut up
  • A cup of cooked yellow lentils
  • Turmeric: A pinch of turmeric
  • Tamarind: One teaspoon of tamarind
  • Sambar spices and tomato

Method

  1. Cook the pod pieces in water with the turmeric for 10 minutes.
  2. Add the lentils, tomato, tamarind and spices.
  3. Simmer for 10 minutes; eat the soft pulp from inside the pods.
How much and when? As part of food, several times a week.
A safe food dish.
