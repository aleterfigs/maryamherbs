Seven ajwa dates in the morning

An established dietary Sunnah that gives energy and fibre to start the day.

Limited evidenceSafe for most peopleAuthentic (sahih) hadith
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Dates and fresh dates (Limited).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Authentic (sahih) hadith.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
Authentic (sahih) hadith«Whoever eats seven ajwa dates every morning will not be harmed that day by poison or magic” (Bukhari and Muslim).

Ingredients (For one cup)

  • Dates and fresh dates (ajwa): Seven ajwa dates (or fewer for children)

Method

  1. Eat in the morning before any other food.
  2. Drink a glass of water afterwards.
How much and when? Daily in the morning.
For diabetics: discuss the amount with the doctor. For children, remove the stones and cut up.

🌿 From traditional medicine; most of its recipes have not been tested in large studies. Choose organic or clean local ingredients from a trusted source where you can, and if you do not improve, see a doctor.
