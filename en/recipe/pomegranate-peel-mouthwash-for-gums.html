Pomegranate-peel mouthwash for gums

An astringent mouthwash for mild gum bleeding.

Limited evidenceSafe for most peopleInherited folk custom
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Pomegranate and its peel (Limited).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Inherited folk custom.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.

Ingredients (For one cup)

  • Pomegranate and its peel: A small piece of dried pomegranate peel
  • A cup of water

Method

  1. Boil the peel for 10 minutes, then strain.
  2. Rinse twice a day without swallowing.
How much and when? 2–3 times a day without swallowing, for a few days until it improves.
Persistent gum bleeding needs a dentist.

🌿 From traditional medicine; most of its recipes have not been tested in large studies. Choose organic or clean local ingredients from a trusted source where you can, and if you do not improve, see a doctor.
