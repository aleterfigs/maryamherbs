Agar jelly with prune juice

A light Japanese dessert (kanten) for mild constipation.

Limited evidenceSafe for most peopleInherited folk custom
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Agar (Limited).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Inherited folk custom.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.

Ingredients (For two small cups)

  • Agar (agar-agar, kanten): Half a teaspoon of agar powder
  • A cup of natural prune or apricot juice
  • Half a cup of water

Method

  1. Mix the agar with the cold water, then boil for two minutes, stirring.
  2. Add the juice and take off the heat.
  3. Pour into small cups and leave to set for half an hour.
  4. Eat one cup and drink two glasses of water afterwards.
How much and when? One cup once or twice a day; can be continued 4–6 weeks, then a week's break.
Always drink enough water with it. Keep two hours apart from medicines.
