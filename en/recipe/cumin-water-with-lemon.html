Cumin water with lemon

For bloating and mild colic.

Limited evidenceSafe for most peopleInherited folk custom
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Cumin (Limited).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Inherited folk custom.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.

Ingredients (For one cup)

  • Cumin: One teaspoon of cumin seeds
  • Juice of a quarter lemon

Method

  1. Boil the cumin in a cup of water for 5 minutes.
  2. Strain and add the lemon.
  3. Drink warm after meals.
How much and when? One cup once or twice a day; can be continued 4–6 weeks, then a week's break.
If bloating keeps coming back with pain, weight loss or blood in the stool, these are signs to see a doctor.
