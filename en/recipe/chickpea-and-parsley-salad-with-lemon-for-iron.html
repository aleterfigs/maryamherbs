Chickpea and parsley salad with lemon for iron

Plant iron with vitamin C to improve its absorption — important with heavy periods.

Traditional, no studiesSafe for most peopleInherited folk custom
  • Evidence: There are no studies on its ingredients for this problem; it relies on traditional use.
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Inherited folk custom.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.

Ingredients (For one dish)

  • A cup of boiled chickpeas
  • A bunch of chopped parsley
  • Juice of one lemon
  • Olives and olive oil: A spoon of olive oil
  • Sumac: A pinch of sumac

Method

  1. Mix the ingredients.
  2. Eat with lunch, and drink tea an hour after the meal, not with it.
How much and when? As part of food, several times a week.
It does not replace iron tablets if the doctor prescribed them.

🌿 From traditional medicine; most of its recipes have not been tested in large studies. Choose organic or clean local ingredients from a trusted source where you can, and if you do not improve, see a doctor.
