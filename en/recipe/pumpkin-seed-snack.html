Pumpkin-seed snack

A daily snack that supports prostate health with zinc and magnesium.

Limited evidenceSafe for most peopleInherited folk custom
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Pumpkin seeds (Limited).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Inherited folk custom.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.

Ingredients (For one portion)

  • Pumpkin seeds: A quarter cup of shelled pumpkin seeds
  • Cumin: A pinch of cumin
  • A small pinch of salt (optional)

Method

  1. Toast the seeds in a dry pan over a medium heat for 3–4 minutes, stirring.
  2. Sprinkle with cumin and leave to cool.
  3. Eat as a snack or add to salad.
How much and when? One portion a day; can be continued.
It does not replace regular prostate checks after forty or fifty as your doctor advises, especially with difficulty passing urine or blood in the urine.
