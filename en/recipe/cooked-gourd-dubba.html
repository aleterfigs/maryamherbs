Cooked gourd (dubba')

A light, easily digested dish of squash or courgette.

Moderate evidenceSafe for most peopleAuthentic (sahih) hadith
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Olives and olive oil (Relatively strong).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Authentic (sahih) hadith.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
Authentic (sahih) hadithThe Prophet ﷺ would pick out the gourd from around the dish. Anas said: I have loved gourd ever since that day (Bukhari and Muslim).

Ingredients (For one cup)

  • A small squash or 2 courgettes
  • Olives and olive oil: A spoon of olive oil
  • Cumin: A pinch of cumin

Method

  1. Cut up the squash and cook with the oil over a low heat.
  2. Add the cumin and salt.
  3. Serve with bread or rice.
How much and when? As part of food, several times a week.
Suitable for a sensitive stomach and for children.
