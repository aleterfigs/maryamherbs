Iranian kachi for new mothers

A flour pudding with saffron and rose water, the food of women after childbirth in Iran.

Traditional, no studiesSafe for most peopleInherited folk custom
  • Evidence: There are no studies on its ingredients for this problem; it relies on traditional use.
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Inherited folk custom.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.

Ingredients (For one bowl)

  • A quarter cup of flour
  • Two spoons of ghee or butter
  • Saffron: Soaked saffron threads
  • Damask rose and rose water: A spoon of rose water
  • Cardamom: A pinch of cardamom
  • A cup of water and a little sugar

Method

  1. Toast the flour in the ghee until it colours.
  2. Add the sweetened water gradually, stirring.
  3. Add the saffron, rose water and cardamom.
How much and when? A bowl daily in the first days after childbirth.
High in calories.
