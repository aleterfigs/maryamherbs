Ramadan liquorice drink (erk sous)

The popular Ramadan drink in Egypt and the Levant, drunk cold.

Moderate evidenceShort-term or supervised useInherited folk custom
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Liquorice (Moderate).
  • Safety: Liquorice: not for long-term use; best under medical supervision.
  • Source: Inherited folk custom.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.

Ingredients (For one litre)

  • Liquorice: One tablespoon of ground liquorice
  • A litre of water
  • A pinch of bicarbonate (traditional, optional)

Method

  1. Moisten the liquorice with a little water and leave for an hour.
  2. Add the rest of the cold water and leave for 4 hours.
  3. Strain well and drink cold — one cup, no more.
How much and when? One cup a day, not every day, and no more than a week in a row.
It raises blood pressure and lowers potassium: forbidden with high blood pressure, heart or kidney disease and in pregnancy, and not to be drunk daily over long periods.
