Linden and lemon balm infusion

For stress and mild insomnia.

Moderate evidenceSafe for most peopleModern home recipe
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Lemon balm (Moderate).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Modern home recipe.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.

Ingredients (For one cup)

  • Linden (lime flower): One teaspoon of linden
  • Lemon balm (melissa): One teaspoon of lemon balm

Method

  1. Steep the herbs in a cup of boiling water for 10 minutes.
  2. Strain.
  3. Drink in the evening.
How much and when? One cup in the evening an hour before bed; can be drunk daily with a week's break each month.
People with heart disease or a thyroid problem should ask their doctor.
