Egyptian tamarind drink

A refreshing Ramadan drink and a mild laxative.

Limited evidenceSafe for most peopleInherited folk custom
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Tamarind (Limited).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Inherited folk custom.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.

Ingredients (For one litre)

  • Tamarind: 100 grams of tamarind pulp
  • A litre of water
  • A little sugar to taste

Method

  1. Soak the pulp in the water for 6 hours or overnight.
  2. Rub it with your hand, then strain.
  3. Sweeten lightly and serve cold.
How much and when? One cup once or twice a day; can be continued 4–6 weeks, then a week's break.
Sour: may worsen heartburn. Caution with aspirin and diabetes medicines.
