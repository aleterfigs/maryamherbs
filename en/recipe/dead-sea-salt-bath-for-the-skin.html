Dead Sea salt bath for the skin

To soothe psoriasis, eczema and dry skin.

Moderate evidenceSafe for most peopleInherited folk custom
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Dead Sea salt and mud (Moderate).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Inherited folk custom.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.

Ingredients (For one cup)

  • Dead Sea salt and mud: One to two cups of Dead Sea salt
  • A bath of warm water
  • Olives and olive oil: Olive oil or moisturiser after the bath

Method

  1. Dissolve the salt in the warm bath water.
  2. Soak your body for 15–20 minutes.
  3. Rinse your body with ordinary water.
  4. Pat dry and moisturise the skin straight away.
How much and when? Once or twice a day when needed.
Two to three times a week. Stings open wounds and cracks. It complements a dermatologist's treatment and does not replace it.
