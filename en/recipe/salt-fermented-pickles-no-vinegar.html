Salt-fermented pickles (no vinegar)

Pickles naturally fermented in salt water contain good bacteria; vinegar pickles do not.

Limited evidenceSafe for most peopleInherited folk custom
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Dill (Limited).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Inherited folk custom.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.

Ingredients (For one jar)

  • Small cucumbers or sliced turnips
  • A litre of boiled then cooled water
  • Two tablespoons of non-iodised salt
  • Garlic: garlic cloves
  • Dill: Dill sprigs

Method

  1. Layer the vegetables, garlic and dill in a clean glass jar.
  2. Dissolve the salt in the water and pour it in to cover them completely.
  3. Close and wait 5–7 days at room temperature, then keep in the fridge.
How much and when? Small pieces with meals.
High in salt: people with high blood pressure or kidney disease should eat little. Throw away if mould or a bad smell appears.
