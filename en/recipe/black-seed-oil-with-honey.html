Black-seed oil with honey

The common way to take black-seed oil, the most studied form.

Moderate evidenceSafe for most peopleAuthentic (sahih) hadith
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Black seed (Moderate).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Authentic (sahih) hadith.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
Authentic (sahih) hadith«In the black seed is healing for every disease except death” (Bukhari and Muslim).

Ingredients (For one cup)

  • Black seed (nigella): Half a teaspoon of cold-pressed black-seed oil
  • Honey: One teaspoon of honey

Method

  1. Mix the oil with the honey to soften its sharp taste.
  2. Take after breakfast.
How much and when? Once a day for 8–12 weeks, then assess the benefit.
Caution with diabetes and blood-pressure medicines and blood thinners. Do not exceed the amount.
