Henna and katam to cover grey hair

The two-step method: henna first, then indigo or katam, for a natural brown or black.

Limited evidenceSafe for most peopleAuthentic (sahih) hadith
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Henna (Limited).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Authentic (sahih) hadith.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
Authentic (sahih) hadith«The best thing with which you change grey hair is henna and katam” (the Sunan collections; graded authentic by Tirmidhi).

Ingredients (For one cup)

  • Henna: Half a cup of pure henna
  • Indigo (nila) and katam: Half a cup of pure indigo or katam
  • Warm water or tea, and the juice of one lemon for the henna

Method

  1. Do an allergy test two days before.
  2. Step one: mix the henna with water and lemon and leave for 4 hours, then apply to clean hair for two hours and rinse with water.
  3. Step two: mix the indigo with warm water and apply at once to damp hair for one to two hours, then rinse without shampoo.
  4. Do not shampoo for two days so the colour sets and darkens.
How much and when? Every 3–4 weeks, depending on how fast the grey grows.
Use only pure henna and indigo, not instant «black» products. Keep away from the eyes.
