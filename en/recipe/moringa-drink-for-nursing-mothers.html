Moringa drink for nursing mothers

Small trials suggest its leaves may increase milk.

Limited evidenceNeeds caution for some groupsModern home recipe
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Moringa (Limited).
  • Safety: Moringa: not suitable for some situations (pregnancy, breastfeeding, illnesses or medicines). Use «Suggest a blend» to find out whether it suits you.
  • Source: Modern home recipe.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.

Ingredients (For one cup)

  • Moringa (ban, shu', yusr): One teaspoon of leaf powder
  • A cup of warm water or milk
  • Dates and fresh dates (ajwa): A date

Method

  1. Dissolve the powder in the warm water or milk.
  2. Drink with a date once or twice a day.
How much and when? Once or twice a day while it helps.
After childbirth only. Frequent feeding is the most important way to increase milk.
