Talbina

Barley porridge with milk and honey, for the sick, the sad and the tired.

Moderate evidenceNeeds caution for some groupsAuthentic (sahih) hadith
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Barley (Moderate).
  • Safety: Barley: not suitable for some situations (pregnancy, breastfeeding, illnesses or medicines). Use «Suggest a blend» to find out whether it suits you.
  • Source: Authentic (sahih) hadith.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
Authentic (sahih) hadith«Talbina gives rest to the heart of the sick and takes away some of the grief” (Bukhari, from Aisha, may God be pleased with her). «Gives rest» means it soothes and revives.

Ingredients (For one cup)

  • Barley: Two tablespoons of barley flour (wholegrain preferred)
  • One and a half cups of milk or water
  • Honey: One teaspoon of honey

Method

  1. Mix the barley flour with a little cold milk so it does not go lumpy.
  2. Add the rest of the milk and heat over a low heat, stirring, for 10–15 minutes until it is like a soup.
  3. Take off the heat and sweeten with honey once lukewarm.
How much and when? A bowl when ill, sad or tired, or several times a week.
Not suitable for people with coeliac disease. For children under one, no honey.
