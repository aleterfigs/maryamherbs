Nettle-root decoction for the prostate

A European use for mild prostate symptoms.

Limited evidenceNeeds caution for some groupsInherited folk custom
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Nettle (Limited).
  • Safety: Nettle: not suitable for some situations (pregnancy, breastfeeding, illnesses or medicines). Use «Suggest a blend» to find out whether it suits you.
  • Source: Inherited folk custom.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.

Ingredients (For one cup)

  • Nettle: One teaspoon of nettle root
  • A cup of water

Method

  1. Boil the root for 10 minutes.
  2. Strain; one cup a day.
How much and when? One cup a day for 6–8 weeks, then see the doctor.
Have the prostate checked by a doctor first.
