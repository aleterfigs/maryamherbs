Pigeon broth after childbirth

The traditional food for women after childbirth in Egypt and the Gulf, warm and rich in protein.

Limited evidenceSafe for most peopleInherited folk custom
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Pigeon (Limited).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Inherited folk custom.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.

Ingredients (For one cup)

  • Pigeon: A pair of cleaned pigeons
  • An onion
  • Cardamom: 3 cardamom pods
  • Two mastic grains and a bay leaf
  • 4 cups of water and a little salt

Method

  1. Simmer the pigeons with the onion, cardamom, mastic and bay leaf for 45 minutes until cooked.
  2. Skim off the foam while cooking.
  3. Drink the broth warm and eat the meat.
How much and when? Once or twice a week during the forty days after birth.
Cook well and buy from a licensed source. Suitable while breastfeeding.
