Etiquette of drinking water

Drinking sitting down in three breaths, without breathing into the cup — it reduces choking and is easier on the stomach.

Traditional, no studiesSafe for most peopleAuthentic (sahih) hadith
  • Evidence: There are no studies on its ingredients for this problem; it relies on traditional use.
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Authentic (sahih) hadith.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
Authentic (sahih) hadithThe Prophet ﷺ used to take three breaths when drinking and say: “It is more thirst-quenching, healthier and more wholesome” (Muslim), and: “When one of you drinks, let him not breathe into the vessel” (Bukhari and Muslim).

Ingredients (For one cup)

  • A cup of water

Method

  1. Sit down and say Bismillah.
  2. Drink in three sips, moving the cup away from your mouth between each.
  3. Do not breathe into the cup, and praise God after drinking.
How much and when? Every time you drink.
Suitable for everyone.
