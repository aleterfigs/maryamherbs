Garden cress with milk for new mothers

A Gulf and Yemeni recipe for women after childbirth.

Limited evidenceNeeds caution for some groupsInherited folk custom
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Garden cress seeds (Limited).
  • Safety: Garden cress seeds: not suitable for some situations (pregnancy, breastfeeding, illnesses or medicines). Use «Suggest a blend» to find out whether it suits you.
  • Source: Inherited folk custom.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
Weak hadithAbout thuffa' (garden cress) the hadith “What healing there is in the two bitter things: aloe and thuffa'” is narrated, but its chain is weak.

Ingredients (For one cup)

  • Garden cress seeds (rashad, hilf): One teaspoon of garden cress seeds
  • A cup of milk
  • Honey: One teaspoon of honey

Method

  1. Simmer the garden cress in the milk over a low heat for 5 minutes.
  2. Add the honey.
  3. Drink warm.
How much and when? One cup a day in the first weeks after childbirth.
After childbirth only; forbidden during pregnancy.
