Violet syrup

One of the best-known syrups of Andalusian and Arab medicine for dry cough and chest heat.

Moderate evidenceSafe for most peopleClassical medical books
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Honey (Moderate).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Classical medical books.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.

Ingredients (For a small bottle)

  • Violet: Two tablespoons of dried violet flowers
  • A cup of boiling water
  • Honey: 3 tablespoons of honey

Method

  1. Steep the flowers in the boiling water, covered, for 8 hours.
  2. Strain and squeeze the flowers.
  3. Warm the liquid slightly and dissolve the honey in it; keep in the fridge for a week.
  4. One teaspoon to one tablespoon diluted in warm water, 3 times a day.
How much and when? One teaspoon to one tablespoon, diluted, 3 times a day, for no more than a week.
Make sure it is a true edible violet. No honey under one year.
