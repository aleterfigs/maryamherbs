Kadha (Indian spice decoction)

A traditional decoction promoted by India's AYUSH ministry in cold seasons.

Moderate evidenceNeeds caution for some groupsInherited folk custom
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Ginger (Relatively strong).
  • Safety: Tulsi: not suitable for some situations (pregnancy, breastfeeding, illnesses or medicines). Use «Suggest a blend» to find out whether it suits you.
  • Source: Inherited folk custom.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.

Ingredients (For one cup)

  • Tulsi (holy basil): 5 Tulsi leaves
  • Ginger: 3 Slices of ginger
  • Cinnamon: A small cinnamon stick
  • Clove: Two cloves
  • 3 Crushed black peppercorns
  • Honey: One teaspoon of honey

Method

  1. Boil all the ingredients except the honey in two cups of water until reduced by half (10–15 minutes).
  2. Strain and add the honey once lukewarm.
  3. Drink once or twice a day for a few days.
How much and when? One cup 2–3 times a day until the symptoms go, for a week at most; if no better, see a doctor.
Strong and hot: may irritate the stomach. Caution with blood thinners.

🌿 From traditional medicine; most of its recipes have not been tested in large studies. Choose organic or clean local ingredients from a trusted source where you can, and if you do not improve, see a doctor.
