Truffles with onion

The winter and spring dish of Najd, the Levant and Iraq when desert truffles appear.

Limited evidenceSafe for most peopleAuthentic (sahih) hadith
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Desert truffle (Limited).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Authentic (sahih) hadith.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
Authentic (sahih) hadith«Truffles are a kind of manna, and their water is a cure for the eye” (Bukhari and Muslim).

Ingredients (For one dish)

  • Desert truffle (faqaa): Half a kilo of desert truffles, peeled and cut
  • A chopped onion
  • Olives and olive oil: Two tablespoons of olive oil or ghee
  • Salt, black pepper and cumin

Method

  1. Scrub the truffles well with a brush to remove the sand, then peel and cut them.
  2. Fry the onion in the oil until golden.
  3. Add the truffles and spices and stir over a medium heat for 10–15 minutes until fully cooked.
How much and when? As part of food, several times a week.
Never drip truffle juice into the eye. Cook well, from a trusted source.
