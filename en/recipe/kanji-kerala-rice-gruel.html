Kanji (Kerala rice gruel)

Kerala's food for convalescence and an upset stomach: rice cooked in plenty of water and eaten with its liquid.

Moderate evidenceSafe for most peopleInherited folk custom
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Ginger (Relatively strong).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Inherited folk custom.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.

Ingredients (For one cup)

  • A quarter cup of rice
  • 4 cups of water
  • A pinch of salt
  • Ginger: A slice of ginger (optional)

Method

  1. Wash the rice well.
  2. Cook it in the water over low heat for 30–40 minutes until soft, with plenty of liquid left.
  3. Add the salt and eat it warm with its liquid.
How much and when? One cup 2–3 times a day until the symptoms go, for a week at most; if no better, see a doctor.
With severe diarrhoea, start with oral rehydration solution first, then kanji. For children, leave out the ginger.

🌿 From traditional medicine; most of its recipes have not been tested in large studies. Choose organic or clean local ingredients from a trusted source where you can, and if you do not improve, see a doctor.
