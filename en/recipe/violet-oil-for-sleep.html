Violet oil for sleep

In Andalusian medicine it was rubbed on the head and forehead to bring sleep and calm headache.

Limited evidenceSafe for most peopleClassical medical books
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Violet (Limited).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Classical medical books.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.

Ingredients (For a small jar)

  • Violet: A quarter cup of dried violet flowers
  • Olives and olive oil: Half a cup of olive or sweet almond oil

Method

  1. Put the flowers in the oil in a glass jar.
  2. Leave in a warm place for two weeks, shaking daily.
  3. Strain, and massage a little into the forehead and scalp before bed.
How much and when? A little in the evening before bed.
For external use. Test on the skin first.
