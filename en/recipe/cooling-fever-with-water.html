Cooling fever with water

Water to drink and relieving the body's heat, with rest — this is how many scholars understood the hadith, and it is what medical guidelines recommend today.

Authentic (sahih) hadithThe Prophet ﷺ said: “Fever is from the heat of Hell, so cool it with water” (Bukhari and Muslim).

Ingredients (For one cup)

  • Plenty of water and fluids to drink
  • Light clothing and a moderately cool room
  • A small towel dampened with lukewarm water (optional)

Method

  1. Reduce clothing and covers, and keep the room moderately cool.
  2. Drink plenty of water and fluids in small, frequent amounts.
  3. A lukewarm damp towel can be placed on the forehead for comfort if the person likes it; remove it if they shiver.
  4. If the fever persists or the person is very unwell, see a doctor.
How much and when? Throughout the fever, watching for danger signs.
Do not sponge a child's whole body with water, do not use iced water or alcohol, and do not wrap a feverish person in blankets. See a doctor at once for: any fever in a baby under 3 months, a temperature of 39° or more, fever lasting more than 3 days, or fever with a seizure, marked drowsiness or difficulty breathing.
