India (Ayurveda)

Triphala before bed

A gentle Ayurvedic laxative for constipation.

Limited evidenceNeeds caution for some groupsInherited folk custom
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Triphala (Limited).
  • Safety: Triphala: not suitable for some situations (pregnancy, breastfeeding, illnesses or medicines). Use «Suggest a blend» to find out whether it suits you.
  • Source: Inherited folk custom.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.

Ingredients (For one cup)

  • Triphala: Half a teaspoon of triphala powder
  • A cup of warm water

Method

  1. Dissolve the powder in the warm water.
  2. Drink before bed.
  3. Do not continue for more than two weeks without advice.
How much and when? Once before bed, for no more than two weeks without advice.
Forbidden in pregnancy and breastfeeding. Choose a licensed product free of heavy metals.
