Soaked fenugreek water

Eases mild constipation thanks to its gel-forming fibre.

Moderate evidenceNeeds caution for some groupsInherited folk custom
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Fenugreek (Moderate).
  • Safety: Fenugreek: not suitable for some situations (pregnancy, breastfeeding, illnesses or medicines). Use «Suggest a blend» to find out whether it suits you.
  • Source: Inherited folk custom.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.

Ingredients (For one cup)

  • Fenugreek: One teaspoon of fenugreek seeds
  • A cup of water

Method

  1. Soak the seeds in the water overnight.
  2. Drink the water in the morning and eat the softened seeds.
  3. Drink enough water during the day.
How much and when? Once a day in the morning for 4–6 weeks, then a week's break.
Avoid in pregnancy. Keep two hours apart from your medicines, as it may reduce their absorption.

🌿 From traditional medicine; most of its recipes have not been tested in large studies. Choose organic or clean local ingredients from a trusted source where you can, and if you do not improve, see a doctor.
