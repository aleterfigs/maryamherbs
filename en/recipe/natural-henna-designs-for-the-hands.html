Natural henna designs for the hands

Henna for celebrations the traditional way, without chemicals.

Limited evidenceSafe for most peopleInherited folk custom
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Henna (Limited).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Inherited folk custom.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.

Ingredients (For one cup)

  • Henna: 3 tablespoons of fine pure henna
  • Juice of a lemon and one teaspoon of sugar
  • A little warm water

Method

  1. Mix the henna with the lemon, sugar and water until like toothpaste, and leave covered for 6–8 hours.
  2. Draw designs with a paper cone on a clean, dry hand.
  3. Leave for 4–6 hours, then scrape it off rather than washing, and avoid water for 12 hours so the colour darkens.
How much and when? On special occasions.
Not for babies and young children, or people with G6PD deficiency. Avoid instant «black henna».
