Fresh pomegranate juice

A juice rich in antioxidants.

Limited evidenceSafe for most peopleClassical medical books
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Pomegranate and its peel (Limited).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Classical medical books.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
Verse of the QuranMentioned in the Quran: “In both of them are fruit, palm trees and pomegranates” [Quran 55:68].

Ingredients (For one cup)

  • Pomegranate and its peel: One pomegranate

Method

  1. Seed it and press the seeds.
  2. Drink fresh.
How much and when? One cup once or twice a day; can be continued 4–6 weeks, then a week's break.
Caution with warfarin. For diabetics, half a cup.
