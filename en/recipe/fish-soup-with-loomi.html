Fish soup with loomi

A warm Gulf recovery meal rich in protein and omega-3.

Limited evidenceNeeds caution for some groupsInherited folk custom
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Cumin (Limited).
  • Safety: Turmeric: not suitable for some situations (pregnancy, breastfeeding, illnesses or medicines). Use «Suggest a blend» to find out whether it suits you.
  • Source: Inherited folk custom.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.

Ingredients (For 4 people)

  • Fish oil (omega-3): Half a kilo of white fish or kingfish, cut up (kingfish is rich in omega-3)
  • Loomi (dried black lime): A pierced loomi
  • Turmeric: A quarter teaspoon of turmeric
  • Cumin: Half a teaspoon of cumin
  • An onion, a tomato and a garlic clove
  • A litre of water and a little salt

Method

  1. Sauté the onion and garlic in a little oil, then add the tomato and spices.
  2. Add the water and loomi and let it boil for 10 minutes.
  3. Add the fish and cook over a low heat for 10–15 minutes until done.
  4. Carefully remove the bones before serving to children.
How much and when? A bowl two or three times a week
Watch for bones with children and older people. Not suitable for people allergic to fish.
