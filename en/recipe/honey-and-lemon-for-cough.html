Honey and lemon for cough

To soothe night-time cough in children aged one and over and in adults. Honey has relatively good evidence here.

Traditional, no studiesSafe for most peopleModern home recipe
  • Evidence: There are no studies on its ingredients for this problem; it relies on traditional use.
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Modern home recipe.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.

Ingredients (For one dose)

  • One teaspoon of honey (half a teaspoon for ages 1–5)
  • A few drops of lemon
  • Warm water (optional)

Method

  1. Mix the honey with the lemon, or dissolve it in half a cup of warm water.
  2. Give half an hour before bed.
  3. Clean the mouth or teeth afterwards.
How much and when? One dose before bed, and it can be repeated once in the day, on cough days only.
Honey is completely forbidden under one year (risk of botulism). See a doctor if the cough comes with shortness of breath, wheezing or high fever.

🌿 From traditional medicine; most of its recipes have not been tested in large studies. Choose organic or clean local ingredients from a trusted source where you can, and if you do not improve, see a doctor.
