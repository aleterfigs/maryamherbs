Watermelon with fresh dates

A hydrating summer food combining the sweetness of dates and the coolness of watermelon.

Limited evidenceSafe for most peopleAuthentic (sahih) hadith
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Dates and fresh dates (Limited).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Authentic (sahih) hadith.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
Authentic (sahih) hadithHe ﷺ used to eat watermelon with fresh dates and say: «We break the heat of this with the coolness of this, and the coolness of this with the heat of this» (Abu Dawud and Tirmidhi, graded authentic by al-Albani).

Ingredients (For one cup)

  • Dates and fresh dates (ajwa): 3 Fresh dates
  • A slice of watermelon

Method

  1. Eat the dates with the watermelon.
  2. Good after exertion in the heat.
How much and when? As part of food, several times a week.
For diabetics: a small amount.
