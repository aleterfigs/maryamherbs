Artichokes braised in oil

A Mediterranean dish for indigestion and cholesterol support.

Relatively strong evidenceNeeds caution for some groupsInherited folk custom
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Olives and olive oil (Relatively strong).
  • Safety: Artichoke: not suitable for some situations (pregnancy, breastfeeding, illnesses or medicines). Use «Suggest a blend» to find out whether it suits you.
  • Source: Inherited folk custom.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.

Ingredients (For one dish)

  • Artichoke: Two trimmed artichoke heads
  • Olives and olive oil: Two spoons of olive oil
  • Lemon and garlic

Method

  1. Cook the artichoke hearts in water and lemon for 20 minutes.
  2. Drain and add the oil, garlic and salt.
How much and when? As part of food, several times a week.
Avoid with gallstones.
