Strong Sudanese karkadeh

Hibiscus as drunk in Sudan, deep red and refreshing.

Moderate evidenceNeeds caution for some groupsInherited folk custom
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Hibiscus (Moderate).
  • Safety: Hibiscus: not suitable for some situations (pregnancy, breastfeeding, illnesses or medicines). Use «Suggest a blend» to find out whether it suits you.
  • Source: Inherited folk custom.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.

Ingredients (For one litre)

  • Hibiscus (karkadeh): Half a cup of hibiscus
  • Mint: Mint leaves
  • A litre of water

Method

  1. Steep the hibiscus in cold water for 8 hours.
  2. Strain and add the mint.
  3. Serve cold with a little sugar.
How much and when? One to two cups a day, checking blood pressure regularly.
People on blood-pressure medicines should ask their doctor.
