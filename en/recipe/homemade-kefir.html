Homemade kefir

Fermented milk rich in many kinds of good bacteria and yeasts.

Traditional, no studiesSafe for most peopleInherited folk custom
  • Evidence: There are no studies on its ingredients for this problem; it relies on traditional use.
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Inherited folk custom.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.

Ingredients (For two cups)

  • Two spoons of kefir grains
  • Two cups of pasteurised milk
  • A clean glass jar and a plastic sieve

Method

  1. Put the grains in the jar and add the milk.
  2. Cover with a cloth and leave at room temperature for 18–24 hours until it thickens slightly.
  3. Strain, return the grains to fresh milk, and keep the kefir in the fridge.
How much and when? Half to one cup a day.
Cleanliness is essential. Avoid it with very weak immunity or during chemotherapy. Keep it two to three hours apart from the antibiotic dose, especially doxycycline and ciprofloxacin, because milk reduces their absorption.

🌿 From traditional medicine; most of its recipes have not been tested in large studies. Choose organic or clean local ingredients from a trusted source where you can, and if you do not improve, see a doctor.
