Sardine salad with lemon and cumin

A small, cheap fish rich in vitamin D, calcium (from its bones), omega-3 and B12.

Moderate evidenceSafe for most peopleInherited folk custom
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Olives and olive oil (Relatively strong).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Inherited folk custom.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.

Ingredients (For one cup)

  • A tin of sardines in olive oil or water (with bones)
  • Cumin: A pinch of cumin
  • Juice of a lemon, spring onion and parsley
  • Olives and olive oil: A spoon of olive oil

Method

  1. Open the tin and mash the sardines with their soft bones using a fork.
  2. Add the lemon, cumin, onion, parsley and oil.
  3. Eat with bread or salad.
How much and when? Once or twice a week.
Tinned sardines are salty; people with high blood pressure should choose low-salt.
