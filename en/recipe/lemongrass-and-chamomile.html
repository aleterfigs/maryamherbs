Lemongrass and chamomile

A lemon-scented evening drink.

Moderate evidenceNeeds caution for some groupsModern home recipe
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Chamomile (Moderate).
  • Safety: Lemongrass: not suitable for some situations (pregnancy, breastfeeding, illnesses or medicines). Use «Suggest a blend» to find out whether it suits you.
  • Source: Modern home recipe.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.

Ingredients (For one cup)

  • Lemongrass: A crushed lemongrass stalk
  • Chamomile: One teaspoon of chamomile

Method

  1. Steep both in a cup of boiling water for 8 minutes.
  2. Strain and drink in the evening.
How much and when? One cup in the evening an hour before bed; can be drunk daily with a week's break each month.
Forbidden in pregnancy.
