Henna for cracked feet

A traditional use that cools the feet and softens cracks.

Limited evidenceSafe for most peopleGood (hasan) hadith
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Henna (Limited).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Good (hasan) hadith.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
Good (hasan) hadithNo one complained to the Messenger of God ﷺ of pain in the legs without him saying: “Dye them with henna” (Abu Dawud and Tirmidhi; graded good by some scholars).

Ingredients (For one cup)

  • Henna: 3 tablespoons of pure henna
  • Warm water

Method

  1. Mix the henna and leave for an hour.
  2. Apply to the soles, wrap the feet in a bag and leave for two hours.
  3. Wash off and apply oil.
How much and when? Once a week.
Black henna is forbidden. People with G6PD deficiency must not use it.
