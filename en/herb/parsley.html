Parsley

Petroselinum crispum
See real photos of the plant ↗
Other names
المعدنوس · Parsley
Part used
Leaves
Strength of evidence
Limited
For children
As food only.
Urinary tractDigestion & bloatingKidneysEasy to grow at home

Common uses

  • Increasing urine (folk use)
  • Flavour and vitamins in food

Usual preparation

A handful of leaves in a cup of boiling water for 10 minutes.

Cautions

  • Medicinal amounts are forbidden in pregnancy
  • Avoid the infusion with kidney disease
  • Celery and carrot family

Possible drug interactions

  • Warfarin (rich in vitamin K)
  • Diuretics and blood-pressure medicines
  • Lithium

Growing and propagation

Easy in the Gulf
Origin and climate
A biennial from the Mediterranean
In the Gulf
Easy in winter: sow October–February
Light, water and soil
Sun or part shade, regular watering
Propagation
By seed: soak overnight, as it is slow to germinate (2–3 weeks)
Harvest
The outer leaves first so it keeps growing

🧪 Soil acidity (pH)from 6 to 7Gulf soil is alkaline (7.5–8.5): mix in organic compost and a little agricultural sulphur, or grow it in a pot with ready-made potting mix.
