Sheep tail fat (alya)

Ovis aries (tail fat)
See real photos ↗
Other names
الإلية، الشحم، اللية · Sheep tail fat
Part used
Fat from a sheep's tail
Strength of evidence
Limited
For children
Not given to children.
Pain & inflammationFrom animals

In Islamic heritage

Authentic (sahih) hadithThe Prophet ﷺ said: «The cure for sciatica is the tail fat of a desert sheep, melted, then divided into three parts, and one part drunk each day on an empty stomach» (Ibn Majah, graded authentic by al-Albani).

Common uses

  • Sciatica: there is a hadith about it, but no scientific studies
  • Rubbed on joints and dry skin in folk use

Usual preparation

In the hadith: the tail fat of a desert sheep is melted, divided into three parts, and one part is drunk each day on an empty stomach.

Cautions

  • Very high in saturated fat: not suitable for heart disease, high cholesterol, obesity or diabetes
  • Sciatica with leg weakness, numbness around the back passage or difficulty passing urine: emergency now
  • Pain lasting more than 6 weeks needs a doctor

Possible drug interactions

  • No known drug interactions
