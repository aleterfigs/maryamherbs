Horse chestnut

Aesculus hippocastanum
See real photos of the plant ↗
Other names
أبو فروة الحصان · Horse chestnut
Part used
Seeds (standardised extract)
Strength of evidence
Moderate
For children
Not recommended for children except as prescribed by a doctor.
Haemorrhoids

Common uses

  • Chronic venous insufficiency and varicose veins (standardised extract)

Usual preparation

A standardised pharmacy product only.

Cautions

  • The raw seeds and leaves are poisonous
  • Not for pregnancy or breastfeeding

Possible drug interactions

  • Blood thinners
  • Diabetes medicines

Growing and propagation

Not usually grown in the Gulf; bought
Origin and climate
A tree of temperate regions
In the Gulf
Does not suit the Gulf, and its raw seeds are poisonous
