Psyllium (ispaghula)

Plantago ovata
See real photos of the plant ↗
Other names
بزر قطونا، الإسبغول · Psyllium
Part used
Seed husks
Strength of evidence
Relatively strong
For children
From age 6 in a smaller amount with plenty of water, after asking the doctor.

Common uses

  • Constipation
  • Mild diarrhoea (regulates stool consistency)
  • Lowering cholesterol
  • Irritable bowel

Usual preparation

One teaspoon in a full cup of water, stirred and drunk at once, then another cup of water.

Cautions

  • Never take without enough water (risk of choking and blockage)
  • Avoid with difficulty swallowing or narrowed bowel
  • May cause bloating at first

Possible drug interactions

  • Reduces absorption of medicines: take two hours apart
  • Diabetes medicines (may lower blood sugar)
