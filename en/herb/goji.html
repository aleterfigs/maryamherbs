Goji berry (wolfberry)

Lycium barbarum
See real photos of the plant ↗
Other names
توت الذئب، القوجي · Goji berry
Part used
Fruit
Strength of evidence
Limited
For children
Not recommended for children except as prescribed by a doctor.
Fatigue & recovery

Common uses

  • Exhaustion (traditionally)
  • Added to tea and porridge

Usual preparation

One tablespoon of dried berries in tea or porridge.

Cautions

  • One case of bleeding with warfarin reported: avoid with blood thinners
  • Nightshade family: caution for people sensitive to it

Possible drug interactions

  • Warfarin
  • Diabetes medicines
  • Blood-pressure medicines

Growing and propagation

Possible with care
Origin and climate
A drought-tolerant shrub from China
In the Gulf
Lives in the Gulf with part shade in summer

Sun, moderate watering, well-drained soil

By seed or cuttings
