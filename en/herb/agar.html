Agar (agar-agar, kanten)

Agar (Gelidium / Gracilaria)
See real photos ↗
Other names
أجار، الجيلاتين النباتي · Agar agar, kanten
Part used
A gel from red algae
Strength of evidence
Limited
For children
Not recommended for children except as prescribed by a doctor.
From the seaConstipationWeight

Common uses

  • Mild constipation (fibre)
  • A light, filling dessert

Usual preparation

Boil half a teaspoon of powder in a cup of liquid for two minutes, then leave to set (see the recipe). Drink enough water with it.

Cautions

  • Drink plenty of water with it, or it may block the oesophagus or bowel
  • Not for people with difficulty swallowing

Possible drug interactions

  • Take two hours apart from medicines (may reduce their absorption)
