Echinacea

Echinacea purpurea
See real photos of the plant ↗
Other names
القنفذية · Echinacea
Part used
Roots and aerial parts
Strength of evidence
Limited
For children
Not recommended for children except as prescribed by a doctor.
Colds & cough

Common uses

  • The start of a cold

Usual preparation

An infusion or pharmacy product, for no more than 10 days.

Cautions

  • Daisy family: caution for people allergic to it
  • Avoid with autoimmune diseases
  • Not recommended under age 12

Possible drug interactions

  • Immunosuppressants
  • Some medicines processed by the liver

Growing and propagation

Hard in the Gulf
