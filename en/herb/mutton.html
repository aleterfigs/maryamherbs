Lamb and mutton

Ovis aries
See real photos ↗
Other names
لحم الغنم، لحم الخروف، لحم الضأن، اللحم الأحمر · Lamb, mutton
Part used
Meat and broth
Strength of evidence
Moderate
For children
Mashed meat from 6 months is an excellent source of iron for babies.
Fatigue & recoveryPostpartumFrom animals

In Islamic heritage

Authentic (sahih) hadithThe Prophet ﷺ was brought meat and the foreleg was offered to him, which he liked, and he bit from it (Bukhari and Muslim).

Common uses

  • Anaemia: iron from meat is absorbed more easily than iron from plants
  • Meat broth for women after childbirth and the weak in tradition
  • Tharid and meat for the sick and those fasting

Usual preparation

Cook as a broth or grill; leaner cuts are better.

Cautions

  • People with gout should cut down on red meat and offal, which raise uric acid
  • Heart disease and high cholesterol: lean red meat two or three times a week, with the fat trimmed
  • Eating a lot of processed meat, such as sausages and smoked meat, is linked to more bowel cancer

Possible drug interactions

  • No important drug interactions
