Harmal (Syrian rue)

Peganum harmala
See real photos of the plant ↗
Other names
الحرمل البري · Harmal
Part used
Seeds
Strength of evidence
Unsafe
For children
Not recommended for children except as prescribed by a doctor.

Common uses

  • We do not recommend any internal use

Usual preparation

Not to be eaten or drunk. Poisonings from drinking it are recorded every year.

Cautions

  • Poisoning causes hallucinations, tremor, vomiting and a fall in blood pressure and heart function
  • Causes miscarriage
  • Forbidden for children

Possible drug interactions

  • Dangerous with antidepressants and many medicines (it inhibits the MAO enzyme)

Growing and propagation

Not recommended to grow
Origin and climate
A wild desert plant of the Gulf and the Levant
In the Gulf
Grows wild; we do not recommend growing it at home — its seeds are poisonous
