Amla (Indian gooseberry)

Phyllanthus emblica
See real photos of the plant ↗
Other names
الأملج، عنب الثعلب الهندي · Amla
Part used
Fruit
Strength of evidence
Limited
For children
As fruit or jam in small amounts.
Fatigue & recoveryColds & coughBlood sugar & lipids

Common uses

  • A source of vitamin C
  • Possible support for lipids and blood sugar (small studies)
  • Strengthening hair (traditional use)

Usual preparation

Eaten fresh with salt, as a powder with honey, or as diluted juice.

Cautions

  • Very sour: may irritate the stomach and reflux
  • May lower blood sugar

Possible drug interactions

  • Blood thinners and antiplatelet drugs
  • Diabetes medicines
