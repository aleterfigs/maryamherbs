Powdered pearl and coral

Pearl & coral powder
See real photos ↗
Other names
اللؤلؤ، المرجان، كالسيوم المرجان · Pearl powder, coral calcium
Part used
Shell powder
Strength of evidence
Unsafe
For children
Not recommended for children except as prescribed by a doctor.
From the sea

In Islamic heritage

Verse of the QuranGod says: “From both of them come pearls and coral” [Quran 55:22]. Muslim physicians mentioned them in their books of simples, but today eating them is not recommended.

Common uses

  • We do not recommend eating it

Usual preparation

We do not recommend using it. If you need calcium, get it from milk, yoghurt and sardines, or a pharmacy supplement.

Cautions

  • Heavy metals such as lead have been found in some powders
  • «Coral calcium» is no better than ordinary calcium, and claims that it treats diseases are false
  • Too much calcium causes kidney stones and constipation

Possible drug interactions

  • Antibiotics and thyroid medicine (reduces their absorption)
