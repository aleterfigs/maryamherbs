Bitter orange (peel extract)

Citrus aurantium (extract)
See real photos of the plant ↗
Other names
النارنج المرّ · Bitter orange extract
Part used
Fruit rind (extract)
Strength of evidence
Unsafe
For children
Not recommended for children except as prescribed by a doctor.
Weight

Common uses

  • We do not recommend it for weight loss

Usual preparation

We do not recommend using it.

Cautions

  • Raises blood pressure and heart rate, especially with caffeine
  • Strokes and heart attacks have been recorded with products containing it
  • Forbidden with heart disease, high blood pressure and in pregnancy

Possible drug interactions

  • Blood-pressure and heart medicines
  • Antidepressants
  • Stimulants and caffeine
