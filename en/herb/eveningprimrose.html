Evening primrose oil

Oenothera biennis
See real photos of the plant ↗
Other names
زهرة الربيع · Evening primrose
Part used
Seed oil
Strength of evidence
Limited
For children
Not recommended for children except as prescribed by a doctor.
Women's healthSkin & wounds

Common uses

  • Premenstrual symptoms (weak evidence)
  • Eczema (weak evidence)

Usual preparation

Capsules as per the leaflet.

Cautions

  • May cause an upset stomach and headache
  • Avoid before surgery

Possible drug interactions

  • Blood thinners
  • Some psychiatric medicines (phenothiazines)

Growing and propagation
