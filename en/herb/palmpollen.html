Date palm pollen

Phoenix dactylifera (pollen)
See real photos of the plant ↗
Other names
لقاح النخيل، طلع النخل، دقيق اللقاح · Date palm pollen
Part used
Pollen from the male palm
Strength of evidence
Limited
For children
Not given to children.
Sexual health

In Islamic heritage

Authentic (sahih) hadithThe people of Madinah used to pollinate their palms, and the Prophet ﷺ told them: «You know best the affairs of your worldly life» (Muslim).

Common uses

  • Fertility: animal studies, plus one small trial in men who took it mixed with black seed and had some improvement in semen tests, so its effect alone is unknown

Usual preparation

Traditionally: a quarter teaspoon with a spoonful of honey. There is no scientifically established dose.

Cautions

  • One of the most common causes of allergy and asthma in the Gulf: not for anyone with allergies or asthma
  • Not used in pregnancy or breastfeeding, nor for children
  • Difficulty conceiving needs both partners checked by a doctor; it is not a substitute
  • Buy it clean from a trusted source, as it can be contaminated with moulds

Possible drug interactions

  • No known interactions, but it may affect hormones; anyone in fertility treatment should ask their doctor
