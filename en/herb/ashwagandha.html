Ashwagandha

Withania somnifera
See real photos of the plant ↗
Other names
الويثانيا · Ashwagandha
Part used
Root
Strength of evidence
Moderate
For children
Not recommended for children except as prescribed by a doctor.
Sleep & anxietyFatigue & recovery

Common uses

  • Stress and anxiety
  • Better sleep
  • Exhaustion

Usual preparation

Root powder or a standardised product as per the leaflet, often in warm milk in the evening.

Cautions

  • Not for pregnancy or breastfeeding
  • May raise thyroid hormones
  • A case of adrenal suppression was reported that resolved on stopping it
  • Rare reports of liver injury: stop it if the eyes turn yellow or the urine dark
  • Avoid with autoimmune diseases
  • Lead has been found in some imported products; buy from a licensed source

Possible drug interactions

  • Sedatives and sleeping pills
  • Thyroid medicines
  • Immunosuppressants
  • Diabetes and blood-pressure medicines
