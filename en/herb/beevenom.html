Bee venom (bee-sting therapy)

Apitoxin
See real photos ↗
Other names
لسع النحل، العلاج بالنحل · Bee venom
Part used
Venom from bee stings
Strength of evidence
Unsafe
For children
Not for children.
Pain & inflammationFrom animals

Common uses

  • There is not enough evidence of benefit, and the risk outweighs any gain

Usual preparation

Not recommended.

Cautions

  • It can cause fatal anaphylaxis even in people stung before without problems, and deaths have been recorded
  • Anyone stung who has difficulty breathing, facial swelling or dizziness: emergency now

Possible drug interactions

  • Blood-pressure medicines (beta-blockers) make anaphylaxis more dangerous and harder to treat

Sources
