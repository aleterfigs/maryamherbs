Maca

Lepidium meyenii
See real photos of the plant ↗
Other names
ماكا · Maca
Part used
Root
Strength of evidence
Limited
For children
Not recommended for children except as prescribed by a doctor.
Fatigue & recoverySexual health

Common uses

  • Energy (limited evidence)

Usual preparation

One teaspoon of cooked (gelatinised) powder in juice or porridge.

Cautions

  • In moderation in pregnancy
  • May affect hormones in theory

Possible drug interactions

  • Hormone therapy (in theory)

Growing and propagation

Not usually grown in the Gulf; bought
Origin and climate
A root from the high Peruvian Andes
In the Gulf
Does not suit the Gulf
