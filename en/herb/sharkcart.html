Common uses

  • We do not recommend it

Usual preparation

We do not recommend it, and medical treatment must never be abandoned for it.

Cautions

  • No proven benefit for cancer, and delaying treatment for it can kill
  • May raise calcium and cause nausea and a bad taste
  • Forbidden in pregnancy and breastfeeding
  • Shark fishing threatens their survival

Possible drug interactions

  • Chemotherapy drugs
  • Calcium supplements
