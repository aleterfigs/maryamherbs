Baobab (gongolez)

Adansonia digitata
See real photos of the plant ↗
Other names
القنقليز · Baobab
Part used
Fruit pulp
Strength of evidence
Limited
For children
As a natural juice low in sugar.
Fatigue & recoveryDigestion & bloating

Common uses

  • A refreshing drink rich in vitamin C
  • Mild constipation (fibre)

Usual preparation

Two spoons of pulp powder in a cup of cold water or milk.

Cautions

  • Commercial juice may be high in sugar

Possible drug interactions

  • No important interactions known

Growing and propagation

Possible with care
