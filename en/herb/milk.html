Milk

Lac

Other names
الحليب، حليب البقر، حليب الغنم، اللبن الحليب · Milk
Part used
Milk of livestock
Strength of evidence
Moderate
For children
Full-fat milk from age one; before that, breast milk or formula.
Fatigue & recoveryFrom animals

In Islamic heritage

Good (hasan) hadithThe Prophet ﷺ said: «When one of you is given milk to drink, let him say: O God, bless it for us and give us more of it, for nothing suffices as both food and drink except milk» (Abu Dawud and Tirmidhi, who graded it good). It is also narrated: «Take cows' milk, for they graze on every kind of plant, and it is a cure for every disease» (al-Hakim, graded authentic by some scholars), while the addition «and their meat is a disease» is weak according to many of them.

Common uses

  • A source of calcium and protein for bones and growth
  • An easy food for the sick and convalescent
  • The base of talbina, aseeda and warm drinks

Usual preparation

Drink it pasteurised or boiled, warm or cold.

Cautions

  • Unpasteurised milk (including fresh camel milk) can transmit brucellosis and other diseases
  • People with lactose intolerance can choose yoghurt or lactose-free milk
  • Cow's milk should not be the main drink before age one

Possible drug interactions

  • Keep it two to four hours apart from some antibiotics (doxycycline and ciprofloxacin), iron and thyroid medicine
