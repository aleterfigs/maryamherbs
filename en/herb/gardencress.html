In Islamic heritage

Weak hadithIt is narrated: “What healing there is in the two bitter things: aloe and thuffa'” — thuffa' being garden cress (Abu Dawud as mursal; weak chain).

Common uses

  • A traditional food for new and nursing mothers
  • Constipation (gel)

Usual preparation

One teaspoon soaked or boiled in milk.

Cautions

  • Not for pregnancy (may stimulate the womb)
  • May affect the thyroid if overused

Possible drug interactions

  • Thyroid medicines
  • Blood thinners
  • Diabetes medicines
