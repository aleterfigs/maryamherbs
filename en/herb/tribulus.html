Tribulus (puncture vine)

Tribulus terrestris
See real photos of the plant ↗
Other names
الحسك، الشرشير، قطب الجمل · Tribulus
Part used
Fruit
Strength of evidence
Limited
For children
Not recommended for children except as prescribed by a doctor.
ProstateSexual health

Common uses

  • Sexual desire (weak and conflicting evidence)

Usual preparation

A pharmacy product as per the leaflet, if wanted.

Cautions

  • Not for pregnancy or breastfeeding
  • Rare reports of kidney and liver damage with overuse
  • May lower blood sugar

Possible drug interactions

  • Diabetes medicines
  • Blood-pressure and heart medicines
  • Lithium

Growing and propagation

Not recommended to grow
Origin and climate
A wild, spiny creeping plant
In the Gulf
Grows wild everywhere and is not cultivated: its spines hurt feet and tyres
