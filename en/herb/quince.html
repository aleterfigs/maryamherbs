Quince seeds (behdaneh)

Cydonia oblonga
See real photos of the plant ↗
Other names
بهدانه، حب السفرجل · Quince seeds
Part used
Whole seeds
Strength of evidence
Limited
For children
The strained gel of whole seeds from age 4.
Colds & coughMouth & throat

Common uses

  • Dry cough and an irritated throat
  • Dry mouth

Usual preparation

Soak one teaspoon of whole seeds in a cup of water for an hour until gel-like, then strain.

Cautions

  • Never grind or chew the seeds: inside they contain a substance that turns into cyanide
  • Drink only the strained gel

Possible drug interactions

  • No known interactions for the gel

Growing and propagation

Not usually grown in the Gulf; bought
Origin and climate
A tree of temperate regions
In the Gulf
Does not fruit well in the Gulf
Propagation
By grafting
Harvest
The fruit in autumn
