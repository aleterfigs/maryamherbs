Nutmeg

Myristica fragrans
See real photos of the plant ↗
Other names
جوز بوا · Nutmeg
Part used
The seed
Strength of evidence
Limited
For children
Not recommended for children except as prescribed by a doctor.
Sleep & anxietyDigestion & bloating

Common uses

  • A pinch in milk at bedtime (folk use)
  • Flavouring for food

Usual preparation

A very small pinch (less than a quarter teaspoon).

Cautions

  • Two teaspoons or more can cause poisoning, hallucinations and palpitations
  • Avoid more than food amounts in pregnancy
  • Keep away from children

Possible drug interactions

  • Sedatives (in large amounts)

Growing and propagation

Not usually grown in the Gulf; bought
Origin and climate
A tropical tree from Indonesia
In the Gulf
Not grown in the Gulf
