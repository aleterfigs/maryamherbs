Hibiscus (karkadeh)

Hibiscus sabdariffa
See real photos of the plant ↗
Other names
الكركدي، الوردة الحبشية · Hibiscus / Roselle
Part used
Flower calyces
Strength of evidence
Moderate
For children
Not recommended for children except as prescribed by a doctor.
Blood pressureEasy to grow at home

Common uses

  • Support for mildly raised blood pressure
  • A refreshing drink, cold or hot

Usual preparation

One tablespoon of hibiscus in a cup of boiling water for 5–10 minutes, or steeped cold for several hours.

Cautions

  • May lower blood pressure too much in people taking BP medicines
  • Avoid in pregnancy

Possible drug interactions

  • Blood-pressure medicines and diuretics (added effect)
  • Acetaminophen (paracetamol)
  • Chloroquine

Growing and propagation

Easy in the Gulf
Origin and climate
A tropical annual that loves heat
In the Gulf
Sown in spring (March–April); takes the summer and gives red calyces in autumn
Light, water and soil
Full sun, regular watering, light soil
Propagation
By seed in spring
Harvest
Pick the fleshy red calyces two weeks after the flower drops, remove the seed pod and dry
🌡️ Ideal temperaturefrom 24 to 35 °CStruggles above 45°C · harmed below 12°C
🧂 Tolerance of salty waterModerateTolerates water up to about 2,000–3,000 ppm, with the soil flushed with fresh water now and then.
🧪 Soil acidity (pH)from 5.5 to 7.5Suits the Gulf's alkaline soil.
