Asian ginseng

Panax ginseng
See real photos of the plant ↗
Other names
جنسنغ · Ginseng
Part used
Root
Strength of evidence
Limited
For children
Not recommended for children except as prescribed by a doctor.
Fatigue & recoverySexual health

Common uses

  • Exhaustion (limited evidence)

Usual preparation

A standardised product as per the leaflet, for a short time.

Cautions

  • May cause insomnia, headache and raised blood pressure
  • Forbidden in pregnancy, breastfeeding and for children

Possible drug interactions

  • Warfarin
  • Diabetes medicines
  • Antidepressants
  • Stimulants

Growing and propagation

Not usually grown in the Gulf; bought
Origin and climate
A plant of cold, shady forests in Korea and China
In the Gulf
Does not suit the Gulf
