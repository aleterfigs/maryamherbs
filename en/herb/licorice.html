Liquorice

Glycyrrhiza glabra
See real photos of the plant ↗
Other names
العرقسوس، السوس، موليتي · Licorice
Part used
Root
Strength of evidence
Moderate
For children
Not recommended for children except as prescribed by a doctor.
Digestion & bloatingMouth & throatColds & cough

Common uses

  • Soothing a sore throat
  • Heartburn (DGL products with glycyrrhizin removed)

Usual preparation

Strict moderation is best; the daily folk drink over long periods is not advisable.

Cautions

  • Raises blood pressure and lowers potassium when overused or taken continuously
  • Not for people with heart disease, kidney disease or high blood pressure
  • Avoid in pregnancy

Possible drug interactions

  • Blood-pressure medicines and diuretics
  • Digoxin
  • Cortisone
  • Warfarin
