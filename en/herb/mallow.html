Mallow (khubbeiza)

Malva sylvestris
See real photos of the plant ↗
Other names
الخباز، الخبيز · Mallow

Leaves and flowers

Digestion & bloatingColds & coughEasy to grow at home

Common uses

  • Mild constipation (a slippery food)
  • Cough and an irritated throat (the flowers)

Usual preparation

Cook the leaves like spinach, or steep the flowers in boiling water.

Cautions

  • Safe as food

Possible drug interactions

  • May reduce absorption of oral medicines
