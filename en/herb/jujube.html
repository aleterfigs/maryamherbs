Jujube (unnab)

Ziziphus jujuba
See real photos of the plant ↗
Other names
العناب · Jujube
Part used
Fruit
Strength of evidence
Limited
For children
As cooked or dried fruit with the stones removed.
Sleep & anxietyColds & coughFatigue & recoveryEasy to grow at home

Common uses

  • Dry cough and a rough chest
  • Better sleep (traditionally)
  • Exhaustion

Usual preparation

5–6 Split dried fruits and boil in water for 15 minutes.

Cautions

  • Generally safe as food
  • May slightly increase drowsiness
  • Contains sugars

Possible drug interactions

  • Sedatives (mild effect)

Growing and propagation

Easy in the Gulf

Full sun, moderate watering, tolerates poor soil
