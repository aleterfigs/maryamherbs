Wormwood (shiba)

Artemisia absinthium
See real photos of the plant ↗
Other names
الأفسنتين · Wormwood
Part used
Leaves
Strength of evidence
Unsafe
For children
Not recommended for children except as prescribed by a doctor.
Digestion & bloating

Common uses

  • Traditional use for indigestion (not recommended)

Usual preparation

We do not recommend it. If added to tea, one leaf rarely, not daily.

Cautions

  • Thujone can cause convulsions, seizures and kidney damage in large amounts
  • Completely forbidden in pregnancy, breastfeeding, epilepsy, kidney disease and for children

Possible drug interactions

  • Epilepsy medicines

Growing and propagation

Not recommended to grow
Origin and climate
A perennial of temperate regions
In the Gulf
We do not recommend growing it at home; toxic in large amounts
