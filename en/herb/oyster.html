Oysters and shellfish (and small sardines)

Common uses

  • Correcting low zinc, iron and B12
  • Food for recovery

Usual preparation

Eat well cooked from a trusted source, once or twice a week.

Cautions

  • Raw oysters are dangerous: they may carry hepatitis and bacteria, especially for people with liver disease, diabetes, weak immunity and pregnant women
  • Shellfish allergy is one of the most common food allergies and can be severe
  • Avoid with gout (rich in purines)

Possible drug interactions

  • Zinc and iron supplements (not needed with a regular diet)
