Eucalyptus

Eucalyptus globulus
See real photos of the plant ↗
Other names
الكافور، الأوكالبتوس · Eucalyptus
Part used
Leaves, oil
Strength of evidence
Moderate
For children
Not recommended for children except as prescribed by a doctor.
Colds & cough

Common uses

  • Nasal congestion (inhalation)
  • Diluted chest rubs for adults

Usual preparation

Leaves in hot water to inhale, or drops of diluted oil.

Cautions

  • The oil is poisonous if swallowed, especially by children
  • Do not put it on the faces of babies and young children (may cause breathing spasm)
  • Avoid the steam with asthma

Possible drug interactions

  • No interactions for external use
