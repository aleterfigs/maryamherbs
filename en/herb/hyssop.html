Hyssop (zoufa)

Hyssopus officinalis
See real photos of the plant ↗
Other names
الزوفى · Hyssop
Part used
Aerial parts
Strength of evidence
Limited
For children
Not recommended for children except as prescribed by a doctor.
Colds & cough

Common uses

  • Cough and phlegm (traditional use)

Usual preparation

One teaspoon in a cup of boiling water for 7 minutes, for a few days.

Cautions

  • Not for pregnancy or breastfeeding
  • Forbidden with epilepsy
  • The essential oil must not be swallowed

Possible drug interactions

  • Epilepsy medicines
