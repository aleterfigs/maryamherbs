Aqit (dried yogurt)

Dried fermented milk
See real photos ↗
Other names
البقل، الكشك، الجميد، المضير · Dried yogurt (aqit)
Part used
Dried yogurt
Strength of evidence
Limited
For children
Dissolved in food, never given in pieces to little ones; it is salty.
Fatigue & recoveryFrom animals

In Islamic heritage

Authentic (sahih) hadithAqit was a food of the Arabs in the Prophet's ﷺ time, and the hadith on zakat al-fitr mentions «a sa' of aqit» (Bukhari and Muslim).

Common uses

  • A preserved source of protein and calcium
  • Food for travel and the desert

Usual preparation

Eat in small pieces, or soak and dissolve in water or broth.

Cautions

  • High in salt: people with high blood pressure or kidney disease should keep it to a minimum
  • Hard: never give pieces to young children because of choking risk
  • Buy from a clean source; aqit sun-dried without oversight can be contaminated

Possible drug interactions

  • No important drug interactions
