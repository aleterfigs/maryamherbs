Nettle

Urtica dioica
See real photos of the plant ↗
Other names
القريص، الحريق · Stinging nettle
Part used
Leaves, root
Strength of evidence
Limited
For children
Not recommended for children except as prescribed by a doctor.
Pain & inflammationProstateSeasonal allergies

Common uses

  • Joint pain (traditionally)
  • Seasonal allergies
  • Enlarged-prostate symptoms (the root, moderate evidence)

Usual preparation

One teaspoon of dried leaves in a cup of boiling water for 10 minutes.

Cautions

  • Fresh leaves sting: use dried or cooked
  • Avoid in pregnancy
  • Diuretic

Possible drug interactions

  • Blood-pressure medicines and diuretics
  • Diabetes medicines
  • Warfarin
  • Lithium
