Ginkgo

Ginkgo biloba
See real photos of the plant ↗
Other names
الجنكو · Ginkgo
Part used
Leaves (extract)
Strength of evidence
Limited
For children
Not recommended for children except as prescribed by a doctor.
Focus & memory

Common uses

  • Memory and focus (evidence has not shown benefit)

Usual preparation

Standardised extract only; the raw seeds are poisonous.

Cautions

  • Increases the risk of bleeding
  • Stop before surgery
  • May trigger epileptic seizures

Possible drug interactions

  • Blood thinners and antiplatelet drugs
  • Epilepsy medicines
  • Some antidepressants

Growing and propagation

Not usually grown in the Gulf; bought
Origin and climate
A tree from China that needs a cold winter
In the Gulf
Not suited to the Gulf
