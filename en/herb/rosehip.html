Rose hip (kuşburnu)

Rosa canina
See real photos of the plant ↗
Other names
النسرين، كوشبرنو · Rose hip
Part used
Fruit
Strength of evidence
Moderate
For children
A diluted, well-strained tea from age 4.
Colds & coughPain & inflammation

Common uses

  • A winter drink rich in vitamin C
  • Osteoarthritis pain (the powder)

Usual preparation

Boil one tablespoon of crushed dried hips in a cup of water for 10 minutes, then strain well.

Cautions

  • Strain well, as the hairs inside the fruit irritate the throat

Possible drug interactions

  • Blood thinners (in theory, with the powder)

Growing and propagation

Not usually grown in the Gulf; bought
Origin and climate
The wild rose of cool temperate regions
In the Gulf
Does not suit the Gulf
