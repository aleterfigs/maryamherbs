Camel meat

Camelus dromedarius
See real photos ↗
Other names
لحم الجمل، لحم الحاشي، لحم البعير · Camel meat
Part used
The meat
Strength of evidence
Limited
For children
Well cooked and finely minced.
Fatigue & recoveryFrom animals

In Islamic heritage

Authentic (sahih) hadithThe Prophet ﷺ was asked: Should we perform ablution after eating camel meat? He said: «Yes, perform ablution after camel meat» (Muslim).

Common uses

  • A source of iron and protein with less fat
  • Traditionally: for strength and after illness

Usual preparation

It needs long, slow cooking as it is tougher than other meats.

Cautions

  • Cook it well; camels can carry the Middle East respiratory syndrome virus (MERS), so wash your hands after touching them
  • People with gout should limit it, like other red meats

Possible drug interactions

  • No important drug interactions
