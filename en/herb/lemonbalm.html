Lemon balm (melissa)

Melissa officinalis
See real photos of the plant ↗
Other names
الترنجان، الحشيشة الليمونية · Lemon balm
Part used
Leaves
Strength of evidence
Moderate
For children
Not recommended for children except as prescribed by a doctor.
Sleep & anxietyDigestion & bloating

Common uses

  • Mild anxiety and stress
  • Trouble sleeping (often with valerian)
  • Nervous indigestion

Usual preparation

Infusion: one teaspoon to one tablespoon in a cup of hot water for 10 minutes.

Cautions

  • May cause drowsiness: take care when driving
  • Safety in pregnancy and breastfeeding is unknown

Possible drug interactions

  • Sedatives and sleeping pills (theoretical)

Growing and propagation

Possible with care
