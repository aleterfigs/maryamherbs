Shih (desert wormwood)

Artemisia herba-alba
See real photos of the plant ↗
Other names
شيح · White wormwood
Part used
Aerial parts
Strength of evidence
Limited
For children
Not recommended for children except as prescribed by a doctor.
Digestion & bloatingBlood sugar & lipids

Common uses

  • Indigestion (folk use)
  • Drunk as a folk remedy for blood sugar (weak evidence)

Usual preparation

We have not included a blend with it. If drunk, a small pinch rarely, not daily.

Cautions

  • Repeated use may cause convulsions and nerve damage
  • Forbidden in pregnancy, breastfeeding, for children and with epilepsy
  • It does not replace diabetes medicine

Possible drug interactions

  • Epilepsy medicines
  • Diabetes medicines
