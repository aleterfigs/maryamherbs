Bone and trotter broth

Bone broth

Other names
الكوارع، مرق العظم، الباجة، الكراعين · Bone broth
Part used
Bones and joints cooked for a long time
Strength of evidence
Limited
For children
Low-salt broth with the fat removed, from one year.
Pain & inflammationPostpartumFrom animals

Common uses

  • Traditionally: for joints and bones and after fractures
  • For women after childbirth and the weak
  • A warm, easy food in cold weather and illness

Usual preparation

Wash the bones and simmer with onion and spices for 3–6 hours, skimming the fat from the surface.

Cautions

  • The evidence that it strengthens joints is limited; collagen is digested into amino acids like any protein
  • It can be high in salt and fat: skim the fat and use less salt for people with high blood pressure or heart disease
  • People with gout should limit concentrated broth

Possible drug interactions

  • No important drug interactions
