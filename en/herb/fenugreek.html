In Islamic heritage

Weak hadithA hadith is narrated about the virtue of fenugreek, but hadith scholars do not consider it authentic. Its use, however, is well known in Arab medicine.

Common uses

  • Support for blood sugar after meals
  • Constipation and indigestion
  • Increasing breast milk (traditional use, weak evidence)

Usual preparation

Soak the seeds in water overnight, or boil one teaspoon in a cup of water.

Cautions

  • Avoid in pregnancy (may stimulate contractions)
  • May give sweat and urine a distinctive smell
  • Allergy in people sensitive to chickpeas and peanuts

Possible drug interactions

  • Diabetes medicines
  • Blood thinners
  • May reduce absorption of oral medicines: take two hours apart
