Olives and olive oil

Olea europaea

In Islamic heritage

Authentic (sahih) hadithThe Prophet ﷺ said: “Eat olive oil and anoint yourselves with it, for it is from a blessed tree” (Tirmidhi and Ibn Majah; graded authentic by al-Albani).

Common uses

  • Heart and artery health as part of a balanced diet
  • Moisturising skin and hair
  • A mild laxative

Usual preparation

Extra-virgin oil on food without heavy cooking, or rubbed on the skin.

Cautions

  • High in calories
  • Concentrated olive-leaf extract may lower blood pressure and blood sugar

Possible drug interactions

  • Leaf extract with blood-pressure and diabetes medicines

Growing and propagation

Possible with care
