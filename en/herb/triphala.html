Triphala

Emblica + Terminalia chebula + T. bellirica
See real photos of the plant ↗
Other names
الإهليلج · Triphala
Part used
Powder of three fruits
Strength of evidence
Limited
For children
Not recommended for children except as prescribed by a doctor.
ConstipationDigestion & bloating

Common uses

  • Mild constipation
  • Regulating digestion

Usual preparation

Half to one teaspoon of powder in warm water at bedtime, or as per the leaflet.

Cautions

  • May cause diarrhoea or cramps
  • Not for pregnancy or breastfeeding
  • Heavy metals have been found in some imported products; buy from a licensed source

Possible drug interactions

  • May reduce absorption of oral medicines: take two hours apart
  • Blood thinners

Sources

  • List of official monographs of the European Medicines Agency (EMA) — search it by the Latin name: Emblica + Terminalia chebula + T. bellirica
