Camel milk

Lac cameli
See real photos ↗
Other names
لبن الإبل، حليب النوق، حليب الناقة · Camel milk
Part used
She-camel's milk
Strength of evidence
Limited
For children
Pasteurised only, and as a drink from one year; not given instead of a baby's milk except under a doctor's supervision.
Blood sugar & lipidsFatigue & recoveryFrom animals

In Islamic heritage

Authentic (sahih) hadithIn the hadith of the people of Urayna, the Prophet ﷺ told them to join the charity camels and drink their milk and urine, and they recovered (Bukhari and Muslim). Doctors today warn against drinking urine because it has been shown to transmit disease.

Common uses

  • Diabetes: small trials found it may lower blood sugar slightly alongside treatment; the evidence is limited
  • Some children with cow's milk allergy may tolerate it, only under a doctor's supervision
  • A food for strength in tradition

Usual preparation

Drink it pasteurised, a glass a day.

Cautions

  • Raw milk straight from the camel can transmit brucellosis and MERS: drink it only pasteurised
  • Do not drink camel urine as a remedy: it can transmit disease, and the World Health Organization has warned against it
  • People with diabetes should not stop their medicine, and should monitor their sugar if they drink it regularly

Possible drug interactions

  • With diabetes medicines: it may lower blood sugar a little further
