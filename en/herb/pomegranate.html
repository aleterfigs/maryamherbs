Pomegranate and its peel

Punica granatum

In Islamic heritage

Verse of the QuranMentioned in the Quran: “In both of them are fruit, palm trees and pomegranates” [Quran 55:68].

Common uses

  • A nourishing juice
  • Mild diarrhoea (peel decoction, folk use)
  • Mouthwash for the gums

Usual preparation

Fresh juice, or a small piece of dried peel boiled in a cup of water.

Cautions

  • Large amounts of peel and root are toxic: a small piece, for a few days
  • Commercial juice is high in sugar

Possible drug interactions

  • Warfarin (reports)
  • Some blood-pressure and cholesterol medicines

Growing and propagation

Easy in the Gulf
Origin and climate
A tree that tolerates heat and drought
In the Gulf
Does very well in the Gulf, especially in highlands such as Jebel Akhdar
Light, water and soil
Full sun, regular watering while fruiting
Propagation
By hardwood cuttings in winter or saplings
Harvest
The fruit in autumn; the peel is dried in the shade
