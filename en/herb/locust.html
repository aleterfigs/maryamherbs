Locusts

Schistocerca gregaria
See real photos ↗
Other names
الجراد المسلوق · Locust
Part used
Cooked locusts
Strength of evidence
Limited
For children
Not given to children.
Fatigue & recoveryFrom animals

In Islamic heritage

Authentic (sahih) hadithThe Prophet ﷺ said: «Two dead things and two bloods have been made lawful for us: the two dead things are fish and locusts, and the two bloods are the liver and the spleen» (Ibn Majah and Ahmad, graded authentic by al-Albani).

Common uses

  • A source of protein and healthy fats

Usual preparation

Boil in salted water, then dry or roast.

Cautions

  • Serious danger: locust swarms are sprayed with pesticides in control campaigns, and eating them has caused poisoning; never eat locusts of unknown origin
  • People allergic to shrimp and shellfish may react to them

Possible drug interactions

  • No known drug interactions
