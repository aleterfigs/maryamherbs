Qaraz (acacia pods)

Acacia nilotica
See real photos of the plant ↗
Other names
السنط العربي · Acacia pods
Part used
Pods
Strength of evidence
Limited
For children
Not recommended for children except as prescribed by a doctor.
Skin & woundsEasy to grow at home

Common uses

  • An astringent wash for the feet and sweating (external)
  • Superficial wounds (traditional use)

Usual preparation

Boil a handful of crushed pods in a litre of water for 15 minutes and use the lukewarm water as a wash.

Cautions

  • External use only
  • May dry the skin and stain it slightly

Possible drug interactions

  • No interactions for external use
