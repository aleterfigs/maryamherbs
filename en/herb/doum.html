Doum palm fruit

Hyphaene thebaica
See real photos of the plant ↗
Other names
دوم · Doum palm
Part used
Fruit
Strength of evidence
Limited
For children
As a diluted drink from age 4.
Blood pressureFatigue & recovery

Common uses

  • A popular summer drink
  • Drunk as a folk remedy for blood pressure (few human data)

Usual preparation

Soak dried doum pieces in water overnight, then strain.

Cautions

  • It does not replace blood-pressure medicine

Possible drug interactions

  • Blood-pressure medicines (in theory)
