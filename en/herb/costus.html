Costus (Indian costus, qust)

Saussurea costus

Limited

Colds & coughPain & inflammation

In Islamic heritage

Authentic (sahih) hadithThe Prophet ﷺ said: “The best of what you treat yourselves with is cupping and sea costus” (Bukhari and Muslim), and he said: “Use this Indian wood, for it contains seven cures” (Bukhari and Muslim).

Common uses

  • Traditional use for the throat and chest
  • Traditional use for pain

Usual preparation

We have not included a blend with it because safety data are scarce. If you wish to use it, buy it from a licensed source and ask a specialist.

Cautions

  • Daisy family: caution for people allergic to it
  • Avoid in pregnancy, breastfeeding and in children
  • An endangered plant, and it has sometimes been adulterated with other plants harmful to the kidneys; buy from a trusted source

Possible drug interactions

  • Not well known; ask a doctor if you take any long-term medicine
