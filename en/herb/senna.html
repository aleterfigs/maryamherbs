In Islamic heritage

Good (hasan) hadithThe Prophet ﷺ said: “Use senna and sanut, for in them is healing for every disease except death” (Ibn Majah; graded authentic by some scholars). The meaning of sanut is disputed: some said honey, some cumin, some dill.

Common uses

  • Occasional constipation for a short time

Usual preparation

Follow the pack of the ready-made product; best taken at bedtime.

Cautions

  • Do not use for more than a week without asking a doctor
  • Causes cramps, and overuse makes the body lose potassium
  • Not with unexplained abdominal pain or bowel obstruction
  • Avoid in pregnancy, breastfeeding and in children except under medical supervision

Possible drug interactions

  • Diuretics and cortisone (risk of low potassium)
  • Digoxin
  • Warfarin

Growing and propagation

Easy in the Gulf
