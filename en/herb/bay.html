Bay leaf

Laurus nobilis
See real photos of the plant ↗
Other names
الرند · Bay leaf
Part used
Leaves
Strength of evidence
Limited
For children
In food only.
Digestion & bloating

Common uses

  • Indigestion and wind

Usual preparation

2–3 Boil leaves in a cup of water for 5 minutes.

Cautions

  • Remove the leaves from food as they are sharp and indigestible
  • In moderation in pregnancy

Possible drug interactions

  • Sedatives and diabetes medicines (in large amounts)

Growing and propagation
