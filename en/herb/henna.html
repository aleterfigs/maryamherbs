In Islamic heritage

Authentic (sahih) hadithThe Prophet ﷺ said: “The best thing with which you change grey hair is henna and katam” (Abu Dawud, Tirmidhi, Nasa'i and Ibn Majah; graded authentic by Tirmidhi). And no one complained to him ﷺ of pain in the legs without him saying: “Dye them with henna” (Abu Dawud and Tirmidhi; graded good by some scholars).

Extended guide

Pure and adulterated henna
  • Pure: a greenish-brown powder with a grassy smell; its colour on the skin starts orange, then darkens to reddish-brown within a day or two.
  • «Black henna»: an instant black colour within minutes. This usually means it contains PPD, which has caused burns, scars and permanent allergy. Avoid it completely.
  • Ready-made henna cones for designs: some contain chemicals; read the ingredients and buy from a trusted source.
  • Compound» henna: some contains metal salts that react with chemical salon dyes; do not dye your hair with a chemical dye afterwards without asking a specialist.
Allergy test before use
  1. Put a little paste behind the ear or inside the elbow.
  2. Leave it for an hour, then wash it off.
  3. Watch the spot for 48 hours: if redness, itching or swelling appears, do not use it.
Henna and grey hair
  • Henna alone gives white hair an orange or copper colour.
  • For brown or black: apply henna first and rinse, then apply indigo or katam in a second step (see the recipe).
  • Mixing henna with indigo in different ratios gives shades of brown.
Safety
  • Do not apply to babies and young children, or to people with G6PD deficiency (favism), which is common in the Gulf; it may cause breakdown of red blood cells and jaundice.
  • Do not apply to broken or deeply cracked skin.
  • External use only.

Common uses

  • Dyeing, strengthening and adding shine to hair
  • Covering grey hair (with katam or indigo for a darker colour)
  • Decorating hands and feet
  • Cracked feet and cooling them (traditional use)

Usual preparation

Mix the leaf powder into a paste with warm water and leave for hours.

Cautions

  • «Black» henna contains PPD and causes burns and severe allergy: forbidden
  • Do not apply to babies or to people with G6PD deficiency (may cause breakdown of red blood cells)
  • External use only

Possible drug interactions

  • No interactions for external use
