Yerba mate

Ilex paraguariensis
See real photos of the plant ↗
Other names
المتي · Yerba mate
Part used
Leaves
Strength of evidence
Limited
For children
Not recommended for children except as prescribed by a doctor.
Fatigue & recovery

Common uses

  • Alertness and energy

Usual preparation

Fill the cup with mate, pour over water at 70–80 °C and drink through the metal straw.

Cautions

  • High in caffeine: caution in pregnancy, palpitations and high blood pressure
  • Drinking it very hot over long periods has been linked to oesophageal cancer: let it cool a little

Possible drug interactions

  • Stimulants
  • Blood-pressure and heart medicines

Growing and propagation

Not usually grown in the Gulf; bought
Origin and climate
A tree of South American forests
In the Gulf
Not suited to the Gulf
