Coriander (seeds)

Coriandrum sativum
See real photos of the plant ↗
Other names
الكسبرة · Coriander
Part used
Seeds, leaves
Strength of evidence
Limited
For children
As a spice in food only.
Digestion & bloatingEasy to grow at home

Common uses

  • Indigestion and bloating
  • A refreshing summer drink

Usual preparation

One teaspoon of crushed seeds soaked overnight in a cup of water, or boiled for 5 minutes.

Cautions

  • Celery and carrot family: caution for people sensitive to it
  • May lower blood sugar slightly

Possible drug interactions

  • Diabetes medicines (in large amounts)

Growing and propagation

Easy in the Gulf
Origin and climate
An annual from the Mediterranean
In the Gulf
Very easy in winter: sow October–February
Light, water and soil
Sun or light shade, regular watering
Propagation
By seed: crack the seed in half and soak overnight to germinate faster
Harvest
The leaves after 4 weeks; the seeds when they turn yellow
🌡️ Ideal temperaturefrom 12 to 24 °CStruggles above 30°C · harmed below 0°C
🧂 Tolerance of salty waterLowFresh or desalinated water (under about 1,000 ppm). Salty well water burns the leaf tips.
🧪 Soil acidity (pH)from 6.2 to 7Gulf soil is alkaline (7.5–8.5): mix in organic compost and a little agricultural sulphur, or grow it in a pot with ready-made potting mix.
💧 WateringWinter: Every 2 daysSummer: not usually grown in summer
