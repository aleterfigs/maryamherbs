Ambergris

Ambergris
See real photos ↗
Other names
العنبر الخام · Ambergris
Part used
A waxy substance cast up by the sea
Strength of evidence
Limited
For children
Not recommended for children except as prescribed by a doctor.
From the sea

In Islamic heritage

Ibn Sina mentioned it in «The Canon» and Ibn al-Baytar in his «Compendium of Simple Medicines and Foods» as strengthening the heart and brain, and it was among the most precious perfumes of the Arabs.

Common uses

  • Perfume and scent (the main use today)
  • Strengthening the heart and brain (heritage use, no modern evidence)

Usual preparation

Used as perfume. We do not recommend eating it: there is no evidence of benefit, and most of what is sold is fake.

Cautions

  • What is common in markets is fake or synthetic
  • No modern evidence of medicinal benefit

Possible drug interactions

  • No known interactions when used as perfume
