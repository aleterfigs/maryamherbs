Oats

Avena sativa

Common uses

  • Lowering cholesterol
  • Constipation
  • Itchy skin and eczema (oat bath)

Usual preparation

Morning porridge, or a cup of finely ground oats in a lukewarm bath.

Cautions

  • May be contaminated with wheat: people with coeliac disease should choose gluten-free oats

Possible drug interactions

  • May lower blood sugar slightly
