Cod liver oil

Cod liver oil
See real photos ↗
Other names
زيت كبد الحوت، زيت كبد السمك، زيت السمك للأطفال · Cod liver oil
Part used
Oil from fish liver
Strength of evidence
Moderate
For children
Only at the dose on the pack for the child's age, and never combined with other vitamins containing vitamin A or D.
From the seaFatigue & recovery

Common uses

  • A source of vitamins D and A, especially for people who get little sun
  • A source of omega-3

Usual preparation

Only as per the pack: usually one teaspoon daily for adults, and less for children according to the age stated. Do not combine with another supplement containing vitamin A or D.

Cautions

  • Vitamin A builds up in the body: too much causes poisoning (headache, peeling skin, liver and bone damage)
  • Pregnant women must not take it without the doctor's permission: excess vitamin A may cause birth defects
  • Never give a child more than the pack states
  • Forbidden for people allergic to fish

Possible drug interactions

  • Blood thinners
  • Other vitamin A and D supplements
  • Acne medicines derived from vitamin A (isotretinoin)
