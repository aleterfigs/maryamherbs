Mint

Mentha × piperita
See real photos of the plant ↗
Other names
النعنع، النعناع الفلفلي · Peppermint
Part used
Leaves, oil
Strength of evidence
Relatively strong
For children
A weak infusion from age 4. Do not put the oil on the face or chest of young children.
Digestion & bloatingColds & coughPain & inflammationHeadacheNauseaEasy to grow at home

Common uses

  • Irritable bowel symptoms (enteric-coated oil capsules)
  • Bloating and wind
  • Steam inhalation for a blocked nose
  • Diluted on the forehead for tension headache

Usual preparation

Infusion: a small handful of fresh leaves or a spoon of dried leaves in a cup of hot water for 5 minutes.

Cautions

  • May worsen heartburn and reflux
  • Do not put the oil on the faces of babies and young children
  • Essential oil must not be swallowed except in a product made for that

Possible drug interactions

  • Antacids may dissolve coated capsules too early
  • Ciclosporin

Growing and propagation

Easy in the Gulf
Origin and climate
A fast-spreading perennial that likes moisture and a mild climate
In the Gulf
One of the easiest plants: plant October–March; it survives summer in part shade with daily watering
Light, water and soil
Morning sun or part shade, generous watering, rich moist soil
Propagation
By cuttings: put a fresh sprig of shop-bought mint in a glass of water until it roots (a week), then plant it. Or by dividing the roots
Harvest
Cut the stems before flowering; the more you cut, the more it branches. Grow it in a pot, as it takes over the garden
🌡️ Ideal temperaturefrom 15 to 28 °CStruggles above 38°C · harmed below 0°C
🧂 Tolerance of salty waterLowFresh or desalinated water (under about 1,000 ppm). Salty well water burns the leaf tips.
🧪 Soil acidity (pH)from 6 to 7.5Suits the Gulf's alkaline soil.
💧 WateringWinter: Daily or every other daySummer: Daily, maybe twice in the heat
Life cycle
Perennial
Where to grow
A wide 25–30 cm pot
First harvest
6–8 weeks from the cutting
Common pests
Red spider mite, whitefly, root rot from standing water
