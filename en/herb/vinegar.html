Apple cider vinegar

Acetum
See real photos of the plant ↗
Other names
خل التفاح · Apple cider vinegar
Part used
Fermented vinegar
Strength of evidence
Limited
For children
Not recommended for children except as prescribed by a doctor.
Blood sugar & lipidsWeight

Common uses

  • May reduce the rise in blood sugar after a meal
  • Seasoning food

Usual preparation

One tablespoon diluted in a large cup of water with a meal, or on salad.

Cautions

  • Never drink it neat: it burns the oesophagus and erodes tooth enamel
  • Avoid with ulcers and reflux
  • Overuse lowers potassium

Possible drug interactions

  • Diabetes medicines and insulin
  • Diuretics and digoxin

Sources

  • Its page in the natural products database on Drugs.com — In English
