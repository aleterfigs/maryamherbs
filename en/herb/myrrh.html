Myrrh

Commiphora myrrha
See real photos of the plant ↗

Mouth & throatSkin & woundsPostpartum

Common uses

  • Inflamed gums and mouth ulcers (mouthwash)
  • Superficial wounds (traditional use)

Usual preparation

Dissolve a small piece in warm water as a mouthwash, without swallowing.

Cautions

  • Not for pregnancy (may stimulate the womb)
  • Drinking large amounts may harm the heart and kidneys
  • May cause allergic dermatitis

Possible drug interactions

  • Diabetes medicines
  • Blood thinners
