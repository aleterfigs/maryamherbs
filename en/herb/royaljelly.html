Royal jelly

Royal jelly
See real photos ↗
Other names
الغذاء الملكي · Royal jelly
Part used
A secretion of worker bees
Strength of evidence
Limited
For children
Not given to children.
Fatigue & recoveryFrom animals

Common uses

  • Energy and tiredness: few, weak studies
  • Menopause symptoms: small, inconclusive trials

Usual preparation

As directed on the pack, from a licensed product.

Cautions

  • It can cause severe allergy and asthma attacks, and deaths have been recorded; not for anyone with asthma or allergy to bees or pollen
  • Not used in pregnancy or breastfeeding, nor for children

Possible drug interactions

  • It may increase the effect of warfarin (a blood thinner)
