In Islamic heritage

Ibn al-Baytar of Málaga mentioned it in his «Compendium of Simple Medicines and Foods», and violet syrup and oil were among the most famous remedies in Arab and Andalusian medical books.

Common uses

  • Dry cough and an irritated throat (traditionally)
  • Insomnia (violet oil on the head, traditionally)

Usual preparation

One teaspoon of dried flowers in a cup of hot water for 10 minutes.

Cautions

  • The flowers are generally safe, but the root and seeds cause vomiting
  • In moderation in pregnancy
  • Make sure it is a true edible violet, not a similar ornamental plant

Possible drug interactions

  • No important interactions known
