Saw palmetto

Serenoa repens
See real photos of the plant ↗
Other names
البلميط · Saw palmetto
Part used
Fruit
Strength of evidence
Limited
For children
Not recommended for children except as prescribed by a doctor.
Prostate

Common uses

  • Benign prostate enlargement symptoms (conflicting results)

Usual preparation

Used as an extract in capsules as per the leaflet, not as a drink.

Cautions

  • May cause an upset stomach or headache
  • May affect the PSA test result: tell your doctor
  • Not for pregnant women or children

Possible drug interactions

  • Blood thinners
  • Hormonal medicines and birth-control pills

Growing and propagation

Not usually grown in the Gulf; bought
Origin and climate
A short palm from the south-eastern United States
In the Gulf
Not common in the Gulf
Propagation
By seed (very slow)
Harvest
The ripe black fruit
