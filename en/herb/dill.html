Dill

Anethum graveolens
See real photos of the plant ↗
Other names
الشبث · Dill
Part used
Seeds, leaves
Strength of evidence
Limited
For children
As a spice in food only.
Digestion & bloatingBreastfeedingEasy to grow at home

Common uses

  • Wind and indigestion

Usual preparation

One teaspoon of crushed seeds in a cup of boiling water for 10 minutes.

Cautions

  • Celery and carrot family
  • Avoid large amounts of the seeds in pregnancy

Possible drug interactions

  • Lithium
  • Diabetes medicines (in large amounts)
