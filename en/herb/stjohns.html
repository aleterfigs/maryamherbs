St John's wort (hypericum)

Hypericum perforatum
See real photos of the plant ↗
Other names
الهيوفاريقون، العرن · St John's wort
Part used
Aerial parts
Strength of evidence
Moderate
For children
Not recommended for children except as prescribed by a doctor.
Sleep & anxiety

Common uses

  • Mild depression (only under medical supervision)

Usual preparation

Use only under a doctor's supervision.

Cautions

  • It cancels the effect of birth-control pills and many medicines
  • With antidepressants it may cause the dangerous serotonin syndrome
  • Not for use in pregnancy or breastfeeding
  • Increases sensitivity to the sun

Possible drug interactions

  • Birth-control pills
  • Antidepressants
  • Warfarin
  • Digoxin
  • Organ-transplant medicines
  • HIV medicines
  • Some cancer medicines

Growing and propagation

Not usually grown in the Gulf; bought
Origin and climate
A perennial of temperate regions
In the Gulf
Does not suit the Gulf, and we do not recommend using it without a pharmacist
