Marshmallow (khatmi)

Althaea officinalis
See real photos of the plant ↗
Other names
الخطمية · Marshmallow root
Part used
Root
Strength of evidence
Moderate
For children
Cold root infusion from age 4.
Colds & coughMouth & throat

Common uses

  • Dry cough
  • An irritated throat

Usual preparation

Soak one teaspoon of root in cold water for two hours, then strain and warm slightly.

Cautions

  • Generally safe

Possible drug interactions

  • Reduces absorption of medicines: take at least an hour apart
  • Diabetes medicines (in theory)
