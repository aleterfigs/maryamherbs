Other names

Part used
Bark
Strength of evidence
Unsafe
For children

Common uses

  • We do not recommend it

Usual preparation

We do not recommend using it.

Cautions

  • High blood pressure, fast heartbeat and panic attacks
  • Heart attacks and seizures have been recorded
  • Forbidden with heart, blood-pressure and kidney disease and psychiatric illness

Possible drug interactions

  • Blood-pressure and heart medicines
  • Antidepressants
  • Stimulants

Growing and propagation

Not usually grown in the Gulf; bought
Origin and climate
A tree of tropical African forests
In the Gulf
Not cultivated
