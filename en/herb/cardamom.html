Cardamom

Elettaria cardamomum
See real photos of the plant ↗
Other names
الحبهان، الهال · Cardamom
Part used
Seeds
Strength of evidence
Limited
For children
As a spice in food only.
Digestion & bloatingNauseaMouth & throat

Common uses

  • Bloating and a heavy stomach
  • Mild nausea
  • Chewed to freshen the breath

Usual preparation

Crush 2–3 pods and boil in water, or add to tea and coffee.

Cautions

  • Generally safe in food amounts
  • May trigger gallbladder pain in people with gallstones

Possible drug interactions

  • No important interactions known in food amounts

Growing and propagation

Not usually grown in the Gulf; bought
Origin and climate
A tropical plant of India's forests
In the Gulf
Very hard in the Gulf; it needs dense shade and high humidity
Propagation
By dividing the rhizomes
Harvest
The green pods before they fully ripen
