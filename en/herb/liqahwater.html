Palm pollen water (maa al-liqah)

Phoenix dactylifera (spathe distillate)
See real photos of the plant ↗
Other names
ماء الطلع، ماي اللقاح، ماء لقاح النخل · Palm pollen water
Part used
Water distilled from palm spathes
Strength of evidence
Limited
For children
A few drops in water for flavour, over one year only; forbidden for babies.
Digestion & bloating

Common uses

  • A fragrant flavouring for coffee, sweets and water
  • Traditionally drunk in pregnancy, for calm and for colic, without human studies

Usual preparation

A few drops to a teaspoon in a glass of water or coffee, for flavour.

Cautions

  • Never give it to babies, for colic or anything else; a baby takes only milk
  • Buy it bottled from a licensed source; home-distilled water can be contaminated
  • Avoid it if you are allergic to palm pollen

Possible drug interactions

  • No known interactions
