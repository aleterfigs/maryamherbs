Elderberry

Sambucus nigra
See real photos of the plant ↗
Other names
الخمان، السمبوكس · Elderberry
Part used
Cooked berries, flowers
Strength of evidence
Moderate
For children
Not recommended for children except as prescribed by a doctor.
Colds & cough

Common uses

  • Colds and flu at the start

Usual preparation

A ready-made pharmacy syrup is best, or cook the dried berries well.

Cautions

  • The raw berries, leaves and stems are poisonous: always cook
  • Not enough safety data for pregnancy and breastfeeding
  • Caution with autoimmune diseases

Possible drug interactions

  • Immunosuppressants
  • Diuretics
  • Diabetes medicines

Growing and propagation

Not usually grown in the Gulf; bought
Origin and climate
A shrub of temperate Europe
In the Gulf
Not suited to the Gulf
Propagation
By cuttings
Harvest
The ripe black berries, always cooked (raw ones are poisonous)
