Fennel

Foeniculum vulgare
See real photos of the plant ↗
Other names
الشومر، الشمرة، البسباس · Fennel
Part used
Seeds
Strength of evidence
Moderate
For children
Infusion from age 4. For babies only under medical supervision.
Digestion & bloatingWomen's healthBreastfeedingEasy to grow at home

Common uses

  • Wind and bloating
  • Period pain
  • Chewed after meals to freshen the breath

Usual preparation

Infusion: one teaspoon of crushed seeds in a cup of boiling water for 10 minutes.

Cautions

  • Not for babies except on a doctor's advice
  • Mild oestrogen-like effect

Possible drug interactions

  • The antibiotic ciprofloxacin (reduces its absorption)
  • Hormone therapy
