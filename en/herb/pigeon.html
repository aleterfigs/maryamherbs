Pigeon

Columba livia domestica
See real photos ↗
Other names
الحمام المحشي، الزغاليل · Pigeon
Part used
Meat and broth
Strength of evidence
Limited
For children
Well cooked, with the small bones removed.
PostpartumFatigue & recoveryFrom animals

Common uses

  • Traditionally: pigeon broth for women after childbirth and the convalescent
  • A source of protein and iron

Usual preparation

Boil with onion, cardamom and mastic, then drink the broth and eat the meat.

Cautions

  • Buy from licensed farms and cook well; wild pigeons can carry disease
  • Remove small bones for children because of choking risk
  • Pigeon keepers should beware of feather dust and droppings, which can cause lung allergy

Possible drug interactions

  • No important drug interactions
