Common uses

  • There is no scientific evidence of any benefit

Usual preparation

Not recommended.

Cautions

  • The dhub is protected from hunting in the UAE and several Gulf countries; hunting or selling it is an offence
  • Animal fats stored without oversight can become contaminated with bacteria
  • Never put anything in the ear, especially if it is painful or discharging, before a doctor examines it

Possible drug interactions

  • No studies
