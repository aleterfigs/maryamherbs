Flaxseed (linseed)

Linum usitatissimum
See real photos of the plant ↗

Seeds

Common uses

  • Mild chronic constipation
  • Support for cholesterol and blood sugar

Usual preparation

One tablespoon freshly ground in yoghurt or water, with at least one extra cup of water.

Cautions

  • Drink enough water with it, or it may cause a blockage
  • Avoid with bowel obstruction
  • In moderation in pregnancy

Possible drug interactions

  • Reduces absorption of oral medicines: take two hours apart
  • Blood thinners and diabetes medicines
