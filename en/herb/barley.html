In Islamic heritage

Authentic (sahih) hadithAisha (may God be pleased with her) used to recommend talbina, saying: I heard the Messenger of God ﷺ say: “Talbina gives rest to the heart of the sick and takes away some of the grief” (Bukhari).

Common uses

  • Lowering cholesterol (good evidence for barley fibre)
  • A light food for the sick and convalescent
  • Regulating digestion

Usual preparation

Ground and cooked with water or milk (talbina), or the grains are boiled and the water drunk.

Cautions

  • Contains gluten: not for people with coeliac disease

Possible drug interactions

  • May lower blood sugar a little with diabetes medicines
