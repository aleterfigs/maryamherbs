Caraway

Carum carvi
See real photos of the plant ↗
Other names
الكروية · Caraway
Part used
Seeds
Strength of evidence
Moderate
For children
As a spice only.
Digestion & bloating

Common uses

  • Bloating and wind
  • Functional indigestion (with mint)

Usual preparation

One teaspoon of crushed seeds in a cup of boiling water for 10 minutes.

Cautions

  • Celery and carrot family: caution for people sensitive to it

Possible drug interactions

  • Diabetes medicines (in large amounts)

Growing and propagation
