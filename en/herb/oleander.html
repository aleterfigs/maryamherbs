Oleander (difla)

Nerium oleander
See real photos of the plant ↗
Other names
الدفلى · Oleander
Part used
All parts of the plant
Strength of evidence
Unsafe
For children
Not recommended for children except as prescribed by a doctor.

Common uses

  • We do not recommend any use

Usual preparation

Not to be eaten or drunk, and its wood must not be burnt for barbecues.

Cautions

  • A single leaf can be fatal to children
  • Causes irregular heartbeat, vomiting and death
  • If swallowed: emergency department immediately

Possible drug interactions

  • Very dangerous with digoxin and heart medicines

Growing and propagation

Not recommended to grow
Origin and climate
An ornamental shrub common on Gulf streets and in gardens
In the Gulf
All parts are poisonous: Do not grow it where children play or animals graze, and never burn its wood

Sources

  • Its page in the list of toxic plants in the UAE, from the Abu Dhabi Department of Health — In English
  • List of official monographs of the European Medicines Agency (EMA) — search it by the Latin name: Nerium oleander
