Anise

Pimpinella anisum
See real photos of the plant ↗
Other names
الينسون، الحبة الحلوة · Anise
Part used
Seeds
Strength of evidence
Limited
For children
A weak infusion from age 4, for a short time.
Digestion & bloatingColds & coughWomen's healthBreastfeeding

Common uses

  • Bloating and colic
  • Mild cough
  • Period pain (traditional use)

Usual preparation

Lightly crush one teaspoon of seeds and steep in a cup of boiling water for 10 minutes.

Cautions

  • Allergy in people sensitive to celery and carrot
  • Mild oestrogen-like effect: caution with hormone-sensitive tumours

Possible drug interactions

  • Birth-control pills and hormone therapy
  • Tamoxifen

Growing and propagation

Possible with care

An annual from the eastern Mediterranean

Sow directly (it dislikes transplanting)
