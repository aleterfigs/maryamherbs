Cranberry

Vaccinium macrocarpon
See real photos of the plant ↗
Other names
الكرانبري · Cranberry
Part used
Fruit
Strength of evidence
Moderate
For children
Unsweetened juice as food.
Urinary tractWomen's health

Common uses

  • Preventing repeated urinary infections in women

Usual preparation

Unsweetened juice or a pharmacy product daily.

Cautions

  • It does not treat an existing infection: that needs a doctor and an antibiotic
  • May raise the risk of oxalate stones in people with a history of them
  • Sweetened juice is high in sugar

Possible drug interactions

  • Warfarin (reports of increased bleeding)

Growing and propagation

Not usually grown in the Gulf; bought
Origin and climate
A shrub of cold North American bogs
In the Gulf
Not suited to the Gulf
