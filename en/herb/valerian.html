Valerian

Valeriana officinalis
See real photos of the plant ↗
Other names
الناردين، الفاليريان · Valerian
Part used
Root
Strength of evidence
Moderate
For children
Not recommended for children except as prescribed by a doctor.
Sleep & anxiety

Common uses

  • Difficulty falling asleep
  • Mild anxiety

Usual preparation

Infusion of the dried root or standardised products; its smell is strong and unpleasant.

Cautions

  • May cause drowsiness: take care when driving
  • Avoid in pregnancy, breastfeeding and in young children
  • Stop gradually after long use
  • Rare cases of liver injury

Possible drug interactions

  • Sedatives, sleeping pills and benzodiazepines
  • Alcohol
  • Drowsy antihistamines

Growing and propagation
