Rooibos (African red tea)

Aspalathus linearis
See real photos of the plant ↗
Other names
الشاي الأحمر الأفريقي · Rooibos
Part used
Leaves
Strength of evidence
Limited
For children
Caffeine-free and suitable from age one, diluted.
Sleep & anxietyDigestion & bloating

Common uses

  • A caffeine-free alternative to tea
  • A quiet evening drink

Usual preparation

A bag or one teaspoon in boiling water for 5 minutes; milk can be added.

Cautions

  • Generally safe
  • Very rare cases of raised liver enzymes with heavy use

Possible drug interactions

  • No important interactions known

Growing and propagation

Not usually grown in the Gulf; bought
Origin and climate
A shrub of the South African mountains only
In the Gulf
Hardly grown outside its homeland
