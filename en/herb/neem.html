Neem

Azadirachta indica
See real photos of the plant ↗

Leaves, oil

Skin & woundsMouth & throatEasy to grow at home

Common uses

  • Acne and spots (on the skin)
  • Dandruff and head lice (diluted oil)
  • Neem twigs for cleaning teeth

Usual preparation

A paste of ground leaves or diluted oil on the skin. Not to be drunk.

Cautions

  • External use only: neem oil is highly poisonous if swallowed by children
  • Not to be drunk in pregnancy or breastfeeding
  • May irritate sensitive skin

Possible drug interactions

  • When drunk: diabetes medicines and immunosuppressants
