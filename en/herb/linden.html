Linden (lime flower)

Tilia spp.
See real photos of the plant ↗
Other names
التيليو · Linden
Part used
Flowers
Strength of evidence
Limited
For children
Not recommended for children except as prescribed by a doctor.
Sleep & anxietyColds & cough

Common uses

  • Stress and trouble sleeping
  • Colds and mild fever (promotes sweating)
  • Dry cough

Usual preparation

One tablespoon of flowers in a cup of boiling water for 10 minutes.

Cautions

  • Avoid with heart disease unless the doctor agrees
  • Too much may cause drowsiness

Possible drug interactions

  • Lithium
  • Sedatives
  • Diuretics

Growing and propagation

Not usually grown in the Gulf; bought
Origin and climate
A tree of cool temperate regions
In the Gulf
Not suited to the Gulf
Propagation
By seed or grafting
Harvest
The flowers with their bracts in early summer
