Yogurt and laban

Lac fermentatum
See real photos ↗
Other names
الروب، اللبن الرائب، اللبن المخيض، الشنينة، الدوغ، الزبادي · Yogurt, laban
Part used
Fermented milk
Strength of evidence
Moderate
For children
Plain yogurt from 6 months with food, and low-salt buttermilk from one year.
Digestion & bloatingDiarrhoeaFrom animals

Common uses

  • Diarrhoea with antibiotics: the beneficial bacteria may reduce it (moderate evidence)
  • Digestion, and for people with lactose intolerance
  • Cold buttermilk replaces fluids and salts in the heat
  • A source of calcium and protein

Usual preparation

A cup a day; choose plain, unsweetened yogurt.

Cautions

  • Sweetened and flavoured yogurt contains a lot of sugar
  • Buttermilk and aqit contain salt; people with high blood pressure should limit them
  • Keep it cold, and do not eat it if the pot is swollen or it smells off

Possible drug interactions

  • Keep it apart from some antibiotics (doxycycline and ciprofloxacin) by two to four hours
