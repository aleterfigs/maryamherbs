Milk thistle

Silybum marianum
See real photos of the plant ↗
Other names
شوك مريم، الخرفيش · Milk thistle
Part used
Seeds
Strength of evidence
Limited
For children
Not recommended for children except as prescribed by a doctor.
Digestion & bloating

Common uses

  • Indigestion (traditional use)
  • Marketed for liver support (weak evidence)

Usual preparation

A standardised product as per the leaflet; we have not included a blend with it.

Cautions

  • Daisy family: caution for people sensitive to it
  • It does not replace follow-up with a liver specialist

Possible drug interactions

  • Diabetes medicines
  • Some medicines processed by the liver
