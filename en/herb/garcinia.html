Garcinia cambogia

Garcinia gummi-gutta
See real photos of the plant ↗
Other names
الجارسينيا · Garcinia cambogia
Part used
Fruit rind (extract)
Strength of evidence
Unsafe
For children
Not recommended for children except as prescribed by a doctor.
Weight

Common uses

  • We do not recommend it for weight loss

Usual preparation

We do not recommend using it.

Cautions

  • Reports of acute hepatitis and liver failure
  • No proven benefit for weight loss
  • Not for pregnancy or breastfeeding

Possible drug interactions

  • Antidepressants
  • Diabetes medicines
  • Warfarin

Growing and propagation

Not usually grown in the Gulf; bought
Origin and climate
A tropical tree from India

Not grown in the Gulf
