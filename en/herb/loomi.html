Loomi (dried black lime)

Citrus aurantiifolia (dried)
See real photos of the plant ↗
Other names
النومي بصرة، الليمون الأسود · Dried lime
Part used
Dried fruit
Strength of evidence
Limited
For children
In food only.
Digestion & bloatingNauseaEasy to grow at home

Common uses

  • Indigestion and a heavy stomach
  • Mild nausea
  • A warm drink in the cold

Usual preparation

Pierce or crack the lime and boil in two cups of water for 10 minutes.

Cautions

  • Sour: may worsen heartburn and reflux

Possible drug interactions

  • No important interactions known

Growing and propagation

Easy in the Gulf
Origin and climate
The Omani lime tree (small local lime)
In the Gulf
Does well in the Gulf with regular watering
Light, water and soil
Sun, regular watering, well-drained soil
Propagation
By grafted saplings
Harvest
Ripe limes are briefly boiled in salt water, then dried in the sun until black
