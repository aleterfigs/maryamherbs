Extended guide

What studies say
  • Moderate Blood sugar: in a 2023 analysis of human trials, mulberry leaf and its extract reduced the rise in blood sugar after meals. The reason is a substance in it that slows the digestion of starch. Its effect on fasting blood sugar and HbA1c is weaker.
  • Limited From UAE research: the Fujairah Research Centre (2025) studied black and white mulberry growing in Fujairah. It found black mulberry leaves very rich in calcium, and found no lead or cadmium in either. In the laboratory, white mulberry roots stopped Staphylococcus aureus and the fungus Candida. These are laboratory results, not a treatment tested in humans.
Black and white mulberry

Black mulberry (Morus nigra) has dark, sweet-sour fruit, and Levantine mulberry syrup is made from it. White mulberry (Morus alba) has white or purple, sweeter fruit; its leaves feed silkworms, and it is the one used in most blood-sugar studies.

Common uses

  • Mulberry leaf and the rise in blood sugar after meals: human trials
  • Black mulberry syrup for sore throat and mouth (traditional use)
  • A fruit rich in antioxidant pigments

Usual preparation

The fruit is eaten fresh. Mulberry leaf: a teaspoon of dried leaves in a cup of boiling water for 10 minutes, drunk before the main meal.

Cautions

  • Mulberry leaf lowers blood sugar: with diabetes medicines or insulin, drink it only with your doctor's approval, because of the risk of low blood sugar
  • It may cause bloating or mild diarrhoea at first
  • Pregnancy and breastfeeding: the fruit is safe as food, but not the leaf or its extract
  • The sap of the branches and unripe fruit may irritate the stomach

Possible drug interactions

  • Diabetes medicines and insulin

Where to find it

  • Supermarkets and grocers
  • Plant nurseries (seeds or seedlings), to grow it at home

Ask for it by its names: الفرصاد، التوت الشامي، التكي, black mulberry, white mulberry, mûrier, dut, tut. Its scientific name Morus nigra · Morus alba helps with pharmacists and foreign shops.

Choose a sealed pack with a packing date, and whole herbs rather than ground if possible, as ground herbs lose their aroma and are easier to adulterate. Bright colour and a strong aroma are signs of quality. Store in an airtight container away from light and moisture.

For growing: choose freshly packed seeds, or a healthy green seedling without spots or insects. How to grow it is in the growing section above.

Beware of «guaranteed mixes», products without a label or ingredients, and unknown online products; some have been found to contain hidden cortisone or drugs. We do not name shops so that the guide stays neutral.
