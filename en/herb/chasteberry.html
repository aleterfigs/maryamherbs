Chasteberry (vitex)

Vitex agnus-castus
See real photos of the plant ↗
Other names
عشبة العفّة، فلفل الراهب، البنجنكشت · Chasteberry
Part used
Fruit
Strength of evidence
Moderate
For children
Not recommended for children except as prescribed by a doctor.
Women's health

Common uses

  • Premenstrual symptoms and cyclical breast pain

Usual preparation

A standardised product as per the leaflet, after asking the doctor.

Cautions

  • Not for pregnancy or breastfeeding
  • Avoid with hormone-sensitive tumours

Possible drug interactions

  • Birth-control pills and hormone therapy
  • Dopamine medicines and antipsychotics

Growing and propagation
