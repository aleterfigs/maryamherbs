Tea tree oil

Melaleuca alternifolia
See real photos of the plant ↗
Other names
شجرة الشاي · Tea tree oil
Part used
Oil
Strength of evidence
Moderate
For children
Not recommended for children except as prescribed by a doctor.
Skin & wounds

Common uses

  • Mild acne
  • Athlete's foot

Usual preparation

Always dilute (5% or less) in an oil or gel.

Cautions

  • Poisonous if swallowed
  • May cause skin allergy: test first
  • Never apply undiluted

Possible drug interactions

  • No interactions for external use

Growing and propagation

Not usually grown in the Gulf; bought
Origin and climate
A shrub of Australian swamps
In the Gulf
Rare in the Gulf; the oil is bought ready-made
