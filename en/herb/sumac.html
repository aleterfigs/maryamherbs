Sumac

Rhus coriaria
See real photos of the plant ↗
Other names
سماق · Sumac
Part used
Fruit
Strength of evidence
Limited
For children
In food only.
Blood sugar & lipidsMouth & throat

Common uses

  • Possible support for blood sugar and lipids
  • Soothing the throat (folk use)

Usual preparation

Sprinkled on food, or one teaspoon steeped in hot water.

Cautions

  • Rare allergy in people sensitive to cashew and mango
  • Completely different from poison sumac in America

Possible drug interactions

  • Diabetes medicines
