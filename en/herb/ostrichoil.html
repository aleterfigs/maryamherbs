Ostrich oil

Struthio camelus (oil)
See real photos ↗
Other names
دهن النعام · Ostrich oil
Part used
Ostrich fat
Strength of evidence
Limited
For children
Only on a small patch of skin after testing.
Skin & woundsPain & inflammationFrom animals

Common uses

  • A moisturiser for dry skin
  • Joint and muscle pain: not enough evidence

Usual preparation

Apply to the skin, from a licensed product.

Cautions

  • Test it first on a small patch of skin
  • Do not apply to open wounds

Possible drug interactions

  • No known interactions
