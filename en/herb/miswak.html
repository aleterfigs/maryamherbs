Miswak (arak)

Salvadora persica
See real photos of the plant ↗

In Islamic heritage

Good (hasan) hadithThe Prophet ﷺ said: “The miswak purifies the mouth and pleases the Lord” (Nasa'i and Ahmad; mentioned by Bukhari without a chain).

Common uses

  • Cleaning the teeth and freshening the mouth
  • Gum health

Usual preparation

Peel about one centimetre of the tip and chew until the fibres spread like a brush; cut off the used tip every day or two.

Cautions

  • Do not rub hard on the gums
  • Best used with a fluoride toothpaste to prevent decay

Possible drug interactions

  • No known interactions
