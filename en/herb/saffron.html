Saffron

Crocus sativus
See real photos of the plant ↗
Other names
زعفران · Saffron
Part used
Stigmas
Strength of evidence
Moderate
For children
A few threads in food only.
Sleep & anxietySexual health

Common uses

  • Better mood (small trials)
  • Calming evening drinks
  • Flavour and colour in coffee and sweets

Usual preparation

Soak a few threads in a spoon of warm water for 10 minutes, then add to the drink.

Cautions

  • Pregnant women stick to food amounts (large doses may stimulate the womb)
  • Very large amounts (several grams) are toxic
  • Often adulterated: buy from a trusted source

Possible drug interactions

  • Blood-pressure medicines
  • Sedatives
  • Blood thinners

Growing and propagation

Hard in the Gulf
Origin and climate
Corms from Iran and Kashmir that need a cold winter and dry summer
In the Gulf
Hard in the Gulf; it needs cold. Try it in a pot in winter
Light, water and soil
Sun, light watering, well-drained soil
Propagation
By corms in autumn
Harvest
The three red stigmas from each flower in the morning, dried at once
