Zamzam water

Aqua Zamzam
See real photos of the plant ↗
Other names
زمزم · Zamzam water
Part used
Well water
Strength of evidence
Limited
For children
Suitable for all ages in moderate amounts.
Fatigue & recovery

In Islamic heritage

Authentic (sahih) hadithThe Prophet ﷺ said of Zamzam: “It is blessed; it is food that nourishes” (Muslim), and another narration adds: “and a cure for illness”. It is also narrated: “Zamzam water is for whatever it is drunk for” (Ibn Majah and Ahmad; scholars differ on its authenticity, and a number graded it good).

Common uses

  • Drinking it for blessing, with prayer
  • Brought back by pilgrims for their families

Usual preparation

Drink it as it is; it is recommended to pray when drinking it.

Cautions

  • Buy it from official bottled channels; unregulated commercially bottled water may be fake or contaminated
  • Drinking it for blessing does not replace taking the means: a sick person's treatment must not be abandoned for it

Possible drug interactions

  • No known interactions
