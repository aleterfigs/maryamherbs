Coffee husk (Yemeni qishr)

Coffea arabica (qishr)
See real photos of the plant ↗
Other names
القشر، قشر البن · Qishr
Part used
Coffee-cherry husks
Strength of evidence
Limited
For children
Not recommended for children except as prescribed by a doctor.
Fatigue & recoveryDigestion & bloating

Common uses

  • Yemeni hospitality drink
  • A mild stimulant

Usual preparation

Boil one tablespoon of husk with ginger and cinnamon in two cups of water for 10 minutes.

Cautions

  • Contains caffeine: in moderation in pregnancy and with palpitations

Possible drug interactions

  • Stimulants

Growing and propagation

Hard in the Gulf
Origin and climate
The coffee shrub of the Yemeni and Ethiopian highlands
In the Gulf
Needs shade and mild highlands; hard on the Gulf coasts
Light, water and soil
Part shade, regular watering
Propagation
By fresh seed
Harvest
The ripe red cherries; the husk is dried
🌡️ Ideal temperaturefrom 18 to 24 °CStruggles above 30°C · harmed below 10°C
🧂 Tolerance of salty waterLowFresh or desalinated water (under about 1,000 ppm). Salty well water burns the leaf tips.
🧪 Soil acidity (pH)from 5 to 6.5Gulf soil is alkaline (7.5–8.5): mix in organic compost and a little agricultural sulphur, or grow it in a pot with ready-made potting mix.
💧 WateringWinter: Every 3 daysSummer: Daily, in dense shade
