Orange blossom and orange-blossom water

Citrus aurantium (flowers)
See real photos of the plant ↗
Other names
ماء الزهر، زهر النارنج · Orange blossom
Part used
Distilled flowers
Strength of evidence
Limited
For children
A few drops of food-grade orange-blossom water in milk or food from age one.
Sleep & anxietyDigestion & bloating

Common uses

  • A calming evening drink
  • Flavouring for milk and sweets
  • Settling the stomach

Usual preparation

One teaspoon of food-grade orange-blossom water in milk or warm water.

Cautions

  • Use only food-grade orange-blossom water
  • Not to be confused with stimulant bitter-orange peel extract (completely different)

Possible drug interactions

  • No known interactions in food amounts

Growing and propagation

Possible with care
