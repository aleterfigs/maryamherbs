Bitter almond

Prunus dulcis var. amara
See real photos of the plant ↗
Other names
لوز مر · Bitter almond
Part used
Seeds
Strength of evidence
Unsafe
For children
Not recommended for children except as prescribed by a doctor.

Common uses

  • We do not recommend any use

Usual preparation

Not to be eaten.

Cautions

  • A few kernels can cause cyanide poisoning in children
  • Causes dizziness, vomiting, difficulty breathing and can kill
  • If swallowed: emergency department immediately

Possible drug interactions

  • Not to be used at all

Growing and propagation

Not recommended to grow
Origin and climate
The wild almond tree
In the Gulf
We do not recommend it; its kernels contain a substance that turns into cyanide
