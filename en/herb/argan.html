Argan oil

Argania spinosa
See real photos of the plant ↗
Other names
زيت أرجان · Argan oil
Part used
Seed oil
Strength of evidence
Limited
For children
Culinary oil in food, cosmetic oil for the skin.
Skin & woundsHair & scalp

Common uses

  • Moisturising skin, hair and nails
  • A culinary oil rich in fatty acids

Usual preparation

A few drops on hair and skin, or a spoonful on bread and food (culinary oil only).

Cautions

  • Often adulterated: buy from a cooperative or trusted source
  • High in calories

Possible drug interactions

  • No known interactions
