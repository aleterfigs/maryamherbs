Dead Sea salt and mud

Dead Sea salt and mud
See real photos ↗
Other names
ملح البحر الميت، طين البحر الميت · Dead Sea salt and mud
Part used
Mineral salts and mud
Strength of evidence
Moderate
For children
Not recommended for children except as prescribed by a doctor.
From the seaSkin & woundsPain & inflammation

Common uses

  • Psoriasis and eczema (salt baths, moderate studies)
  • Mud packs for osteoarthritis pain (small studies)
  • Softening dry skin

Usual preparation

Bath: one to two cups of salt in a warm bath for 15–20 minutes, then rinse with ordinary water and moisturise.

Cautions

  • External use only; not to be eaten
  • Stings wounds and cracked skin
  • Not for the eyes; rinse at once if it gets in

Possible drug interactions

  • No interactions for external use
