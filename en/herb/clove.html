Clove

Syzygium aromaticum
See real photos of the plant ↗
Other names
عود النوار، المسمار · Clove
Part used
Flower buds, oil
Strength of evidence
Moderate
For children
Not recommended for children except as prescribed by a doctor.
Mouth & throatPain & inflammationDigestion & bloating

Common uses

  • Temporary relief of toothache until you see a dentist
  • Gargle for bad breath
  • Wind and indigestion

Usual preparation

Place a clove next to the painful tooth, or steep 3–4 cloves in a cup of boiling water for 10 minutes.

Cautions

  • The concentrated oil can burn the gums if applied undiluted
  • Clove oil is very dangerous if swallowed by children
  • Stop before surgery

Possible drug interactions

  • Blood thinners and antiplatelet drugs
  • Diabetes medicines

Growing and propagation

Not usually grown in the Gulf; bought
Origin and climate
A tropical tree from the Maluku Islands
In the Gulf
Not grown at home; it needs a humid tropical climate
Propagation
By fresh seed in tropical regions
Harvest
The flower buds before they open, dried until brown
