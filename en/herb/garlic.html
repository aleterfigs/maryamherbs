Garlic

Allium sativum
See real photos of the plant ↗

Common uses

  • Support for blood pressure and cholesterol
  • An everyday addition to food

Usual preparation

Crush and leave for 10 minutes before adding to food to activate its compounds.

Cautions

  • Bad breath and heartburn
  • Stop before surgery (increases bleeding)

Possible drug interactions

  • Blood thinners
  • Some HIV medicines (saquinavir)
  • Blood-pressure medicines

Growing and propagation

Easy in the Gulf
Origin and climate
A temperate bulb crop
In the Gulf

Full sun, moderate watering reduced before harvest, light soil
Propagation
By cloves: a large clove 3 cm deep with the pointed end up
Harvest
When half the leaves turn yellow (after 5–6 months); leave to dry in the shade for two weeks
