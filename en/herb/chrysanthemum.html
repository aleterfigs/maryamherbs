Chinese chrysanthemum

Chrysanthemum morifolium
See real photos of the plant ↗
Other names
زهر الأقحوان · Chrysanthemum
Part used
Flowers
Strength of evidence
Limited
For children
Not recommended for children except as prescribed by a doctor.
HeadacheFatigue & recovery

Common uses

  • Eye strain from screens (traditionally)
  • Mild headache in the heat

Usual preparation

5–6 Dried flowers in a cup of hot water for 5 minutes.

Cautions

  • Daisy family: avoid if allergic to chamomile or chrysanthemum

Possible drug interactions

  • Not well known

Growing and propagation

Possible with care
