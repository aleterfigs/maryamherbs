Pumpkin seeds

Cucurbita pepo
See real photos of the plant ↗
Other names
بذور اليقطين، اللب الأبيض · Pumpkin seeds
Part used
Seeds
Strength of evidence
Limited
For children
The seeds are a healthy food for older children (a choking risk for little ones).
ProstateEasy to grow at home

In Islamic heritage

Authentic (sahih) hadithIt is authentically reported that the Prophet ﷺ loved gourd (squash) and would pick it out in the food (Bukhari and Muslim).

Common uses

  • Easing urinary symptoms of benign prostate enlargement (passing urine at night)
  • A source of zinc, magnesium and protein

Usual preparation

A small handful of raw or unsalted roasted seeds daily, or pharmacy products.

Cautions

  • They do not treat the enlargement itself, only ease the symptoms
  • The prostate must first be checked by a doctor to rule out other causes
  • High in calories

Possible drug interactions

  • No important interactions known in food amounts
