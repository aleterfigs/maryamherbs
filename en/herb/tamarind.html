Tamarind

Tamarindus indica
See real photos of the plant ↗
Other names
العرديب · Tamarind
Part used
Fruit pulp
Strength of evidence
Limited
For children
As food or a diluted, low-sugar drink.
ConstipationDigestion & bloating

Common uses

  • Mild constipation
  • A refreshing drink in the heat

Usual preparation

Soak the pulp in water for several hours, then strain and sweeten lightly.

Cautions

  • Sour: may worsen heartburn
  • The commercial drink is high in sugar

Possible drug interactions

  • Increases absorption of aspirin and some painkillers
  • Diabetes medicines
