Basil

Ocimum basilicum
See real photos of the plant ↗
Other names
الحبق · Basil
Part used
Leaves
Strength of evidence
Limited
For children
In food only.
Digestion & bloatingEasy to grow at home

In Islamic heritage

Authentic (sahih) hadithThe Prophet ﷺ said: “Whoever is offered rayhan should not refuse it, for it is light to carry and sweet-smelling” (Muslim). In Arabic, rayhan covers every fragrant plant.

Common uses

  • Wind and indigestion

Usual preparation

5–6 Leaves in a cup of hot water for 5 minutes.

Cautions

  • In moderation in pregnancy
  • Mint family

Possible drug interactions

  • Blood thinners (in large amounts)

Growing and propagation

Easy in the Gulf
Origin and climate
A tropical annual from India
In the Gulf
Very easy; grows in spring and autumn and even summer with light shade
Light, water and soil
Sun, regular watering, rich soil
Propagation
By seed or cuttings in water
Harvest
Cut the tips before flowering
🌡️ Ideal temperaturefrom 20 to 32 °CStruggles above 40°C · harmed below 10°C
🧂 Tolerance of salty waterModerateTolerates water up to about 2,000–3,000 ppm, with the soil flushed with fresh water now and then.
🧪 Soil acidity (pH)from 6 to 7.5Suits the Gulf's alkaline soil.
💧 WateringWinter: Every 2 daysSummer: Daily
