Chicken and chicken broth

Gallus gallus domesticus
See real photos ↗
Other names
الفراخ، الدجاج البلدي، مرق الدجاج · Chicken
Part used
Meat and broth
Strength of evidence
Limited
For children
Its broth and mashed meat from 6 months with food, without salt.
Colds & coughFatigue & recoveryFrom animals

Common uses

  • Soup for colds and flu: it replaces fluids and soothes the throat
  • Easily digested protein for the convalescent and older people
  • Free-range chicken broth for women after childbirth in tradition

Usual preparation

Simmer with the bones and vegetables for an hour, and drink the broth warm.

Cautions

  • Cook it well so no pink remains at the bone, and wash hands and boards after handling raw chicken
  • People with high blood pressure should cut down on salt and stock cubes
  • Do not leave cooked broth out of the fridge for more than two hours

Possible drug interactions

  • No important drug interactions
