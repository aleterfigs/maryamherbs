Liver

Hepar (ovis, bos, gallus)
See real photos ↗
Other names
الكبد، كبدة الغنم، كبدة الدجاج · Liver
Part used
Lamb, beef or chicken liver
Strength of evidence
Relatively strong
For children
A small, well-cooked portion once a week is enough.
Fatigue & recoveryFrom animals

Common uses

  • Iron-deficiency anaemia: one of the strongest food sources
  • Vitamin B12 deficiency

Usual preparation

Cook well with onions; once a week is usually enough.

Cautions

  • Pregnant women should not eat a lot of it: too much vitamin A can harm the baby, so ask your doctor how much
  • Never eat it raw or pink inside
  • People with gout or high cholesterol should limit it

Possible drug interactions

  • No important drug interactions

Sources
