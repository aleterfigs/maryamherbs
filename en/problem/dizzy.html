Dizziness and vertigo

These results are for a non-pregnant adult without chronic illness or medicines. In the app you choose your age, condition and medicines to see the blends that suit you.

Dizziness has many causes, the commonest in hot countries being dehydration. The recipes here ease the accompanying nausea. Dizziness with weakness, numbness or trouble speaking is an emergency.
Types of dizziness
  • Vertigo: a feeling that the room is spinning, usually from the inner ear. The commonest is benign positional vertigo: short attacks when turning in bed or looking up, treated with a simple head-movement manoeuvre done by a doctor or physiotherapist.
  • Dizziness on standing: a drop in blood pressure when getting up quickly, especially with dehydration, blood pressure drugs and older age.
  • Feeling faint: from dehydration, low blood sugar, anaemia, or sometimes a heart problem.
In the Gulf and Ramadan
  • Dehydration and summer heat are among the commonest causes of dizziness. Drink before you are thirsty, and more when working outdoors.
  • When fasting: dizziness late in the day is usually from dehydration or low blood sugar. Diabetics should check their sugar, and break the fast immediately if it is low.
  • Anyone on blood pressure or diabetes drugs who feels dizzy should see their doctor, as the dose may need adjusting, especially in Ramadan.
What really helps
  • Relatively strong Getting up in stages: sit on the edge of the bed for a minute before standing.
  • Relatively strong When dizzy: sit or lie down immediately to avoid falling.
  • Moderate Enough water and regular meals.
  • Moderate Ginger eases the accompanying nausea and motion sickness.
  • Limited Ginkgo: trials in vertigo are conflicting, and it is dangerous with blood thinners.
Myths about dizziness
  • «Dizziness is always from blood pressure»: it has many causes: the ear, dehydration, blood sugar, anaemia and the heart.
  • «Ear vertigo cannot be treated»: the commonest type is treated with head movements in minutes.
  • «Salt is forbidden for everyone»: in intense heat, people working outdoors need water and salt, unless their doctor has restricted it.

Common home recipes

Morning ginger water

To stimulate digestion and ease mild morning nausea.

Relatively strong evidenceSafe for most peopleModern home recipe
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Ginger (Relatively strong).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Modern home recipe.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
How much and when? One cup once or twice a day; can be continued 4–6 weeks, then a week's break.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For one cup)
  • Ginger: 3 Slices of fresh ginger
  • Juice of a quarter lemon
Method
  1. Steep the ginger in a cup of boiling water for 10 minutes.
  2. Strain and add the lemon.
  3. Drink warm a quarter of an hour before breakfast.
Note

May cause heartburn in people with a sensitive stomach; drink it after a light bite.

🌿 From traditional medicine; most of its recipes have not been tested in large studies. Choose organic or clean local ingredients from a trusted source where you can, and if you do not improve, see a doctor.

Verse of the QuranGinger is mentioned in the Quran: “And they will be given to drink a cup whose mixture is ginger” [Quran 76:17], in the context of the delights of Paradise, not as a medical description.
How much and when? One cup 2–3 times a day until the symptoms go, for a week at most; if no better, see a doctor.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For one cup)
  • Ginger: 4 Slices of fresh ginger
  • Honey: One teaspoon of honey
Method
  1. Steep the ginger in a cup of boiling water for 10 minutes.
  2. Strain and add the honey once lukewarm.
Note

Caution with blood thinners.

See a doctor instead of herbs if:

  • Dizziness with weakness or numbness on one side of the body, trouble speaking, double vision, or inability to walk: it may be a stroke, call an ambulance immediately (998 in the UAE, or your local ambulance number if you are elsewhere)
  • Dizziness with chest pain, severe palpitations or fainting: an emergency
  • Dizziness with a sudden severe headache
  • Vertigo with sudden hearing loss in one ear: see a doctor within a day
  • An older person with repeated dizziness or a fall because of it
