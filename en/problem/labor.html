The last weeks of pregnancy

These results are for a non-pregnant adult without chronic illness or medicines. In the app you choose your age, condition and medicines to see the blends that suit you.

In pregnancy, do not take any herbs or supplements without your doctor's approval. Dates are a safe food, and the evidence that they ease labour is moderate. Daily moderate walking, rest and antenatal care are the foundation.

One suitable blend

Prophetic medicine

Six dates in the last month of pregnancy

Trials found that about 6 dates a day in the last four weeks may ease labour.

Limited evidenceSafe for most peopleVerse of the Quran
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Dates and fresh dates (Limited).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Verse of the Quran.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
Verse of the QuranGod said to Maryam during labour: “Shake the trunk of the palm tree toward you; it will drop on you ripe, fresh dates” [Quran 19:25].
How much and when? 6 dates daily from week 36 until birth.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For one cup)
  • Dates and fresh dates (ajwa): 6 dates (about 60–80 grams)
Method
  1. Eat them spread through the day with water or yoghurt.
  2. Start from about week 36 of pregnancy.
Note

Ask your doctor first; with gestational diabetes, do not start without her approval.

🌿 From traditional medicine; most of its recipes have not been tested in large studies. Choose organic or clean local ingredients from a trusted source where you can, and if you do not improve, see a doctor.

See a doctor instead of herbs if:

  • Reduced baby movements: go to the hospital immediately
  • Bleeding, leaking fluid, or regular contractions before week 37
  • Severe headache, blurred vision, or sudden swelling of the face and hands
  • Fever or persistent abdominal pain
