Diarrhoea

These results are for a non-pregnant adult without chronic illness or medicines. In the app you choose your age, condition and medicines to see the blends that suit you.

7 suitable blends

Common home recipes

Live yoghurt with banana and oats

Good bacteria with the fibre that feeds them, to help the gut recover.

Traditional, no studiesSafe for most peopleModern home recipe
  • Evidence: There are no studies on its ingredients for this problem; it relies on traditional use.
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Modern home recipe.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
How much and when? Once a day during treatment and for two weeks after.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For one cup)
  • A pot of yoghurt with live cultures
  • A mashed banana
  • Oats: Two spoons of oats
Method
  1. Mix the ingredients and leave for 10 minutes for the oats to soften.
  2. Eat daily during the antibiotic and for two weeks after.
Note

Keep it two to three hours apart from the antibiotic dose, especially doxycycline and ciprofloxacin, because milk reduces their absorption.

🌿 From traditional medicine; most of its recipes have not been tested in large studies. Choose organic or clean local ingredients from a trusted source where you can, and if you do not improve, see a doctor.

Arab-Islamic medicine

Psyllium for diarrhoea and constipation

The same fibre regulates stools in both cases.

Relatively strong evidenceSafe for most peopleClassical medical books
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Psyllium (Relatively strong).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Classical medical books.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
How much and when? Once or twice a day with plenty of water; can be continued for long periods.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For one cup)
  • Psyllium (ispaghula): One teaspoon of psyllium husk
  • A full cup of water
Method
  1. Mix the psyllium with the water and stir well.
  2. Drink at once before it sets, then another cup of water.
  3. Once or twice a day.
Note

Never take without enough water, and keep two hours apart from medicines.

🌿 From traditional medicine; most of its recipes have not been tested in large studies. Choose organic or clean local ingredients from a trusted source where you can, and if you do not improve, see a doctor.

India (Ayurveda)

Kanji (Kerala rice gruel)

Kerala's food for convalescence and an upset stomach: rice cooked in plenty of water and eaten with its liquid.

Moderate evidenceSafe for most peopleInherited folk custom
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Ginger (Relatively strong).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Inherited folk custom.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
How much and when? One cup 2–3 times a day until the symptoms go, for a week at most; if no better, see a doctor.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For one cup)
  • A quarter cup of rice
  • 4 cups of water
  • A pinch of salt
  • Ginger: A slice of ginger (optional)
Method
  1. Wash the rice well.
  2. Cook it in the water over low heat for 30–40 minutes until soft, with plenty of liquid left.
  3. Add the salt and eat it warm with its liquid.
Note

With severe diarrhoea, start with oral rehydration solution first, then kanji. For children, leave out the ginger.

🌿 From traditional medicine; most of its recipes have not been tested in large studies. Choose organic or clean local ingredients from a trusted source where you can, and if you do not improve, see a doctor.

Egypt

Guava-leaf infusion

An Egyptian and Asian recipe for mild diarrhoea.

Moderate evidenceSafe for most peopleInherited folk custom
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Guava leaf (Moderate).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Inherited folk custom.

How much and when? One cup 2–3 times a day until the symptoms go, for a week at most; if no better, see a doctor.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For two cups)
  • Guava leaf: 6 Guava leaves
  • Two cups of water
Method
  1. Rinse the leaves and boil them in the water for 10 minutes.
  2. Strain and drink lukewarm, one cup twice a day.
Note

The most important thing in diarrhoea is replacing fluids with oral rehydration solution.

Common home recipes

Rice water

A light starchy liquid to replace fluids and calm the bowel.

Traditional, no studiesSafe for most peopleModern home recipe
  • Evidence: There are no studies on its ingredients for this problem; it relies on traditional use.
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Modern home recipe.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
How much and when? Frequent sips through the day throughout the diarrhoea, with rehydration solution.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For three cups)
  • Half a cup of white rice
  • 4 cups of water
  • A pinch of salt
Method
  1. Boil the rice in the water for 20 minutes until the water turns cloudy.
  2. Strain and add the salt.
  3. Drink lukewarm in sips.
Note

It does not replace oral rehydration solution from the pharmacy, especially for children.

🌿 From traditional medicine; most of its recipes have not been tested in large studies. Choose organic or clean local ingredients from a trusted source where you can, and if you do not improve, see a doctor.

See a doctor instead of herbs if:

  • Blood in the stool or black stools
  • High fever or severe abdominal pain
  • Signs of dehydration: little urine, dizziness, a dry mouth, no tears in a child
  • Diarrhoea for more than two days in children or 3 days in adults
  • Any diarrhoea in a baby under 6 months
