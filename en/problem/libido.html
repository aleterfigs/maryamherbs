Desire and sexual weakness

These results are for a non-pregnant adult without chronic illness or medicines. In the app you choose your age, condition and medicines to see the blends that suit you.

Sexual health is linked to the health of the heart, blood vessels, hormones and mind. Beware «herbal stimulants», «royal honey» and imported stimulant coffee: drug authorities have found that many secretly contain sildenafil or tadalafil, which are very dangerous with heart medicines (nitrates). Sexually transmitted diseases and genital infections are not treated with herbs.

4 suitable blends

The Gulf

Saffron and cardamom milk

A calming Gulf evening drink.

Moderate evidenceSafe for most peopleInherited folk custom
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Saffron (Moderate).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Inherited folk custom.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
How much and when? One cup in the evening an hour before bed; can be drunk daily with a week's break each month.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For one cup)
  • Saffron: 4–5 Soaked saffron threads
  • Cardamom: One crushed cardamom pod
  • A cup of milk
  • Honey: One teaspoon of honey
Method
  1. Warm the milk with the cardamom.
  2. Add the soaked saffron and leave for two minutes.
  3. Sweeten with honey and drink before bed.
Note

Pregnant women stick to food amounts of saffron.

🌿 From traditional medicine; most of its recipes have not been tested in large studies. Choose organic or clean local ingredients from a trusted source where you can, and if you do not improve, see a doctor.

See a doctor instead of herbs if:

  • Erectile weakness may be the first sign of heart disease, diabetes or high blood pressure: get checked
  • Pain, sores or discharge in the genitals: these need a doctor, and herbs do not treat sexually transmitted infections
  • A painful erection lasting more than 4 hours: emergency immediately
  • Low desire with persistent sadness or after childbirth: talk to a professional
