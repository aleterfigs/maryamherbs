Polycystic ovary syndrome (PCOS)

These results are for a non-pregnant adult without chronic illness or medicines. In the app you choose your age, condition and medicines to see the blends that suit you.

PCOS needs follow-up with a doctor. What helps most is weight loss for those who are overweight, activity, and less sugar. The herbs here are a help and their evidence is limited.
What you need to know
  • PCOS is one of the commonest hormonal disorders in women, affecting about 1 in 10, and more in the Gulf. They are not real cysts, but small eggs that did not mature.
  • Signs: irregular periods, acne, excess hair and difficulty getting pregnant. Not all of them need to be present.
  • PCOS does not mean you will not get pregnant. Most women with it conceive, naturally or with simple treatment.
  • It is linked to insulin resistance, so sugar and lipids are checked regularly.
What actually helps
  • Relatively strong Losing 5–10% of weight for those who are overweight can bring back regular periods and ovulation.
  • Relatively strong Movement: 150 minutes of brisk walking a week, plus resistance exercise, improve insulin even without weight loss.
  • Moderate Diet: less sugar, white bread and sweetened drinks, and more vegetables, legumes and protein.
  • Limited Spearmint tea: two small trials in which two cups a day slightly lowered male hormones.
  • Limited Cinnamon: a small trial in which periods became slightly more regular with 1.5 g a day.
  • Inositol supplements have trials for regulating periods; take them under a doctor's supervision.
Myths about PCOS
  • «It only affects overweight women»: it affects slim women too.
  • «A herbal mix dissolves the cysts»: there are no cysts to dissolve, and no herb treats it alone.
  • «Marriage cures PCOS»: marriage has nothing to do with it.
  • «Acne and excess hair are a hygiene or beauty problem»: the cause is hormonal, and a doctor can treat it.

Oat porridge with flax and dates

A breakfast that lowers cholesterol and eases the bowels.

Relatively strong evidenceSafe for most peopleModern home recipe
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Oats (Relatively strong).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Modern home recipe.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
How much and when? As part of food, several times a week.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For one cup)
  • Oats: Half a cup of oats
  • Flaxseed (linseed): One teaspoon of ground flax
  • Dates and fresh dates (ajwa): Two chopped dates
  • A cup of milk or water
Method
  1. Cook the oats in the milk for 5 minutes.
  2. Add the flax and dates.
  3. Serve warm.
Note

For diabetics: one date.

🌿 From traditional medicine; most of its recipes have not been tested in large studies. Choose organic or clean local ingredients from a trusted source where you can, and if you do not improve, see a doctor.

Spearmint tea for PCOS

Two small trials found that two cups a day for a month lowered excess male hormones and slightly reduced unwanted hair.

Traditional, no studiesSafe for most peopleModern home recipe
  • Evidence: There are no studies on its ingredients for this problem; it relies on traditional use.
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Modern home recipe.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
How much and when? One cup once or twice a day; can be continued 4–6 weeks, then a week's break.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For one cup)
  • Mint: 1 tsp dried spearmint, or a handful of fresh leaves
Method
  1. Put the mint in a cup.
  2. Pour over boiling water and cover for 5–10 minutes.
  3. Strain and drink one cup in the morning and one in the evening.
Note

Spearmint is not peppermint. Avoid with severe reflux, and it does not replace follow-up with your doctor.

🌿 From traditional medicine; most of its recipes have not been tested in large studies. Choose organic or clean local ingredients from a trusted source where you can, and if you do not improve, see a doctor.

See a doctor instead of herbs if:

  • No period for more than 3 months: needs a doctor to protect the womb lining
  • Sudden severe pain on one side of the lower abdomen, with vomiting or dizziness: an emergency
  • Excess hair growing quickly, a deepening voice, or new hair loss at the front
  • Thirst and frequent urination, or dark patches on the neck and armpits: get your sugar checked
