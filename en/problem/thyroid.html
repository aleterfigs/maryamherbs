Thyroid

These results are for a non-pregnant adult without chronic illness or medicines. In the app you choose your age, condition and medicines to see the blends that suit you.

If you take thyroid medicine (levothyroxine)
  • Take it on an empty stomach in the morning with water, half an hour to an hour before breakfast and coffee.
  • Keep it four hours apart from calcium, iron, milk, soya and fibre supplements.
  • Biotin supplements (the hair vitamin) distort thyroid test results; stop them two days before the test and tell your doctor.
Food and the thyroid
  • Iodine from iodised salt, fish, eggs and dairy, in moderation.
  • Selenium from fish, eggs and one or two Brazil nuts.
  • Cabbage and broccoli are safe when cooked and eaten in moderation.
  • Avoid iodine supplements and concentrated seaweed without a doctor.
  • Ashwagandha, lemon balm, moringa and garden cress may interact with the thyroid or its medicine.

3 suitable blends

The Maghreb

Sardine salad with lemon and cumin

A small, cheap fish rich in vitamin D, calcium (from its bones), omega-3 and B12.

Moderate evidenceSafe for most peopleInherited folk custom
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Olives and olive oil (Relatively strong).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Inherited folk custom.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
How much and when? Once or twice a week.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For one cup)
  • A tin of sardines in olive oil or water (with bones)
  • Cumin: A pinch of cumin
  • Juice of a lemon, spring onion and parsley
  • Olives and olive oil: A spoon of olive oil
Method
  1. Open the tin and mash the sardines with their soft bones using a fork.
  2. Add the lemon, cumin, onion, parsley and oil.
  3. Eat with bread or salad.
Note

Tinned sardines are salty; people with high blood pressure should choose low-salt.

🌿 From traditional medicine; most of its recipes have not been tested in large studies. Choose organic or clean local ingredients from a trusted source where you can, and if you do not improve, see a doctor.

The Levant

Eggs with spinach and za'atar

A breakfast rich in vitamin D (the yolk), B12, folate and iron.

Moderate evidenceSafe for most peopleInherited folk custom
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Eggs (Moderate).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Inherited folk custom.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
How much and when? Several times a week with breakfast.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For one cup)
  • Eggs: Two eggs
  • A handful of chopped spinach
  • Thyme: ¼ teaspoon of thyme
  • Olives and olive oil: A spoon of olive oil
Method
  1. Fry the spinach in the oil until wilted.
  2. Break the eggs over it and cook until well done.
  3. Sprinkle with za'atar and salt.
Note

Cook eggs well for children and pregnant women.

Two Brazil nuts for selenium

Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For one cup)
  • One to two Brazil nuts
Method
  1. Eat with breakfast or as a snack.
Note

Do not exceed two a day; too much selenium is harmful. Avoid with nut allergy.

See a doctor instead of herbs if:

  • Severe palpitations, tremor or rapid weight loss
  • Severe tiredness with feeling cold, weight gain and constipation
  • A swelling or lump in the neck, or difficulty swallowing
  • In pregnancy: the thyroid must be checked and monitored
