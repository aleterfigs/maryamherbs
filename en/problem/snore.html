Snoring and sleep apnoea

These results are for a non-pregnant adult without chronic illness or medicines. In the app you choose your age, condition and medicines to see the blends that suit you.

No herb treats sleep apnoea. The basics: weight loss, sleeping on your side, and avoiding sleeping pills and smoking. If your family notices your breathing stops, ask for a sleep study.
Ordinary snoring and sleep apnoea
  • Snoring is common, and mostly just disturbs those around you.
  • Obstructive sleep apnoea is more serious: breathing stops many times a night for seconds, so the person wakes up tired. It raises the risk of high blood pressure, heart disease, diabetes and car accidents.
  • Signs: loud, broken snoring, pauses in breathing noticed by a partner, daytime sleepiness and morning headaches. A sleep study diagnoses it, and there is effective treatment with a device that pushes air during sleep.
What really helps
  • Relatively strong Weight loss: one of the most effective ways to reduce snoring and apnoea.
  • Moderate Sleeping on your side: snoring is worse on the back. An old trick: a small ball in a pocket on the back of the pyjama top.
  • Moderate Stopping smoking and shisha, which irritate the throat and nose.
  • Moderate Avoiding sleeping pills, sedatives and heavy meals before bed; they relax the throat muscles.
  • Treating a blocked nose and allergies, rinsing the nose with salt water, and raising the head slightly.
Herbs and snoring
  • No herb treats sleep apnoea.
  • Limited Inhaling mint or eucalyptus gives a feeling of an open nose, without treating the cause.
  • Anti-snoring sprays and herbs sold online have no evidence behind them.
Myths about snoring
  • «Snoring is a sign of deep, restful sleep»: it may be a sign of broken sleep and paused breathing.
  • «Snoring is harmless»: if it comes with pauses in breathing, it can harm the heart and blood pressure.
  • «A sleeping pill improves a snorer's sleep»: it worsens apnoea and makes it more dangerous.
  • «Children do not get it»: persistent snoring in a child may come from the tonsils or adenoids, and affects growth and concentration.

Common home recipes

Saline nasal rinse

For a blocked nose with colds, sinusitis and seasonal allergy.

Moderate evidenceSafe for most peopleModern home recipe
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Salt water (Moderate).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Modern home recipe.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
How much and when? Once or twice a day when needed.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For one cup)
  • Salt water (sea salt): A quarter teaspoon of pure non-iodised salt
  • A pinch of sodium bicarbonate (optional; reduces stinging)
  • A cup of water boiled for 3 minutes then cooled to lukewarm, or distilled water
Method
  1. Boil the water for 3 minutes and let it cool to lukewarm, or use distilled water.
  2. Dissolve the salt and bicarbonate.
  3. Lean over the sink and tilt your head to one side, and pour the solution with a nasal pot or squeeze bottle into the upper nostril so it comes out of the other, breathing through your open mouth.
  4. Repeat on the other side, then blow the nose gently.
  5. Wash and dry the pot, and make a fresh solution every day.
Note

Do not use tap water without boiling it: it rarely carries an organism that causes a fatal brain infection. For children: a ready-made saline spray from the pharmacy is easier and safer.

🌿 From traditional medicine; most of its recipes have not been tested in large studies. Choose organic or clean local ingredients from a trusted source where you can, and if you do not improve, see a doctor.

Common home recipes

Salt-water nasal rinse

One of the most effective ways to relieve nasal allergy symptoms.

Traditional, no studiesSafe for most peopleModern home recipe
  • Evidence: There are no studies on its ingredients for this problem; it relies on traditional use.
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Modern home recipe.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
How much and when? Once or twice a day in the allergy or cold season.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For one cup)
  • A cup of boiled-then-cooled water (or sterile water)
  • A quarter teaspoon of non-iodised salt
  • A pinch of bicarbonate
Method
  1. Dissolve the salt and bicarbonate in the lukewarm water.
  2. Use a nasal rinse bottle or a neti pot.
  3. Rinse each nostril with the head tilted, then blow the nose gently.
Note

Use only boiled or sterile water, never tap water straight from the tap.

See a doctor instead of herbs if:

  • Pauses in breathing during sleep noticed by others, with severe daytime sleepiness: needs a sleep study
  • Drowsiness while driving: a risk of accidents; do not drive until you are checked
  • Morning headaches, or high blood pressure that will not come down despite treatment
  • A child who snores every night with pauses in breathing, bedwetting or poor concentration: it may be the tonsils or adenoids
