Headache

Common home recipes

Diluted peppermint oil for headache

For tension headache, rubbed lightly on the forehead and temples.

Moderate evidenceSafe for most peopleModern home recipe
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Mint (Relatively strong).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Modern home recipe.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
How much and when? When you have a headache; can be repeated after 15 minutes, up to 3 times a day.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For one cup)
  • Mint: Two drops of peppermint essential oil
  • One teaspoon of olive or coconut oil
Method
  1. Mix the essential oil with the carrier oil.
  2. Rub gently on the forehead and temples.
  3. Repeat after 15 minutes if needed.
Note

Keep away from the eyes. Not for young children, and never on a baby's face.

🌿 From traditional medicine; most of its recipes have not been tested in large studies. Choose organic or clean local ingredients from a trusted source where you can, and if you do not improve, see a doctor.

Lavender compress for headache

A cold compress and calming scent for tension headache.

Moderate evidenceSafe for most peopleModern home recipe
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Lavender (Moderate).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Modern home recipe.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
How much and when? Once or twice a day when needed.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For one cup)
  • Lavender: One tablespoon of lavender flowers
  • A cup of boiling water
  • A small towel
Method
  1. Steep the lavender for 10 minutes, then cool the water.
  2. Soak the towel and wring it out.
  3. Place it on the forehead for 15 minutes in a quiet room.
Note

If the headache is severe and sudden, or comes with numbness, go to the emergency department.

🌿 From traditional medicine; most of its recipes have not been tested in large studies. Choose organic or clean local ingredients from a trusted source where you can, and if you do not improve, see a doctor.

Ground ginger at the start of a migraine

In a small trial, ground ginger taken at the start of an attack eased pain and nausea.

Moderate evidenceSafe for most peopleModern home recipe
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Ginger (Relatively strong).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Modern home recipe.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
How much and when? Once at an attack, may be repeated after 4 hours, twice a day at most.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For one cup)
  • Ginger: ⅛ teaspoon ground ginger (about 250 mg)
  • A cup of warm water
Method
  1. Mix the ginger into the warm water.
  2. Drink it at the first signs of an attack.
  3. Lie down in a dark, quiet room.
Note

It does not replace treatment your doctor prescribes. Avoid large amounts with blood thinners. If attacks happen more than 4 times a month, see a doctor about preventive treatment.

Al-Andalus

Violet oil for sleep

In Andalusian medicine it was rubbed on the head and forehead to bring sleep and calm headache.

Limited evidenceSafe for most peopleClassical medical books
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Violet (Limited).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Classical medical books.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
How much and when? A little in the evening before bed.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For a small jar)
  • Violet: A quarter cup of dried violet flowers
  • Olives and olive oil: Half a cup of olive or sweet almond oil
Method
  1. Put the flowers in the oil in a glass jar.
  2. Leave in a warm place for two weeks, shaking daily.
  3. Strain, and massage a little into the forehead and scalp before bed.
Note

For external use. Test on the skin first.

China

Chrysanthemum and goji tea

A Chinese tea for eye strain and headache in the heat.

Limited evidenceNeeds caution for some groupsInherited folk custom
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Chinese chrysanthemum (Limited).
  • Safety: Goji berry: not suitable for some situations (pregnancy, breastfeeding, illnesses or medicines). Use «Suggest a blend» to find out whether it suits you.
  • Source: Inherited folk custom.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
How much and when? One cup 2–3 times a day until the symptoms go, for a week at most; if no better, see a doctor.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For one cup)
  • Chinese chrysanthemum: 5 Chinese chrysanthemum flowers
  • Goji berry (wolfberry): One tablespoon of goji berries
  • A cup of hot water
Method
  1. Steep the ingredients for 5–7 minutes.
  2. Eat the goji berries after drinking.
Note

Avoid with chamomile or chrysanthemum allergy, and with warfarin.

Prophetic medicine

Cupping (hijama): what to know

A well-known Sunnah; small trials suggest benefit for neck and lower-back pain, and what matters most is doing it safely.

Limited evidenceShort-term or supervised useAuthentic (sahih) hadith
  • Evidence: Small clinical trials on neck and lower-back pain, of limited quality.
  • Safety: Cupping is a procedure that needs a licensed therapist: not for long-term use; best under medical supervision.
  • Source: Authentic (sahih) hadith.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
Authentic (sahih) hadith«Healing is in three: a cut of the cupping blade, a drink of honey, or cautery with fire — and I forbid my community from cautery” (Bukhari), and «The best of what you treat yourselves with is cupping and sea costus» (Bukhari and Muslim).
How much and when? At wide intervals, under the therapist's supervision.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For one cup)
  • A therapist licensed by the health authorities
  • Sterile single-use cups and tools
Method
  1. Choose a licensed centre, and make sure the tools are opened in front of you and thrown away after use.
  2. Tell the therapist about your medicines and illnesses, especially blood thinners, diabetes and anaemia.
  3. After the session: keep the small cuts clean, drink water, and rest that day.
Note

Not to be done with blood thinners, anaemia, low platelets, pregnancy, uncontrolled diabetes or weak immunity, nor for children. Do not stop your medicines because of it. The Prophet ﷺ forbade his community from cauterising, so children must never be branded with fire (wasm), as happens in some homes; it causes burns and disfigurement.

See a doctor instead of herbs if:

  • The worst headache of your life, or one that starts suddenly like a thunderclap
  • Headache with fever and a stiff neck
  • Headache with weakness, numbness, or disturbed vision or speech: emergency
