Bad breath and gums

These results are for a non-pregnant adult without chronic illness or medicines. In the app you choose your age, condition and medicines to see the blends that suit you.

Most toothache, bleeding gums and bad breath come from tooth decay or the layer of bacteria on the teeth. The remedies here help, but the basis is the toothbrush, the miswak and the dentist.

Salt-water gargle

For a sore throat and mouth ulcers.

Moderate evidenceSafe for most peopleModern home recipe
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Salt water (Moderate).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Modern home recipe.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
How much and when? 2–3 times a day without swallowing, for a few days until it improves.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For one cup)
  • Salt water (sea salt): A quarter to half a teaspoon of salt
  • A cup of warm (not hot) water
Method
  1. Dissolve the salt in the warm water.
  2. Gargle a small mouthful for 30 seconds, then spit it out.
  3. Repeat 3–4 times a day.
Note

Do not swallow the water. For children aged 6 and over only, when the child can spit without swallowing.

  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Miswak (Moderate).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Good (hasan) hadith.

Good (hasan) hadith«The miswak purifies the mouth and pleases the Lord” (Nasa'i and Ahmad; mentioned by Bukhari without a chain).
How much and when? On waking, before prayers and after eating.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For one cup)
  • Miswak (arak): A fresh arak stick
Method
  1. Peel about one centimetre from the tip of the stick.
  2. Chew it until the fibres spread.
  3. Clean the teeth with a gentle motion, and cut off the used tip every day.
Note

Best together with a brush and fluoride toothpaste to prevent decay.
