Common home recipes

Light ginger water for treatment nausea

According to some trials, ginger alongside anti-sickness medicines may ease chemotherapy nausea.

Relatively strong evidenceSafe for most peopleModern home recipe
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Ginger (Relatively strong).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Modern home recipe.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
How much and when? Sips through the day on nausea days, up to two cups a day.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For one cup)
  • Ginger: Two thin slices of fresh ginger
  • A cup of warm water
  • A slice of lemon (optional)
Method
  1. Steep the ginger in boiling water for 5 minutes, then remove it.
  2. Sip slowly, lukewarm or slightly cool.
Note

Take only with the treating team's approval; avoid if platelets are low or with blood thinners. It does not replace anti-sickness medicines.

🌿 From traditional medicine; most of its recipes have not been tested in large studies. Choose organic or clean local ingredients from a trusted source where you can, and if you do not improve, see a doctor.

Drink for nausea and travel sickness

For mild nausea or before a trip by car or boat.

Relatively strong evidenceSafe for most peopleModern home recipe
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Ginger (Relatively strong).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Modern home recipe.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
How much and when? One cup 2–3 times a day until the symptoms go, for a week at most; if no better, see a doctor.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For one cup)
  • Ginger: 4–5 Thin slices of fresh ginger
  • Cardamom: Two crushed cardamom pods
  • A slice of lemon
Method
  1. Steep the ginger and cardamom in a covered cup of boiling water for 10 minutes.
  2. Strain and add the lemon.
  3. Sip slowly; it can be drunk half an hour before travelling.
Note

Pregnancy nausea should be discussed with the doctor first. Continuous vomiting or signs of dehydration need medical review.

Okayu (rice porridge with ginger)

The food of the sick in Japan, very light on the stomach.

Relatively strong evidenceSafe for most peopleInherited folk custom
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Ginger (Relatively strong).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Inherited folk custom.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
How much and when? As part of food, several times a week.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For one bowl)
  • A quarter cup of rice
  • Two and a half cups of water
  • Ginger: A small slice of ginger
Method
  1. Rinse the rice and cook it with the water and ginger over a very low heat for 40 minutes, stirring now and then.
  2. Remove the ginger and add a little salt.
  3. Serve warm in small amounts.
Note

Suitable after vomiting or diarrhoea, with plenty of fluids.

Al-Andalus

Quince syrup (al-mayba)

Quince was beloved in al-Andalus, and its syrup was given to strengthen the stomach and calm nausea.

Moderate evidenceSafe for most peopleClassical medical books
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Honey (Moderate).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Classical medical books.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
How much and when? One teaspoon to one tablespoon 2–3 times a day, for a few days.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For a small bottle)
  • A large quince, peeled and chopped
  • Two cups of water
  • Honey: 3 tablespoons of honey
  • Cinnamon: A small cinnamon stick
Method
  1. Simmer the quince with the cinnamon in the water for 40 minutes until soft.
  2. Strain the liquid and squeeze the fruit.
  3. Add the honey and return to the heat for 5 minutes.
  4. tablespoon in a cup of water.
Note

Do not grind the quince seeds. Diabetics count the honey.

Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For one cup)
  • Mint: 4 to 5 mint leaves
  • Juice of half a lemon
