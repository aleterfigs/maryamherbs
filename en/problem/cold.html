Saline nasal rinse

For a blocked nose with colds, sinusitis and seasonal allergy.

Moderate evidenceSafe for most peopleModern home recipe
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Salt water (Moderate).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Modern home recipe.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
How much and when? Once or twice a day when needed.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For one cup)
  • Salt water (sea salt): A quarter teaspoon of pure non-iodised salt
  • A pinch of sodium bicarbonate (optional; reduces stinging)
  • A cup of water boiled for 3 minutes then cooled to lukewarm, or distilled water
Method
  1. Boil the water for 3 minutes and let it cool to lukewarm, or use distilled water.
  2. Dissolve the salt and bicarbonate.
  3. Lean over the sink and tilt your head to one side, and pour the solution with a nasal pot or squeeze bottle into the upper nostril so it comes out of the other, breathing through your open mouth.
  4. Repeat on the other side, then blow the nose gently.
  5. Wash and dry the pot, and make a fresh solution every day.
Note

Do not use tap water without boiling it: it rarely carries an organism that causes a fatal brain infection. For children: a ready-made saline spray from the pharmacy is easier and safer.

Warm cold-relief drink

To soothe the throat and feel warm during a cold.

Moderate evidenceSafe for most peopleModern home recipe
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Ginger (Relatively strong).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Modern home recipe.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
How much and when? One cup 2–3 times a day until the symptoms go, for a week at most; if no better, see a doctor.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For one cup)
  • Ginger: 3–4 Slices of fresh ginger
  • Cinnamon: A small cinnamon stick (Ceylon preferred)
  • Juice of half a lemon
  • One teaspoon of honey
Method
  1. Simmer the ginger and cinnamon in one and a half cups of water over a low heat for 10 minutes.
  2. Strain and let it cool a little.
  3. Add the lemon and honey once it is warm, not boiling.
Note

No honey for children under one year. See a doctor if the fever lasts more than 3 days or comes with shortness of breath.

How much and when? One dose before bed, and it can be repeated once in the day, on cough days only.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For one dose)
  • One teaspoon of honey (half a teaspoon for ages 1–5)
  • A few drops of lemon
  • Warm water (optional)
Method
  1. Mix the honey with the lemon, or dissolve it in half a cup of warm water.
  2. Give half an hour before bed.
  3. Clean the mouth or teeth afterwards.
Note

Honey is completely forbidden under one year (risk of botulism). See a doctor if the cough comes with shortness of breath, wheezing or high fever.

Salt-water nasal rinse

One of the most effective ways to relieve nasal allergy symptoms.

How much and when? Once or twice a day in the allergy or cold season.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For one cup)
  • A cup of boiled-then-cooled water (or sterile water)
  • A quarter teaspoon of non-iodised salt
  • A pinch of bicarbonate
Method
  1. Dissolve the salt and bicarbonate in the lukewarm water.
  2. Use a nasal rinse bottle or a neti pot.
  3. Rinse each nostril with the head tilted, then blow the nose gently.
Note

Use only boiled or sterile water, never tap water straight from the tap.

Cooling fever with water

Water to drink and relieving the body's heat, with rest — this is how many scholars understood the hadith, and it is what medical guidelines recommend today.

Authentic (sahih) hadithThe Prophet ﷺ said: “Fever is from the heat of Hell, so cool it with water” (Bukhari and Muslim).
How much and when? Throughout the fever, watching for danger signs.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For one cup)
  • Plenty of water and fluids to drink
  • Light clothing and a moderately cool room
  • A small towel dampened with lukewarm water (optional)
Method
  1. Reduce clothing and covers, and keep the room moderately cool.
  2. Drink plenty of water and fluids in small, frequent amounts.
  3. A lukewarm damp towel can be placed on the forehead for comfort if the person likes it; remove it if they shiver.
  4. If the fever persists or the person is very unwell, see a doctor.
Note

Do not sponge a child's whole body with water, do not use iced water or alcohol, and do not wrap a feverish person in blankets. See a doctor at once for: any fever in a baby under 3 months, a temperature of 39° or more, fever lasting more than 3 days, or fever with a seizure, marked drowsiness or difficulty breathing.
