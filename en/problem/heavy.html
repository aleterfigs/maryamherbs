Heavy periods

These results are for a non-pregnant adult without chronic illness or medicines. In the app you choose your age, condition and medicines to see the blends that suit you.

When is a period heavy?
  • Changing your pad every one to two hours.
  • Clots bigger than a coin.
  • A period lasting more than 7 days.
  • Tiredness, dizziness and paleness.

Heavy periods in teenage girls may be a sign of a blood-clotting disorder or anaemia, so they need a check-up, not herbs.

What helps
  • A blood test at the doctor's (iron and haemoglobin levels).
  • Iron-rich food with vitamin C, and tea and coffee an hour after meals, not with them.
  • A doctor will find the cause of the bleeding and how to treat it.
  • No herb has been shown to stop heavy bleeding safely; do not use «astringents» without a diagnosis.

The Levant

Chicken livers with pomegranate molasses

A Levantine dish among the richest foods in iron, B12 and vitamin A.

Relatively strong evidenceSafe for most peopleInherited folk custom
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Liver (Relatively strong).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Inherited folk custom.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
How much and when? Once a week.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For two people)
  • Liver: A quarter kilo of cleaned chicken livers
  • Garlic and chilli to taste
  • Pomegranate and its peel: One tablespoon of pomegranate molasses
  • Olives and olive oil: A spoon of olive oil
Method
  1. Fry the garlic in the oil, then add the livers and stir over a high heat until fully cooked.
  2. Add the pomegranate molasses, salt and pepper.
  3. Serve with bread and lemon.
Note

Pregnant women should not eat much liver, as it is rich in vitamin A. Cook it well.

🌿 From traditional medicine; most of its recipes have not been tested in large studies. Choose organic or clean local ingredients from a trusted source where you can, and if you do not improve, see a doctor.

See a doctor instead of herbs if:

  • Changing your pad every one to two hours, or needing to change more than once at night
  • Clots bigger than a coin, or a period lasting more than 7 days
  • Dizziness, fainting, severe tiredness or paleness (anaemia)
  • Bleeding from the gums or nose, or bruising easily: it may be a blood-clotting disorder, which is more common in teenage girls than people think
