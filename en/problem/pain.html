Joint and muscle pain

These results are for a non-pregnant adult without chronic illness or medicines. In the app you choose your age, condition and medicines to see the blends that suit you.

Common home recipes

Ginger massage oil

For massaging tired joints and muscles.

Moderate evidenceSafe for most peopleModern home recipe
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Ginger (Relatively strong).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Modern home recipe.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
How much and when? Once or twice a day on the area.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For one cup)
  • Ginger: Two tablespoons of grated ginger
  • Half a cup of olive oil
Method
  1. Warm the oil with the ginger over a very low heat for 15 minutes.
  2. Strain into a clean jar and leave to cool.
  3. Massage into the area twice a day.
Note

Test on a small area first. Not on broken skin.

🌿 From traditional medicine; most of its recipes have not been tested in large studies. Choose organic or clean local ingredients from a trusted source where you can, and if you do not improve, see a doctor.

The red Turkish tea rich in vitamin C.

How much and when? One cup 2–3 times a day until the symptoms go, for a week at most; if no better, see a doctor.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For one cup)
  • Rose hip (kuşburnu): One tablespoon of crushed rose hips
  • One and a half cups of water
  • Honey: One teaspoon of honey
Method
  1. Boil the hips for 10 minutes.
  2. Strain through a very fine sieve or cloth.
  3. Add the honey once lukewarm.
Note

Good straining is essential. No honey under one year.

Sardine salad with lemon and cumin

A small, cheap fish rich in vitamin D, calcium (from its bones), omega-3 and B12.

  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Olives and olive oil (Relatively strong).

  • Source: Inherited folk custom.

How much and when? Once or twice a week.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For one cup)
  • A tin of sardines in olive oil or water (with bones)
  • Cumin: A pinch of cumin
  • Juice of a lemon, spring onion and parsley
  • Olives and olive oil: A spoon of olive oil
Method
  1. Open the tin and mash the sardines with their soft bones using a fork.
  2. Add the lemon, cumin, onion, parsley and oil.
  3. Eat with bread or salad.

India (Ayurveda)

golden milk

A warm evening drink; the turmeric is absorbed better with pepper and fat.

Moderate evidenceNeeds caution for some groupsInherited folk custom
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Turmeric (Moderate).
  • Safety: Turmeric: not suitable for some situations (pregnancy, breastfeeding, illnesses or medicines). Use «Suggest a blend» to find out whether it suits you.
  • Source: Inherited folk custom.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
How much and when? One cup in the evening; can be continued with breaks.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For one cup)
  • Turmeric: One teaspoon of ground turmeric
  • Ginger: A quarter teaspoon of ground ginger
  • Cinnamon: A pinch of cinnamon
  • A pinch of black pepper
  • A cup of milk or plant milk
Method
  1. Warm the milk over a low heat.
  2. Add the spices, stirring for 3–5 minutes without letting it boil hard.
  3. Sweeten with honey if you like and drink warm.
Note

People on blood thinners or with gallstones should ask their doctor before having it often.

Good (hasan) hadithNo one complained to the Messenger of God ﷺ of pain in the legs without him saying: “Dye them with henna” (Abu Dawud and Tirmidhi; graded good by some scholars).
How much and when? Once a week.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For one cup)
  • Henna: 3 tablespoons of pure henna
  • Warm water
Method
  1. Mix the henna and leave for an hour.
  2. Apply to the soles, wrap the feet in a bag and leave for two hours.
  3. Wash off and apply oil.
Note

Black henna is forbidden. People with G6PD deficiency must not use it.

Prophetic medicine

Cupping (hijama): what to know

A well-known Sunnah; small trials suggest benefit for neck and lower-back pain, and what matters most is doing it safely.

Limited evidenceShort-term or supervised useAuthentic (sahih) hadith
  • Evidence: Small clinical trials on neck and lower-back pain, of limited quality.
  • Safety: Cupping is a procedure that needs a licensed therapist: not for long-term use; best under medical supervision.
  • Source: Authentic (sahih) hadith.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
Authentic (sahih) hadith«Healing is in three: a cut of the cupping blade, a drink of honey, or cautery with fire — and I forbid my community from cautery” (Bukhari), and «The best of what you treat yourselves with is cupping and sea costus» (Bukhari and Muslim).
How much and when? At wide intervals, under the therapist's supervision.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For one cup)
  • A therapist licensed by the health authorities
  • Sterile single-use cups and tools
Method
  1. Choose a licensed centre, and make sure the tools are opened in front of you and thrown away after use.
  2. Tell the therapist about your medicines and illnesses, especially blood thinners, diabetes and anaemia.
  3. After the session: keep the small cuts clean, drink water, and rest that day.
Note

Not to be done with blood thinners, anaemia, low platelets, pregnancy, uncontrolled diabetes or weak immunity, nor for children. Do not stop your medicines because of it. The Prophet ﷺ forbade his community from cauterising, so children must never be branded with fire (wasm), as happens in some homes; it causes burns and disfigurement.

Fenugreek poultice for joints

A traditional warm Arab compress for tired joints.

Traditional, no studiesSafe for most peopleInherited folk custom
  • Evidence: There are no studies on its ingredients for this problem; it relies on traditional use.
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Inherited folk custom.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
How much and when? Once or twice a day when needed.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For one cup)
  • Fenugreek: 3 tablespoons of ground fenugreek
  • Hot water
  • A piece of cloth
Method
  1. Mix the fenugreek with hot water into a paste.
  2. Put it on the joint warm (not hot) and wrap with the cloth.
  3. Leave for 20 minutes.
Note

Check its temperature on your hand first.
