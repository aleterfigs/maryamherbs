Mildly raised blood pressure

These results are for a non-pregnant adult without chronic illness or medicines. In the app you choose your age, condition and medicines to see the blends that suit you.

What really lowers blood pressure
  • Relatively strong Follow-up with your doctor and measuring blood pressure at home.
  • Relatively strong Cutting salt to under 5 grams a day.
  • Relatively strong A diet rich in vegetables, fruit and whole grains and low in processed fats.
  • Relatively strong Walking 30 minutes on most days, andLosing weight.
  • Moderate Hibiscus and garlic: a small drop.
  • Treating snoring and sleep apnoea if present.
What raises blood pressure
  • Liquorice and its Ramadan drink.
  • Anti-inflammatory painkillers, and decongestant cold medicines.
  • Stimulants, slimming products and bitter orange.
  • Too much caffeine and energy drinks.

Common home recipes

A salt-reduction plan using spices

Cutting salt is one of the strongest ways to lower blood pressure. Spices and lemon make up for the taste.

Moderate evidenceSafe for most peopleModern home recipe
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Garlic (Moderate).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Modern home recipe.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
How much and when? Daily, as a way of life.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For one cup)
  • Sumac: Sumac
  • Cumin: Cumin
  • Garlic: Garlic
  • Lemon, black pepper and fresh herbs
Method
  1. Remove the salt shaker from the table, and cut salt in cooking gradually over two weeks — your taste adapts.
  2. Use lemon, sumac, garlic and cumin instead of salt.
  3. Cut down on pickles, salty cheeses, tinned foods, crisps and stock cubes.
  4. Read labels: the goal is under 5 grams of salt a day (one teaspoon) from all sources.
Note

Do not use salt substitutes with kidney disease or potassium-sparing BP medicines without asking the doctor.

🌿 From traditional medicine; most of its recipes have not been tested in large studies. Choose organic or clean local ingredients from a trusted source where you can, and if you do not improve, see a doctor.

How much and when? Once a day for 8–12 weeks, then assess the benefit.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For one cup)
  • Black seed (nigella): Half a teaspoon of cold-pressed black-seed oil
  • Honey: One teaspoon of honey
Method
  1. Mix the oil with the honey to soften its sharp taste.
  2. Take after breakfast.
Note

Caution with diabetes and blood-pressure medicines and blood thinners. Do not exceed the amount.

Egypt

Iced hibiscus with mint

A refreshing summer drink instead of sweetened juices.

Moderate evidenceNeeds caution for some groupsInherited folk custom
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Hibiscus (Moderate).
  • Safety: Hibiscus: not suitable for some situations (pregnancy, breastfeeding, illnesses or medicines). Use «Suggest a blend» to find out whether it suits you.
  • Source: Inherited folk custom.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
How much and when? One to two cups a day, checking blood pressure regularly.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For one cup)
  • Hibiscus (karkadeh): A quarter cup of dried hibiscus
  • Mint: A handful of fresh mint leaves
  • A litre of cold water
Method
  1. Steep the hibiscus in the cold water in the fridge for 6–8 hours.
  2. Strain and add the mint.
  3. Serve cold with a little honey if you like.
Note

People on blood-pressure medicines should ask their doctor; blood pressure may drop too much.
