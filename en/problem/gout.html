Gout

These results are for a non-pregnant adult without chronic illness or medicines. In the app you choose your age, condition and medicines to see the blends that suit you.

What helps
  • Relatively strong Follow-up with your doctor for those with repeated attacks.
  • Relatively strong Drinking plenty of water.
  • Moderate Tart cherries, coffee and low-fat dairy.
  • Losing weight gradually, not with crash diets.
What makes gout worse
  • Liver, kidneys and red meat.
  • Seafood, especially sardines, anchovies and prawns.
  • Sweetened drinks and sweetened fruit juices (fructose).
  • Long fasting and dehydration.
  • Choose «Gout» under «For more accurate results» so the helper rules out recipes rich in purines.

Lemon water to prevent stones

Lemon juice is rich in citrate, which prevents some kinds of stones from forming; studies support it.

Moderate evidenceSafe for most peopleModern home recipe
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Mint (Relatively strong).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Modern home recipe.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
How much and when? Daily, through the day.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For two litres)
  • Half a cup of fresh lemon juice
  • Two litres of water
  • Mint: 4 to 5 mint leaves (optional)
Method
  1. Mix the lemon juice with the water in a large bottle.
  2. Drink it through the day, along with the rest of your water.
Note

Drink through a straw and rinse your mouth to protect the teeth. People with reflux may reduce the amount.

Tart cherry juice for gout

Studies suggest tart cherries may reduce gout attacks.

Traditional, no studiesSafe for most peopleInherited folk custom
  • Evidence: There are no studies on its ingredients for this problem; it relies on traditional use.
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Inherited folk custom.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
How much and when? One cup a day.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For one cup)
  • Half a cup of unsweetened tart cherry juice, or a handful of fresh cherries
  • Half a cup of water
Method
  1. Dilute the juice with water.
  2. Drink daily.
Note

Choose unsweetened juice; sugar and fructose worsen gout. It does not replace gout medicines.

Mint and lemon infused water

A refreshing calorie-free alternative to juices, made in a jug for the fridge.

Moderate evidenceSafe for most peopleModern home recipe
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Mint (Relatively strong).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Modern home recipe.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
How much and when? Through the day instead of juices and fizzy drinks.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For one litre)
  • Mint: A handful of mint
  • A lemon, sliced
  • A cucumber, sliced
  • A litre of cold water
Method
  1. Put the ingredients in the water jug.
  2. Leave in the fridge for two hours.
  3. Drink through the day.
Note

Safe for everyone.
