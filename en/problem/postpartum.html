Postpartum and recovery after birth

These results are for a non-pregnant adult without chronic illness or medicines. In the app you choose your age, condition and medicines to see the blends that suit you.

What matters most after birth: rest, fluids, food rich in iron and protein, and emotional support from family. If you are breastfeeding, choose «Breastfeeding» at the age question so the helper rules out anything unsuitable while nursing. Do not put herbs or oils inside the vagina.

Meghli (caraway pudding for new mothers)

The Levantine caraway pudding served to new mothers and their visitors.

Moderate evidenceSafe for most peopleInherited folk custom
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Caraway (Moderate).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Inherited folk custom.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
How much and when? As part of food, several times a week.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For six bowls)
  • Caraway: One tablespoon of ground caraway
  • Cinnamon: One teaspoon of cinnamon
  • Half a cup of rice flour
  • 4 cups of water and a little sugar
  • Nuts and coconut to decorate
Method
  1. Mix the rice flour, caraway and cinnamon with the cold water.
  2. Cook, stirring, until it thickens (20 minutes), and add the sugar.
  3. Pour into bowls and decorate with nuts.
Note

A sweet high in sugar.

Homemade bone broth

A warm broth of bones and vegetables, drunk in cold weather, after childbirth and after illness.

Limited evidenceSafe for most peopleInherited folk custom
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Bone and trotter broth (Limited).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Inherited folk custom.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
How much and when? A warm cup daily when needed.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For one cup)
  • Bone and trotter broth: A kilo of meat or chicken bones with the joints
  • An onion, a carrot and a celery stick
  • A spoonful of vinegar
  • Water to cover the bones, and a little salt
Method
  1. Wash the bones, cover them with water and the vinegar, and leave for half an hour.
  2. Add the vegetables, bring to the boil, then simmer for 3–6 hours, skimming off the foam.
  3. Strain it, cool it, and lift off the layer of fat from the top.
Note

Keeps 3 days in the fridge or can be frozen. People with high blood pressure should use less salt, and people with gout should limit it.
