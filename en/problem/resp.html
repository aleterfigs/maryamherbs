Asthma and breathing conditions

These results are for a non-pregnant adult without chronic illness or medicines. In the app you choose your age, condition and medicines to see the blends that suit you.

The basic rule
  • Asthma needs a doctor's follow-up, and herbs do not take the place of the treatment they prescribe. Do not stop your treatment without asking them.
Common irritants in our homes
  • Incense, oud and burners: among the best-known asthma triggers in the Gulf; reduce them and ventilate the room.
  • Smoke and shisha, strong perfumes, air fresheners and essential-oil diffusers.
  • Dust and sandstorms: close the windows and wear a mask.
  • Cold air straight from the air-conditioner, mould, and dust mites in bedding.
What else helps
  • Honey to soothe cough.
  • Pursed-lip breathing exercise for COPD patients.
  • Hot steam may irritate asthma in some people; avoid it during attacks.

Common home recipes

Saline nasal rinse

For a blocked nose with colds, sinusitis and seasonal allergy.

Moderate evidenceSafe for most peopleModern home recipe
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Salt water (Moderate).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Modern home recipe.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
How much and when? Once or twice a day when needed.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For one cup)
  • Salt water (sea salt): A quarter teaspoon of pure non-iodised salt
  • A pinch of sodium bicarbonate (optional; reduces stinging)
  • A cup of water boiled for 3 minutes then cooled to lukewarm, or distilled water
Method
  1. Boil the water for 3 minutes and let it cool to lukewarm, or use distilled water.
  2. Dissolve the salt and bicarbonate.
  3. Lean over the sink and tilt your head to one side, and pour the solution with a nasal pot or squeeze bottle into the upper nostril so it comes out of the other, breathing through your open mouth.
  4. Repeat on the other side, then blow the nose gently.
  5. Wash and dry the pot, and make a fresh solution every day.
Note

Do not use tap water without boiling it: it rarely carries an organism that causes a fatal brain infection. For children: a ready-made saline spray from the pharmacy is easier and safer.

🌿 From traditional medicine; most of its recipes have not been tested in large studies. Choose organic or clean local ingredients from a trusted source where you can, and if you do not improve, see a doctor.

Pursed-lip breathing

An exercise that eases breathlessness in chronic asthma and COPD, taught by respiratory therapists.

Traditional, no studiesSafe for most peopleModern home recipe
  • Evidence: There are no studies on its ingredients for this problem; it relies on traditional use.
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Modern home recipe.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
How much and when? Several times a day, and when breathless.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For one cup)
  • A comfortable chair
  • 5 minutes
Method
  1. Sit with relaxed shoulders.
  2. Breathe in slowly through your nose, counting to two.
  3. Purse your lips as if whistling and breathe out slowly, counting to four.
  4. Repeat for 5 minutes, and use it during exertion.
Note

It does not replace the asthma treatment your doctor prescribed. If breathlessness gets worse, go to the emergency department.

Thyme tea with honey

For cough, phlegm and colds.

Moderate evidenceSafe for most peopleModern home recipe
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Thyme (Moderate).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Modern home recipe.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
How much and when? One cup 2–3 times a day until the symptoms go, for a week at most; if no better, see a doctor.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For one cup)
  • Thyme: One teaspoon of thyme
  • One teaspoon of honey
Method
  1. Steep the thyme in a cup of boiling water for 10 minutes.
  2. Strain and add the honey once lukewarm.
  3. Up to 3 cups a day.
Note

No honey under one year.

Cold marshmallow infusion for cough

Marshmallow is one of Arab medicine's remedies for the chest.

Moderate evidenceSafe for most peopleClassical medical books
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Marshmallow (Moderate).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Classical medical books.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
How much and when? One cup 2–3 times a day until the symptoms go, for a week at most; if no better, see a doctor.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For one cup)
  • Marshmallow (khatmi): One teaspoon of dried marshmallow root
  • A cup of cold water
  • Honey: One teaspoon of honey
Method
  1. Soak the root in cold water for two hours, stirring now and then.
  2. Strain and warm slightly.
  3. Add the honey and drink in sips.
Note

Keep an hour apart from medicines.

Honey and lemon for cough

To soothe night-time cough in children aged one and over and in adults. Honey has relatively good evidence here.

Traditional, no studiesSafe for most peopleModern home recipe
  • Evidence: There are no studies on its ingredients for this problem; it relies on traditional use.
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Modern home recipe.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
How much and when? One dose before bed, and it can be repeated once in the day, on cough days only.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For one dose)
  • One teaspoon of honey (half a teaspoon for ages 1–5)
  • A few drops of lemon
  • Warm water (optional)
Method
  1. Mix the honey with the lemon, or dissolve it in half a cup of warm water.
  2. Give half an hour before bed.
  3. Clean the mouth or teeth afterwards.
Note

Honey is completely forbidden under one year (risk of botulism). See a doctor if the cough comes with shortness of breath, wheezing or high fever.

Salt-water nasal rinse

One of the most effective ways to relieve nasal allergy symptoms.

Traditional, no studiesSafe for most peopleModern home recipe
  • Evidence: There are no studies on its ingredients for this problem; it relies on traditional use.
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Modern home recipe.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
How much and when? Once or twice a day in the allergy or cold season.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For one cup)
  • A cup of boiled-then-cooled water (or sterile water)
  • A quarter teaspoon of non-iodised salt
  • A pinch of bicarbonate
Method
  1. Dissolve the salt and bicarbonate in the lukewarm water.
  2. Use a nasal rinse bottle or a neti pot.
  3. Rinse each nostril with the head tilted, then blow the nose gently.
Note

Use only boiled or sterile water, never tap water straight from the tap.
