Irregular periods

These results are for a non-pregnant adult without chronic illness or medicines. In the app you choose your age, condition and medicines to see the blends that suit you.

When are irregular periods normal?
  • In the first two to three years after the first period.
  • With severe stress, weight change, travel or intense exercise.
  • Before menopause.
What helps
  • A healthy weight, a balanced diet and regular sleep.
  • Recording your periods to learn their pattern.
  • Limited Cinnamon: small trials in women with polycystic ovaries.
  • Limited Marjoram: traditional use; forbidden if pregnancy is possible.
  • Polycystic ovaries and thyroid problems are common causes, and diagnosing them matters.

Marjoram infusion

Traditional use for period cramps.

Limited evidenceNeeds caution for some groupsInherited folk custom
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Marjoram (Limited).
  • Safety: Marjoram: not suitable for some situations (pregnancy, breastfeeding, illnesses or medicines). Use «Suggest a blend» to find out whether it suits you.
  • Source: Inherited folk custom.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
How much and when? One cup twice a day on painful days.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For one cup)
  • Marjoram: One teaspoon of marjoram
  • A cup of boiling water
Method
  1. Steep the marjoram for 7 minutes.
  2. Strain.
  3. One cup a day on period days.
Note

Forbidden in pregnancy or if pregnancy is suspected.

How much and when? One cup twice a day on the first 3 days of the period.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For one cup)
  • Cinnamon: A Ceylon cinnamon stick
  • One and a half cups of water
  • Honey: One teaspoon of honey
Method
  1. Boil the cinnamon for 10 minutes.
  2. Strain and add the honey.
Note

Ceylon cinnamon is preferred.

Spearmint tea for PCOS

Two small trials found that two cups a day for a month lowered excess male hormones and slightly reduced unwanted hair.

Traditional, no studiesSafe for most peopleModern home recipe
  • Evidence: There are no studies on its ingredients for this problem; it relies on traditional use.
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Modern home recipe.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
How much and when? One cup once or twice a day; can be continued 4–6 weeks, then a week's break.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For one cup)
  • Mint: 1 tsp dried spearmint, or a handful of fresh leaves
Method
  1. Put the mint in a cup.
  2. Pour over boiling water and cover for 5–10 minutes.
  3. Strain and drink one cup in the morning and one in the evening.
Note

Spearmint is not peppermint. Avoid with severe reflux, and it does not replace follow-up with your doctor.

See a doctor instead of herbs if:

  • No first period by age 15
  • No period for 3 months or more (with possible pregnancy, weight loss or intense exercise)
  • Two to three years after the first period: cycles shorter than 21 days or longer than 45 days
  • Irregular periods with severe acne, excess hair or weight gain (it may be polycystic ovaries)
