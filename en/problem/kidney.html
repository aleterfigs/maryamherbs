Kidney health and kidney patients

These results are for a non-pregnant adult without chronic illness or medicines. In the app you choose your age, condition and medicines to see the blends that suit you.

Kidney patients are among those most harmed by herbs. Choose «Kidney disease» under «For more accurate results» so the helper rules out everything that harms the kidneys from all the recipes.
The basic rule for kidney patients
  • No herb, supplement or «cleansing tea» without the kidney doctor's approval. Diseased kidneys do not clear herbal substances well, and some herbs have caused kidney failure.
  • The diet for a kidney patient differs by stage and test results, and is set by a dietitian.
  • Even the amount of water may be restricted in advanced stages; do not drink extra without guidance.
Common things that harm the kidneys
  • Anti-inflammatory painkillers such as ibuprofen and diclofenac, especially with repeated use.
  • Star fruit (carambola): toxic to kidney patients.
  • Salt substitutes: usually potassium chloride, which can raise potassium to a level dangerous for the heart.
  • Coconut water: very rich in potassium.
  • Liquorice وDiuretic herbs and «kidney cleanse blends».
  • Chinese herbs or Indian costus from an unknown source: adulteration with plants containing aristolochic acid, which destroys the kidneys, has been documented.
  • Dark fizzy drinks and processed meats and cheeses: rich in phosphorus.
  • High-dose protein and creatine supplements.
Foods to ask the kidney doctor about

You may need to reduce them if your potassium is high in your tests:

  • Dates, bananas and dried fruit.
  • Potatoes and tomatoes.
  • Oranges and their juice, pomegranate and baobab.
  • Pulses and nuts.
What protects the kidneys
  • Controlling blood pressure and blood sugar, the two most common causes of kidney disease.
  • Cutting salt.
  • Not overusing painkillers.
  • Drinking water regularly, for people with healthy kidneys.
  • A yearly kidney check for people with diabetes and high blood pressure.

A salt-reduction plan using spices

Cutting salt is one of the strongest ways to lower blood pressure. Spices and lemon make up for the taste.

Moderate evidenceSafe for most peopleModern home recipe
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Garlic (Moderate).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Modern home recipe.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
How much and when? Daily, as a way of life.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For one cup)
  • Sumac: Sumac
  • Cumin: Cumin
  • Garlic: Garlic
  • Lemon, black pepper and fresh herbs
Method
  1. Remove the salt shaker from the table, and cut salt in cooking gradually over two weeks — your taste adapts.
  2. Use lemon, sumac, garlic and cumin instead of salt.
  3. Cut down on pickles, salty cheeses, tinned foods, crisps and stock cubes.
  4. Read labels: the goal is under 5 grams of salt a day (one teaspoon) from all sources.
Note

Do not use salt substitutes with kidney disease or potassium-sparing BP medicines without asking the doctor.

🌿 From traditional medicine; most of its recipes have not been tested in large studies. Choose organic or clean local ingredients from a trusted source where you can, and if you do not improve, see a doctor.

Soaking and boiling vegetables to reduce potassium

A method taught by dietitians to kidney patients on a potassium restriction.

Traditional, no studiesSafe for most peopleModern home recipe
  • Evidence: There are no studies on its ingredients for this problem; it relies on traditional use.
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Modern home recipe.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
How much and when? According to the dietitian's plan.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For one cup)
  • Potatoes, carrots or leafy greens
  • Plenty of warm water
Method
  1. Peel the vegetables and cut them into thin slices or small cubes.
  2. Soak them in ten times their volume of warm water for at least two hours, then discard the water.
  3. Boil them in plenty of fresh water, then discard the cooking water and do not use it for cooking.
Note

Follow only if your kidney doctor or dietitian advised you to reduce potassium.

Cucumber, cabbage and pepper salad

Relatively low-potassium vegetables that suit many kidney patients.

Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For one dish)
  • A cucumber, a cup of shredded cabbage and half a red pepper
  • Olives and olive oil: A spoon of olive oil
  • Lemon juice, with no salt or salt substitute
  • Sumac: A pinch of sumac
Method
  1. Wash and cut up the vegetables.
  2. Dress with the oil, lemon and sumac, without salt.
Note

Do not use salt substitute: it is usually potassium chloride, which is dangerous for kidney patients.

See a doctor instead of herbs if:

  • Swelling in the legs, face or around the eyes
  • Foamy urine, very little urine, or a change in its colour
  • Severe itching, nausea, shortness of breath or confusion
  • Diabetes or blood-pressure patients: have your kidneys checked (creatinine and urine protein) at least once a year, because kidney disease is usually silent
