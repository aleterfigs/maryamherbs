Premenstrual symptoms

These results are for a non-pregnant adult without chronic illness or medicines. In the app you choose your age, condition and medicines to see the blends that suit you.

What really helps
  • Moderate Calcium from milk, yoghurt and sesame.
  • Moderate Regular exercise.
  • Cutting salt, sugar and caffeine in the two weeks before the period.
  • Enough sleep and less stress.
  • Moderate Chasteberry: approved in Europe for adult women, but it is hormonal, so not for teenage girls and not without advice.

Stretches for period pain

Gentle movements that relax the abdominal and pelvic muscles; regular exercise reduces pain over time.

Traditional, no studiesSafe for most peopleModern home recipe
  • Evidence: There are no studies on its ingredients for this problem; it relies on traditional use.
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Modern home recipe.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
How much and when? Daily, and twice a day on painful days.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For one cup)
  • A mat or rug
  • 10 minutes
Method
  1. Child's pose: kneel, stretch your arms in front with your forehead on the floor, for a minute.
  2. Cat and cow: on hands and knees, arch your back up then down slowly, 10 times.
  3. Lie on your back and hug your knees to your chest, for a minute.
  4. Walk 20–30 minutes on most days of the month.
Note

Avoid strenuous exercise if the pain increases.

🌿 From traditional medicine; most of its recipes have not been tested in large studies. Choose organic or clean local ingredients from a trusted source where you can, and if you do not improve, see a doctor.

Fennel tea for periods

For period cramps and the bloating that comes with them.

Moderate evidenceSafe for most peopleModern home recipe
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Fennel (Moderate).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Modern home recipe.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
How much and when? One cup twice a day on painful days.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For one cup)
  • Fennel: One teaspoon of crushed fennel seeds
  • A cup of boiling water
Method
  1. Steep the fennel for 10 minutes.
  2. Strain.
  3. Drink twice a day, two days before the period and on its first days.
Note

In moderation with birth-control pills and hormone therapy.

Calcium yoghurt with dates and sesame

According to studies, calcium is one of the things that most eases premenstrual symptoms.

Limited evidenceSafe for most peopleModern home recipe
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Dates and fresh dates (Limited).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Modern home recipe.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
How much and when? Daily, especially in the two weeks before the period.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For one cup)
  • A pot of yoghurt or labneh
  • Dates and fresh dates (ajwa): Two chopped dates
  • One teaspoon of toasted sesame
Method
  1. Mix the ingredients.
  2. Eat as a snack.
Note

Avoid with sesame or milk allergy.

Chamomile and mint for period bloating

Combines easing cramps with relieving bloating.

Traditional, no studiesSafe for most peopleModern home recipe
  • Evidence: There are no studies on its ingredients for this problem; it relies on traditional use.
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Modern home recipe.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
How much and when? One cup twice a day on painful days.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For one cup)
  • Chamomile: One teaspoon of chamomile
  • Mint: 5 to 6 fresh mint leaves, or ½ teaspoon of dried mint
Method
  1. Steep them in a cup of boiling water for 8 minutes.
  2. Strain and drink warm.
Note

Safe for teenagers in moderate amounts.
