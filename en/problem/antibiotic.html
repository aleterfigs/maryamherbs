After antibiotics

These results are for a non-pregnant adult without chronic illness or medicines. In the app you choose your age, condition and medicines to see the blends that suit you.

First of all
  • Finish the antibiotic as the doctor prescribed and do not stop when you feel better.
  • Antibiotics do not treat colds and flu, because they are viruses.
  • Never take an antibiotic without a prescription, and never give leftovers to others.
Yoghurt and antibiotics: watch the timing

Milk, yoghurt, cheese, calcium, iron, zinc and antacids reduce the absorption of some antibiotics when taken with them, including:

  • Doxycycline and tetracycline.
  • Ciprofloxacin and levofloxacin.

Keep them at least two to three hours apart from the dose, and ask the pharmacist about your medicine.

Foods that help the gut recover
  • Fermented foods: yoghurt with live cultures, kefir, laban, labneh and salt-fermented pickles.
  • Foods that feed good bacteria: oats, barley, bananas, onions, garlic, pulses and dates.
  • Fluids: water, and rehydration solution with diarrhoea.
  • For women: If itching and white discharge appear after the antibiotic, it is usually a yeast infection; see a doctor. Do not put garlic or yoghurt inside the vagina.

Live yoghurt with banana and oats

Good bacteria with the fibre that feeds them, to help the gut recover.

Traditional, no studiesSafe for most peopleModern home recipe
  • Evidence: There are no studies on its ingredients for this problem; it relies on traditional use.
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Modern home recipe.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
How much and when? Once a day during treatment and for two weeks after.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For one cup)
  • A pot of yoghurt with live cultures
  • A mashed banana
  • Oats: Two spoons of oats
Method
  1. Mix the ingredients and leave for 10 minutes for the oats to soften.
  2. Eat daily during the antibiotic and for two weeks after.
Note

Keep it two to three hours apart from the antibiotic dose, especially doxycycline and ciprofloxacin, because milk reduces their absorption.

The Levant

Salt-fermented pickles (no vinegar)

Pickles naturally fermented in salt water contain good bacteria; vinegar pickles do not.

Limited evidenceSafe for most peopleInherited folk custom
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Dill (Limited).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Inherited folk custom.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
How much and when? Small pieces with meals.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For one jar)
  • Small cucumbers or sliced turnips
  • A litre of boiled then cooled water
  • Two tablespoons of non-iodised salt
  • Garlic: garlic cloves
  • Dill: Dill sprigs
Method
  1. Layer the vegetables, garlic and dill in a clean glass jar.
  2. Dissolve the salt in the water and pour it in to cover them completely.
  3. Close and wait 5–7 days at room temperature, then keep in the fridge.
Note

High in salt: people with high blood pressure or kidney disease should eat little. Throw away if mould or a bad smell appears.

Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For three cups)
  • Half a cup of white rice
  • 4 cups of water
  • A pinch of salt
Method
  1. Boil the rice in the water for 20 minutes until the water turns cloudy.
  2. Strain and add the salt.
  3. Drink lukewarm in sips.
Note

Homemade kefir

Fermented milk rich in many kinds of good bacteria and yeasts.

Traditional, no studiesSafe for most peopleInherited folk custom
  • Evidence: There are no studies on its ingredients for this problem; it relies on traditional use.
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Inherited folk custom.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
How much and when? Half to one cup a day.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For two cups)
  • Two spoons of kefir grains
  • Two cups of pasteurised milk
  • A clean glass jar and a plastic sieve
Method
  1. Put the grains in the jar and add the milk.
  2. Cover with a cloth and leave at room temperature for 18–24 hours until it thickens slightly.
  3. Strain, return the grains to fresh milk, and keep the kefir in the fridge.
Note

Cleanliness is essential. Avoid it with very weak immunity or during chemotherapy. Keep it two to three hours apart from the antibiotic dose, especially doxycycline and ciprofloxacin, because milk reduces their absorption.

See a doctor instead of herbs if:

  • Severe watery diarrhoea (3 or more times a day) with abdominal pain or fever, during an antibiotic or up to two months after: it may be bacterial colitis that needs treatment
  • Blood or mucus in the stool
  • A skin rash, facial swelling or difficulty breathing (allergy to the antibiotic): emergency
  • No improvement 48–72 hours after starting the antibiotic
