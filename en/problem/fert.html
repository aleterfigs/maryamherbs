Delayed pregnancy and fertility

These results are for a non-pregnant adult without chronic illness or medicines. In the app you choose your age, condition and medicines to see the blends that suit you.

Delayed pregnancy needs both partners to be examined together by a doctor. The recipes here are healthy food, and the date palm pollen recipe is for men only.
What you both need to know
  • Of every 100 couples trying regularly, about 84 conceive within a year and about 92 within two years.
  • Delay is shared: a male factor is present in about half of cases, so both partners are tested together. A semen test is simple and quick.
  • Intercourse every two to three days throughout the month is better than waiting for a single day.
  • Testosterone supplements and hormones sold in gyms stop sperm production, and recovery can take months.

Authentic (sahih) hadith«Eat olive oil and anoint yourselves with it, for it is from a blessed tree” (Tirmidhi and Ibn Majah; graded authentic by al-Albani).
How much and when? As part of food, several times a week.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For one cup)

Method

  2. For the skin: rub it on cracked hands and feet before bed.
Note

High in calories, so count it within your food.

Sardine salad with lemon and cumin

A small, cheap fish rich in vitamin D, calcium (from its bones), omega-3 and B12.

Moderate evidenceSafe for most peopleInherited folk custom
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Olives and olive oil (Relatively strong).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Inherited folk custom.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
How much and when? Once or twice a week.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For one cup)
  • A tin of sardines in olive oil or water (with bones)
  • Cumin: A pinch of cumin
  • Juice of a lemon, spring onion and parsley
  • Olives and olive oil: A spoon of olive oil
Method
  1. Open the tin and mash the sardines with their soft bones using a fork.
  2. Add the lemon, cumin, onion, parsley and oil.
  3. Eat with bread or salad.
Note

Tinned sardines are salty; people with high blood pressure should choose low-salt.

Dates with almonds or walnuts

How much and when? 3 dates between meals or before exercise.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For one cup)
  • Dates and fresh dates (ajwa): 3 dates
  • 3 An almond or half a walnut per date
Method
  1. Remove the stone and put an almond or piece of walnut in its place.

For young children: ground nuts only. Avoid with nut allergy.

See a doctor instead of herbs if:

  • Severe pain on one side of the lower abdomen with a late period or bleeding: it may be an ectopic pregnancy, an emergency
  • A year of trying without pregnancy, or 6 months if the wife is 35 or older
  • Irregular or absent periods, or severe period pain
  • Two or more miscarriages
  • Pain or swelling in a testicle, or erection or ejaculation problems
