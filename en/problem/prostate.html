Pumpkin-seed snack

A daily snack that supports prostate health with zinc and magnesium.

Limited evidenceSafe for most peopleInherited folk custom
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Pumpkin seeds (Limited).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Inherited folk custom.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
How much and when? One portion a day; can be continued.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For one portion)
  • Pumpkin seeds: A quarter cup of shelled pumpkin seeds
  • Cumin: A pinch of cumin
  • A small pinch of salt (optional)
Method
  1. Toast the seeds in a dry pan over a medium heat for 3–4 minutes, stirring.
  2. Sprinkle with cumin and leave to cool.
  3. Eat as a snack or add to salad.
Note

It does not replace regular prostate checks after forty or fifty as your doctor advises, especially with difficulty passing urine or blood in the urine.

🌿 From traditional medicine; most of its recipes have not been tested in large studies. Choose organic or clean local ingredients from a trusted source where you can, and if you do not improve, see a doctor.

Pumpkin-seed and garlic salad

A zinc-rich meal to support men's health.

Limited evidenceSafe for most peopleModern home recipe
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Pumpkin seeds (Limited).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Modern home recipe.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
How much and when? As part of food, several times a week.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For one cup)
  • Pumpkin seeds: Two tablespoons of pumpkin seeds
  • Garlic: One crushed garlic clove
  • Leafy greens and olive oil
Method
  1. Lightly toast the seeds.
  2. Mix the garlic with the oil.
  3. Add everything to the greens.
Note

Difficulty passing urine or blood in the urine needs a doctor.

Nettle-root decoction for the prostate

A European use for mild prostate symptoms.

Limited evidenceNeeds caution for some groupsInherited folk custom
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Nettle (Limited).
  • Safety: Nettle: not suitable for some situations (pregnancy, breastfeeding, illnesses or medicines). Use «Suggest a blend» to find out whether it suits you.
  • Source: Inherited folk custom.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
How much and when? One cup a day for 6–8 weeks, then see the doctor.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For one cup)
  • Nettle: One teaspoon of nettle root
  • A cup of water
Method
  1. Boil the root for 10 minutes.
  2. Strain; one cup a day.
Note

Have the prostate checked by a doctor first.
