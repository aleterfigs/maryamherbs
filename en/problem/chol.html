Prophetic medicine

Olive oil to eat and to anoint

Daily use of the oil in food and on dry skin.

Relatively strong evidenceSafe for most peopleAuthentic (sahih) hadith
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Olives and olive oil (Relatively strong).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Authentic (sahih) hadith.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
Authentic (sahih) hadith«Eat olive oil and anoint yourselves with it, for it is from a blessed tree” (Tirmidhi and Ibn Majah; graded authentic by al-Albani).
How much and when? As part of food, several times a week.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For one cup)
  • Olives and olive oil: One tablespoon of extra-virgin olive oil
Method
  1. Add it to salad or bread daily.
  2. For the skin: rub it on cracked hands and feet before bed.
Note

High in calories, so count it within your food.

The same fibre regulates stools in both cases.

Relatively strong evidenceSafe for most peopleClassical medical books
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Psyllium (Relatively strong).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Classical medical books.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
How much and when? Once or twice a day with plenty of water; can be continued for long periods.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For one cup)
  • Psyllium (ispaghula): One teaspoon of psyllium husk
  • A full cup of water
Method
  1. Mix the psyllium with the water and stir well.
  2. Drink at once before it sets, then another cup of water.
  3. Once or twice a day.
Note

Never take without enough water, and keep two hours apart from medicines.

Talbina

Barley porridge with milk and honey, for the sick, the sad and the tired.

Moderate evidenceNeeds caution for some groupsAuthentic (sahih) hadith
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Barley (Moderate).
  • Safety: Barley: not suitable for some situations (pregnancy, breastfeeding, illnesses or medicines). Use «Suggest a blend» to find out whether it suits you.
  • Source: Authentic (sahih) hadith.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
Authentic (sahih) hadith«Talbina gives rest to the heart of the sick and takes away some of the grief” (Bukhari, from Aisha, may God be pleased with her). «Gives rest» means it soothes and revives.
How much and when? A bowl when ill, sad or tired, or several times a week.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For one cup)
  • Barley: Two tablespoons of barley flour (wholegrain preferred)
  • One and a half cups of milk or water
  • Honey: One teaspoon of honey
Method
  1. Mix the barley flour with a little cold milk so it does not go lumpy.
  2. Add the rest of the milk and heat over a low heat, stirring, for 10–15 minutes until it is like a soup.
  3. Take off the heat and sweeten with honey once lukewarm.
Note

Not suitable for people with coeliac disease. For children under one, no honey.

Artichoke-leaf infusion

A bitter tea drunk before rich meals.

Limited evidenceNeeds caution for some groupsInherited folk custom

How much and when? One cup once or twice a day; can be continued 4–6 weeks, then a week's break.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For one cup)
  • Artichoke: One teaspoon of dried artichoke leaves
  • A cup of boiling water
Method
  1. Steep it for 10 minutes.
  2. Strain and drink half an hour before the meal.
Note

Avoid with gallstones and daisy-family allergy.
