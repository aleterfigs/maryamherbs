Urinary incontinence

These results are for a non-pregnant adult without chronic illness or medicines. In the app you choose your age, condition and medicines to see the blends that suit you.

The basics are pelvic floor exercises and bladder training, and no herb replaces them. Incontinence is common and treatable, so do not be shy with your doctor.
Types
  • Stress incontinence: leaking with coughing, sneezing, laughing or lifting. More common in women after childbirth and after menopause.
  • Urge incontinence: a sudden strong urge you cannot delay, sometimes with an overactive bladder.
  • In men: usually with an enlarged prostate or after prostate surgery.
  • People with incontinence have eased rulings for purification and prayer; ask scholars about them.
What actually helps
  • Relatively strong Pelvic floor exercises (Kegel): tighten the muscles you use to hold in urine and wind, hold for 10 seconds, then relax for 10 seconds. Repeat 10 times, three times a day, for at least 3 months. Do not practise while urinating.
  • Relatively strong Losing weight for anyone overweight, reduces pressure on the bladder.
  • Moderate Bladder training: urinate at fixed times, then gradually lengthen the time between them.
  • Moderate Less coffee, tea, fizzy drinks and energy drinks, as they irritate the bladder.
  • Do not cut water too much: concentrated urine irritates the bladder, and in the Gulf heat it raises the risk of stones. Drink less only in the two hours before bed.
  • Treating constipation, and stopping smoking, as a chronic cough worsens incontinence.
Herbs and incontinence
  • Limited Pumpkin seeds: small trials of the extract in overactive bladder. Eating them is safe, but the effect is uncertain.
  • No herb strengthens the pelvic floor muscles; exercise is the basis.
Myths about incontinence
  • «Incontinence is normal with age or after childbirth and cannot be treated»: it is common but not normal, and most cases improve with exercises and treatment.
  • «Drink less water and the problem is solved»: it irritates the bladder more.
  • «It is too embarrassing to tell the doctor»: doctors see it every day, and silence delays simple treatment.

Common home recipes

Oat porridge with flax and dates

A breakfast that lowers cholesterol and eases the bowels.

Relatively strong evidenceSafe for most peopleModern home recipe
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Oats (Relatively strong).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Modern home recipe.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
How much and when? As part of food, several times a week.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For one cup)
  • Oats: Half a cup of oats
  • Flaxseed (linseed): One teaspoon of ground flax
  • Dates and fresh dates (ajwa): Two chopped dates
  • A cup of milk or water
Method
  1. Cook the oats in the milk for 5 minutes.
  2. Add the flax and dates.
  3. Serve warm.
Note

For diabetics: one date.

🌿 From traditional medicine; most of its recipes have not been tested in large studies. Choose organic or clean local ingredients from a trusted source where you can, and if you do not improve, see a doctor.

Date and pumpkin-seed balls

A snack with zinc from pumpkin seeds and energy from dates.

Limited evidenceSafe for most peopleModern home recipe
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Dates and fresh dates (Limited).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Modern home recipe.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
How much and when? Two balls a day.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For twenty balls)
  • Dates and fresh dates (ajwa): A cup of pitted dates
  • Pumpkin seeds: Half a cup of ground pumpkin seeds
  • A quarter cup of oats
  • Cinnamon: A pinch of cinnamon
Method
  1. Blitz the ingredients in a food processor until they hold together.
  2. Roll into small balls.
  3. Keeps in the fridge for a week.
Note

For young children: finely ground seeds.

See a doctor instead of herbs if:

  • Blood in the urine
  • New incontinence with burning or fever: it may be an infection
  • Sudden incontinence with leg weakness, numbness around the buttocks or severe back pain: an emergency
  • In men: difficulty urinating, constant dribbling or not emptying the bladder
  • Incontinence that is changing your life: there is effective treatment, so do not be shy with your doctor
