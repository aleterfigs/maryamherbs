Weight loss

These results are for a non-pregnant adult without chronic illness or medicines. In the app you choose your age, condition and medicines to see the blends that suit you.

No herb meaningfully reduces weight on its own. What actually works: cutting sugars and sweetened drinks, fibre, activity and enough sleep. Beware «herbal slimming products»: many on the market are adulterated with banned drugs such as sibutramine, which harms the heart, and «slimming tea» is usually a laxative (senna) that makes you lose water, not fat.

Common home recipes

Oat porridge with flax and dates

A breakfast that lowers cholesterol and eases the bowels.

Relatively strong evidenceSafe for most peopleModern home recipe
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Oats (Relatively strong).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Modern home recipe.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
How much and when? As part of food, several times a week.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For one cup)
  • Oats: Half a cup of oats
  • Flaxseed (linseed): One teaspoon of ground flax
  • Dates and fresh dates (ajwa): Two chopped dates
  • A cup of milk or water
Method
  1. Cook the oats in the milk for 5 minutes.
  2. Add the flax and dates.
  3. Serve warm.
Note

For diabetics: one date.

🌿 From traditional medicine; most of its recipes have not been tested in large studies. Choose organic or clean local ingredients from a trusted source where you can, and if you do not improve, see a doctor.

Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For one cup)

Green tea instead of sweetened drinks

Replacing fizzy drinks and juices with unsweetened green tea saves hundreds of calories.

Moderate evidenceSafe for most peopleModern home recipe
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Green tea (Moderate).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Modern home recipe.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
How much and when? One to two cups a day; not in the evening.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For one cup)
  • Green tea (matcha and sencha): One teaspoon of green tea
  • Mint: 4 to 5 mint leaves (optional)
  • A cup of water at 75 °C
Method
  1. Steep the tea for two minutes only.
  2. Add the mint; drink without sugar, hot or iced.
Note

Its direct effect on burning fat is tiny; its benefit is in replacing sugary drinks. Not in the evening.

Prophetic medicine

The rule of thirds in eating

A Prophetic measure for food: a third for food, a third for drink and a third for breath — it prevents overeating and helps with weight control.

Traditional, no studiesSafe for most peopleAuthentic (sahih) hadith
  • Evidence: There are no studies on its ingredients for this problem; it relies on traditional use.
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Authentic (sahih) hadith.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
Authentic (sahih) hadith«No human fills a vessel worse than his stomach. A few morsels to keep his back straight are enough for the son of Adam; if he must, then a third for his food, a third for his drink and a third for his breath” (Tirmidhi and Ibn Majah; Tirmidhi said: good and authentic).
How much and when? At every meal.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For one cup)
  • A medium-sized plate
  • A cup of water
Method
  1. Begin with Bismillah, eat with your right hand from what is near you, and eat slowly.
  2. Serve an amount that fills about a third of the stomach, and sip water calmly.
  3. Stop before you are full, and leave a third for your breath.
Note

People with diabetes follow their doctor's plan for amounts and meal times.

🌿 From traditional medicine; most of its recipes have not been tested in large studies. Choose organic or clean local ingredients from a trusted source where you can, and if you do not improve, see a doctor.

See a doctor instead of herbs if:

  • Rapid weight loss without a clear reason
  • Constant preoccupation with weight and fear of eating, or vomiting after eating: these are signs of an eating disorder and need a specialist
  • Palpitations, insomnia or raised blood pressure after a slimming product: stop it immediately and see a doctor
  • Obesity with diabetes or high blood pressure: needs a doctor's follow-up
