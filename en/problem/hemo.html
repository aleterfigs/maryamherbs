Haemorrhoids

These results are for a non-pregnant adult without chronic illness or medicines. In the app you choose your age, condition and medicines to see the blends that suit you.

6 suitable blends

Arab-Islamic medicine

Psyllium for diarrhoea and constipation

The same fibre regulates stools in both cases.

Relatively strong evidenceSafe for most peopleClassical medical books
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Psyllium (Relatively strong).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Classical medical books.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
How much and when? Once or twice a day with plenty of water; can be continued for long periods.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For one cup)
  • Psyllium (ispaghula): One teaspoon of psyllium husk
  • A full cup of water
Method
  1. Mix the psyllium with the water and stir well.
  2. Drink at once before it sets, then another cup of water.
  3. Once or twice a day.
Note

Never take without enough water, and keep two hours apart from medicines.

🌿 From traditional medicine; most of its recipes have not been tested in large studies. Choose organic or clean local ingredients from a trusted source where you can, and if you do not improve, see a doctor.

The Levant

Cooked mallow

A popular dish in the Levant, Egypt and Morocco, gentle on the bowel.

Moderate evidenceSafe for most peopleInherited folk custom
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Olives and olive oil (Relatively strong).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Inherited folk custom.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
How much and when? As part of food, several times a week.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For one dish)
  • Mallow (khubbeiza): A bunch of chopped mallow
  • Olives and olive oil: Two spoons of olive oil
  • An onion, garlic and lemon
Method
  1. Fry the onion and garlic in the oil.
  2. Add the mallow and stir until it wilts (10 minutes).
  3. Add the salt and lemon.
Note

Safe as food.

Common home recipes

Chamomile sitz bath

Sitting in warm water eases the pain of haemorrhoids and anal fissures.

Traditional, no studiesSafe for most peopleModern home recipe
  • Evidence: There are no studies on its ingredients for this problem; it relies on traditional use.
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Modern home recipe.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
How much and when? 2–3 times a day and after a bowel movement until the pain eases.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For one cup)
  • Chamomile: 3 tablespoons of chamomile
  • A sitz basin with warm water
Method
  1. Steep the chamomile in a litre of boiling water for 10 minutes, then add it to the warm water in the basin.
  2. Sit in it for 10–15 minutes.
  3. Pat dry; 2–3 times a day.
Note

Continuous bleeding or severe pain needs a doctor.

Common home recipes

Sitz bath after childbirth

Sitting in warm water eases the pain of stitches and swelling after a vaginal birth.

Traditional, no studiesSafe for most peopleModern home recipe
  • Evidence: There are no studies on its ingredients for this problem; it relies on traditional use.
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Modern home recipe.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
How much and when? 2–3 times a day in the first two weeks.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For one cup)
  • A sitz basin with clean warm water
  • Chamomile: Chamomile infusion (optional)
Method
  1. Fill the basin with comfortably warm water.
  2. Sit in it for 10–15 minutes.
  3. Pat dry with a clean towel.
Note

Do not put herbs or oils inside the vagina. Foul-smelling discharge or fever needs a doctor.

See a doctor instead of herbs if:

  • Heavy or continuous bleeding
  • Dark blood or blood mixed with the stool (not just bright red blood on the paper)
  • A change in bowel habits after forty or with weight loss
  • Severe pain with a hard blue lump (it may be a clot in the haemorrhoid)
