Kidney stones and preventing them

These results are for a non-pregnant adult without chronic illness or medicines. In the app you choose your age, condition and medicines to see the blends that suit you.

What really prevents stones
  • Relatively strong Water: drink enough to pass 2–2.5 litres of urine a day, enough to keep your urine pale. In the Gulf heat you need more.
  • Relatively strong Cutting salt and processed and tinned foods.
  • Moderate Lemons and oranges: are rich in citrate, which prevents stones from forming.
  • Moderate Less red meat and excess animal protein.
  • Moderate Fewer sweetened and fizzy drinks.
  • A healthy weight and regular activity.
Common myths
  • «Milk causes stones»: not true; calcium in food reduces oxalate stones because it binds to oxalate in the gut. Do not cut out dairy.
  • «Drinks that dissolve stones»: no herb has been shown to dissolve existing stones.
  • «Lots of vitamin C is good»: high-dose supplements increase oxalate and stones.
  • «Lots of parsley cleans the kidneys»: there is no evidence for this, and medicinal amounts are avoided with kidney disease.
If you have oxalate stones
  • Be moderate with spinach, beetroot, nuts, strong tea and chocolate, without cutting them out completely.
  • Eat oxalate-rich foods with a source of calcium such as yoghurt.
  • Analysing a stone after it passes tells its type and the right diet.

Common home recipes

Lemon water to prevent stones

Lemon juice is rich in citrate, which prevents some kinds of stones from forming; studies support it.

Moderate evidenceSafe for most peopleModern home recipe
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Mint (Relatively strong).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Modern home recipe.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
How much and when? Daily, through the day.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For two litres)
  • Half a cup of fresh lemon juice
  • Two litres of water
  • Mint: 4 to 5 mint leaves (optional)
Method
  1. Mix the lemon juice with the water in a large bottle.
  2. Drink it through the day, along with the rest of your water.
Note

Drink through a straw and rinse your mouth to protect the teeth. People with reflux may reduce the amount.

🌿 From traditional medicine; most of its recipes have not been tested in large studies. Choose organic or clean local ingredients from a trusted source where you can, and if you do not improve, see a doctor.

Ingredients (For one litre)

  • A lemon, sliced
  • A cucumber, sliced

Arab-Islamic medicine

Barley water

One of the oldest drinks in Arab and Greek medicine, for the sick, the feverish and the thirsty.

Moderate evidenceNeeds caution for some groupsClassical medical books
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Barley (Moderate).
  • Safety: Barley: not suitable for some situations (pregnancy, breastfeeding, illnesses or medicines). Use «Suggest a blend» to find out whether it suits you.
  • Source: Classical medical books.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
How much and when? One cup once or twice a day; can be continued 4–6 weeks, then a week's break.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For three cups)
  • Barley: A quarter cup of barley grains
  • 4 cups of water
  • Juice of one lemon (optional)
Method
  1. Rinse the barley and simmer in the water for 30–40 minutes.
  2. Strain and add the lemon.
  3. Drink lukewarm or cold through the day.
Note

Contains gluten: not suitable for people with coeliac disease.

Corn-silk tea

A popular Arab diuretic.

Limited evidenceSafe for most peopleInherited folk custom
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Corn silk (Limited).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Inherited folk custom.

How much and when? One cup once or twice a day; can be continued 4–6 weeks, then a week's break.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For one cup)
  • Corn silk: One tablespoon of dried corn silk
  • A cup of boiling water
Method
  1. Steep the silk for 10 minutes.
  2. Strain; one to two cups a day.
  3. Drink plenty of water.
Note

Kidney or heart patients should ask the doctor.

See a doctor instead of herbs if:

  • Severe pain in the side with fever or shivering: emergency immediately; it may be a blocked, infected kidney
  • Vomiting that prevents drinking fluids
  • Visible blood in the urine, or no urine
  • Stone pain during pregnancy
  • Repeated stones: you need a stone and urine analysis to know their type
