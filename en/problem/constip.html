Psyllium for diarrhoea and constipation

The same fibre regulates stools in both cases.

Relatively strong evidenceSafe for most peopleClassical medical books
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Psyllium (Relatively strong).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Classical medical books.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
How much and when? Once or twice a day with plenty of water; can be continued for long periods.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For one cup)
  • Psyllium (ispaghula): One teaspoon of psyllium husk
  • A full cup of water
Method
  1. Mix the psyllium with the water and stir well.
  2. Drink at once before it sets, then another cup of water.
  3. Once or twice a day.
Note

Never take without enough water, and keep two hours apart from medicines.

Sudan

Gum water (gum arabic soak)

A traditional Sudanese drink rich in soluble fibre for the gut and digestion.

Moderate evidenceSafe for most peopleInherited folk custom
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Gum arabic (Moderate).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Inherited folk custom.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
How much and when? One cup once or twice a day; can be continued 4–6 weeks, then a week's break.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For one cup)
  • Gum arabic (hashab): 1 tablespoon gum arabic lumps (about 10 grams)
  • Two cups of water
Method
  1. Rinse the lumps quickly with cold water.
  2. Soak them in the water overnight in a covered container, until they dissolve and the water turns thick.
  3. Stir well and drink in the morning; you can add a squeeze of lemon.
  4. Start with half the amount for a week, then increase gradually.
Note

Take it 2 hours apart from medicines. Bloating in the first days is common and usually eases within a week. Kidney patients should only start it with their doctor's approval.

🌿 From traditional medicine; most of its recipes have not been tested in large studies. Choose organic or clean local ingredients from a trusted source where you can, and if you do not improve, see a doctor.

For occasional constipation only, and for a very short time.

Traditional, no studiesShort-term or supervised useInherited folk custom
  • Evidence: There are no studies on its ingredients for this problem; it relies on traditional use.
  • Safety: Senna of Mecca: not for long-term use; best under medical supervision.
  • Source: Inherited folk custom.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
How much and when? Once before bed; no more than 3–5 days in a row, and never more than a week.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For one cup)
  • Senna of Mecca (sanamaki, ushriq): A ready-made senna tea bag (at the amount stated on the pack)
  • A cup of hot water
Method
  1. Steep the bag as per the pack (usually 10 minutes).
  2. Drink at bedtime; it works after 6–12 hours.
  3. Do not repeat it for more than a few days.
Note

A strong laxative: not for more than a week, not with unexplained abdominal pain, and not for pregnant or breastfeeding women or children. Drink plenty of water.
