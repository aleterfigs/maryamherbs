Weight gain

These results are for a non-pregnant adult without chronic illness or medicines. In the app you choose your age, condition and medicines to see the blends that suit you.

Healthy weight gain comes from extra meals rich in energy and protein, such as dates, milk, nuts, tahini and porridges, with strength exercise to build muscle. Beware unknown «weight-gain pills» and «appetite blends»: many contain cortisone or harmful medicines.

14 suitable blends

The Maghreb

Moroccan sellou (sfouf)

A mix of toasted flour, almonds, sesame and spices, served to new mothers in Morocco.

Moderate evidenceSafe for most peopleInherited folk custom
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Fennel (Moderate).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Inherited folk custom.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
How much and when? Two tablespoons with tea in the morning during the postpartum weeks.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For several days)
  • A cup of flour toasted until brown
  • A cup of toasted ground almonds
  • Half a cup of toasted sesame
  • Anise: One teaspoon of ground anise
  • Fennel: One teaspoon of ground fennel
  • Cinnamon: One teaspoon of cinnamon
  • Honey: A quarter cup of honey or melted ghee
Method
  1. Toast the flour in a dry pan, stirring, until brown (20 minutes).
  2. Mix with the almonds, sesame and spices.
  3. Add the honey or ghee until it holds together; serve by the spoonful.
Note

Very high in calories. Avoid with nut or sesame allergy.

Prophetic medicine

Talbina

Barley porridge with milk and honey, for the sick, the sad and the tired.

Moderate evidenceNeeds caution for some groupsAuthentic (sahih) hadith
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Barley (Moderate).
  • Safety: Barley: not suitable for some situations (pregnancy, breastfeeding, illnesses or medicines). Use «Suggest a blend» to find out whether it suits you.
  • Source: Authentic (sahih) hadith.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
Authentic (sahih) hadith«Talbina gives rest to the heart of the sick and takes away some of the grief” (Bukhari, from Aisha, may God be pleased with her). «Gives rest» means it soothes and revives.
How much and when? A bowl when ill, sad or tired, or several times a week.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For one cup)
  • Barley: Two tablespoons of barley flour (wholegrain preferred)
  • One and a half cups of milk or water
  • Honey: One teaspoon of honey
Method
  1. Mix the barley flour with a little cold milk so it does not go lumpy.
  2. Add the rest of the milk and heat over a low heat, stirring, for 10–15 minutes until it is like a soup.
  3. Take off the heat and sweeten with honey once lukewarm.
Note

Not suitable for people with coeliac disease. For children under one, no honey.

Aseeda with ghee and dates

The warm porridge of the Gulf and Yemen, an energy food for the underweight and new mothers.

Limited evidenceSafe for most peopleInherited folk custom
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Dates and fresh dates (Limited).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Inherited folk custom.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
How much and when? A bowl daily or several times a week.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For one bowl)
  • Half a cup of brown wheat flour
  • One and a half cups of water
  • Dates and fresh dates (ajwa): Two spoons of date syrup or mashed dates
  • Ghee (samn baladi): A spoon of local ghee
  • Cardamom: A pinch of cardamom
Method
  1. Boil the water and add the flour gradually, stirring hard, until it becomes a firm dough.
  2. Leave on a low heat for 10 minutes.
  3. Shape it on a plate and make a well in the middle for the date syrup and ghee.
Note

High in calories. Diabetics should use less date syrup.

How much and when? One or two pieces a day.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For a large dish)
  • Dates and fresh dates (ajwa): Two cups of pitted dates
  • A cup of brown wheat flour toasted until golden
  • A quarter cup of ghee
  • Cardamom: One teaspoon of ground cardamom
Method
  1. Toast the flour in a dry pot, stirring, until golden.
  2. Add the ghee and cardamom, then the crumbled dates, and knead while warm.
  3. Press into a dish and cut into pieces.
Note

High in calories and sugar. For diabetics, a small piece.
