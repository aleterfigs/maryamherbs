Common home recipes

Saline nasal rinse

For a blocked nose with colds, sinusitis and seasonal allergy.

Moderate evidenceSafe for most peopleModern home recipe
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Salt water (Moderate).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Modern home recipe.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
How much and when? Once or twice a day when needed.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For one cup)
  • Salt water (sea salt): A quarter teaspoon of pure non-iodised salt
  • A pinch of sodium bicarbonate (optional; reduces stinging)
  • A cup of water boiled for 3 minutes then cooled to lukewarm, or distilled water
Method
  1. Boil the water for 3 minutes and let it cool to lukewarm, or use distilled water.
  2. Dissolve the salt and bicarbonate.
  3. Lean over the sink and tilt your head to one side, and pour the solution with a nasal pot or squeeze bottle into the upper nostril so it comes out of the other, breathing through your open mouth.
  4. Repeat on the other side, then blow the nose gently.
  5. Wash and dry the pot, and make a fresh solution every day.
Note

Do not use tap water without boiling it: it rarely carries an organism that causes a fatal brain infection. For children: a ready-made saline spray from the pharmacy is easier and safer.

🌿 From traditional medicine; most of its recipes have not been tested in large studies. Choose organic or clean local ingredients from a trusted source where you can, and if you do not improve, see a doctor.

Black-seed oil with honey

The common way to take black-seed oil, the most studied form.

Moderate evidenceSafe for most peopleAuthentic (sahih) hadith
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Black seed (Moderate).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Authentic (sahih) hadith.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
Authentic (sahih) hadith«In the black seed is healing for every disease except death” (Bukhari and Muslim).
How much and when? Once a day for 8–12 weeks, then assess the benefit.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For one cup)
  • Black seed (nigella): Half a teaspoon of cold-pressed black-seed oil
  • Honey: One teaspoon of honey
Method
  1. Mix the oil with the honey to soften its sharp taste.
  2. Take after breakfast.
Note

Caution with diabetes and blood-pressure medicines and blood thinners. Do not exceed the amount.

Nettle tea

A traditional European tea for joint pain and seasonal allergies.

Limited evidenceNeeds caution for some groupsInherited folk custom
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Nettle (Limited).
  • Safety: Nettle: not suitable for some situations (pregnancy, breastfeeding, illnesses or medicines). Use «Suggest a blend» to find out whether it suits you.
  • Source: Inherited folk custom.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
How much and when? One cup once or twice a day; can be continued 4–6 weeks, then a week's break.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For one cup)
  • Nettle: One teaspoon of dried nettle
  • A cup of boiling water
Method
  1. Steep the nettle for 10 minutes.
  2. Strain.
  3. One to two cups a day.
Note

A diuretic. Caution with blood-pressure and diabetes medicines and warfarin; avoid in pregnancy.

Common home recipes

Salt-water nasal rinse

One of the most effective ways to relieve nasal allergy symptoms.

Traditional, no studiesSafe for most peopleModern home recipe
  • Evidence: There are no studies on its ingredients for this problem; it relies on traditional use.
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Modern home recipe.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
How much and when? Once or twice a day in the allergy or cold season.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For one cup)
  • A cup of boiled-then-cooled water (or sterile water)
  • A quarter teaspoon of non-iodised salt
  • A pinch of bicarbonate
Method
  1. Dissolve the salt and bicarbonate in the lukewarm water.
  2. Use a nasal rinse bottle or a neti pot.
  3. Rinse each nostril with the head tilted, then blow the nose gently.
Note

Use only boiled or sterile water, never tap water straight from the tap.

Chamomile steam for nasal allergy

A soothing inhalation for allergic congestion.

Traditional, no studiesSafe for most peopleModern home recipe
  • Evidence: There are no studies on its ingredients for this problem; it relies on traditional use.
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Modern home recipe.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
How much and when? A 5–10-minute session, once or twice a day, for a few days only.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For one cup)
  • Chamomile: 3 tablespoons of chamomile
  • A bowl of hot water
Method
  1. Steep the chamomile in hot (not boiling) water.
  2. Cover your head with a towel and inhale for 5–10 minutes.
Note

Risk of burns. Avoid with asthma and daisy-family allergy.

🌿 From traditional medicine; most of its recipes have not been tested in large studies. Choose organic or clean local ingredients from a trusted source where you can, and if you do not improve, see a doctor.

See a doctor instead of herbs if:

  • Wheezing or shortness of breath (it may be asthma)
  • Swelling of the face or lips, or difficulty swallowing: emergency immediately
  • Symptoms that disturb sleep and work: see a doctor
