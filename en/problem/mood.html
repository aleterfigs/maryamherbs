Anxiety and low mood

These results are for a non-pregnant adult without chronic illness or medicines. In the app you choose your age, condition and medicines to see the blends that suit you.

The recipes here are gentle calming drinks, not a treatment for depression. If sadness lasts more than two weeks or affects your life, talking to a professional helps most.
What really helps
  • Relatively strong Talking to a mental health professional: talking therapy, such as cognitive behavioural therapy, is effective for anxiety and mild to moderate depression.
  • Relatively strong Movement: walking 30 minutes on most days of the week improves mood and anxiety.
  • Moderate Regular sleep, sunlight, and staying in touch with family and friends.
  • Moderate Less coffee, tea and energy drinks for anyone with anxiety or palpitations.
  • ﴿Verily, in the remembrance of Allah do hearts find rest﴾ (Ar-Ra'd: 28). Prayer, dhikr and supplication bring calm, alongside taking the means and treatment.
Herbs and mood
  • Moderate Saffron: many trials of its extract (30 mg a day) found it improves mild to moderate depression. Food amounts are much smaller, and large amounts are avoided in pregnancy.
  • Moderate Lavender: standardised lavender oil capsules have trials in anxiety. The tea has less evidence, and the concentrated oil must not be swallowed.
  • Limited Chamomile: a trial of its extract for generalised anxiety; the infusion is a gentle calmer.
  • St John's wort: it helped mild depression in trials, but it is one of the herbs with the most dangerous interactions: with antidepressants it can cause serious toxicity, and it undermines birth control pills, blood thinners, heart drugs and transplant drugs. Never combine it with any medicine without a doctor.
For anyone taking psychiatric medicine
  • Do not stop the medicine suddenly, and do not replace it with a herb without your doctor.
  • Tell your doctor about every herb or supplement, especially St John's wort, saffron, kava and valerian.
  • Choose «Sedatives, sleeping pills or antidepressants» in the «For more accurate results» box so the assistant excludes what conflicts with them.
Myths about mental health
  • «Depression is weak faith»: depression is an illness that affects believers and others alike, and treating it is part of taking the means.
  • «Keep busy and you will forget»: being busy helps passing sadness, but depression needs treatment.
  • «Every psychiatric medicine is addictive»: most antidepressants are not addictive, and the doctor decides what suits you.
  • «Herbs are natural, so they are safe with psychiatric medicines»: some are dangerous with them, such as St John's wort.

Lavender and chamomile infusion

To relax before sleep after a tiring day.

Moderate evidenceSafe for most peopleModern home recipe
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Lavender (Moderate).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Modern home recipe.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
How much and when? One cup in the evening an hour before bed; can be drunk daily with a week's break each month.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For one cup)
  • Lavender: Half a teaspoon of lavender flowers
  • Chamomile: One teaspoon of chamomile
Method
  1. Steep the herbs in a covered cup of hot water for 8 minutes.
  2. Strain.
  3. Drink an hour before bed.
Note

May increase drowsiness with sedatives.

🌿 From traditional medicine; most of its recipes have not been tested in large studies. Choose organic or clean local ingredients from a trusted source where you can, and if you do not improve, see a doctor.

Saffron and cardamom milk

A calming Gulf evening drink.

Moderate evidenceSafe for most peopleInherited folk custom
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Saffron (Moderate).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Inherited folk custom.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
How much and when? One cup in the evening an hour before bed; can be drunk daily with a week's break each month.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For one cup)
  • Saffron: 4–5 Soaked saffron threads
  • Cardamom: One crushed cardamom pod
  • A cup of milk
  • Honey: One teaspoon of honey
Method
  1. Warm the milk with the cardamom.
  2. Add the soaked saffron and leave for two minutes.
  3. Sweeten with honey and drink before bed.
Note

Pregnant women stick to food amounts of saffron.

See a doctor instead of herbs if:

  • Thoughts of self-harm or suicide: seek help immediately from emergency services (998 in the UAE, or your local ambulance number if you are elsewhere), or from a doctor or someone you trust
  • Sadness or loss of enjoyment for more than two weeks that affects work or home
  • Repeated panic attacks, or anxiety that stops you going out or working
  • A big change in sleep, appetite or weight
  • After childbirth: deep sadness, or thoughts of harming the baby or yourself: see a doctor immediately
  • Hearing voices or having strange thoughts that people around you do not share
