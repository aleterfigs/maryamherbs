Salt-water gargle

For a sore throat and mouth ulcers.

Moderate evidenceSafe for most peopleModern home recipe
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Salt water (Moderate).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Modern home recipe.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
How much and when? 2–3 times a day without swallowing, for a few days until it improves.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For one cup)
  • Salt water (sea salt): A quarter to half a teaspoon of salt
  • A cup of warm (not hot) water
Method
  1. Dissolve the salt in the warm water.
  2. Gargle a small mouthful for 30 seconds, then spit it out.
  3. Repeat 3–4 times a day.
Note

Do not swallow the water. For children aged 6 and over only, when the child can spit without swallowing.

How much and when? One cup 2–3 times a day until the symptoms go, for a week at most; if no better, see a doctor.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For one cup)
  • Ginger: 3–4 Slices of fresh ginger
  • Cinnamon: A small cinnamon stick (Ceylon preferred)
  • Juice of half a lemon
  • One teaspoon of honey
Method
  1. Simmer the ginger and cinnamon in one and a half cups of water over a low heat for 10 minutes.
  2. Strain and let it cool a little.
  3. Add the lemon and honey once it is warm, not boiling.
Note

No honey for children under one year. See a doctor if the fever lasts more than 3 days or comes with shortness of breath.

Traditional, no studiesSafe for most peopleInherited folk custom
  • Evidence: There are no studies on its ingredients for this problem; it relies on traditional use.
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Inherited folk custom.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
How much and when? 2–3 times a day without swallowing, for a few days until it improves.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For one cup)
  • Turmeric: Half a teaspoon of turmeric
  • Half a teaspoon of salt
  • A cup of warm water
Method
  1. Dissolve the turmeric and salt in the warm water.
  2. Gargle 3 times a day and do not swallow.
Note

May stain the teeth temporarily.

🌿 From traditional medicine; most of its recipes have not been tested in large studies. Choose organic or clean local ingredients from a trusted source where you can, and if you do not improve, see a doctor.

See a doctor instead of herbs if:

  • Difficulty swallowing, breathing or opening the mouth
  • Swelling in the neck or a high fever
  • Pain lasting more than a week
