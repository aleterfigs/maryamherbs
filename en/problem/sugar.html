Blood sugar support

These results are for a non-pregnant adult without chronic illness or medicines. In the app you choose your age, condition and medicines to see the blends that suit you.

What really controls blood sugar
  • Relatively strong Follow-up with your doctor and blood tests, and not stopping prescribed treatment.
  • Relatively strong Walking after meals 10–15 minutes.
  • Relatively strong Cutting sugars, sweetened drinks and white bread, with half the plate vegetables.
  • Relatively strong Losing weight even 5–10%.
  • Moderate Fibre: oats, barley, pulses and psyllium.
  • Moderate Fenugreek, and apple cider vinegar with a meal: a small effect.
  • Limited Cinnamon: conflicting results.
Important warnings
  • Do not stop diabetes medicine or insulin to try a herb.
  • Herbs that lower blood sugar may cause a dangerous low with medicine; check your readings.
  • Dangerous folk «diabetes cures» include colocynth, ja'dah and shih. See their pages.
  • Dates and honey are natural sugar: the amount is what matters.
  • Fasting in Ramadan with diabetes needs a plan from the doctor before the month.

Walking after meals

A light 10–15-minute walk after eating clearly lowers the rise in blood sugar after the meal — one of the most powerful things you can do.

Traditional, no studiesSafe for most peopleModern home recipe
  • Evidence: There are no studies on its ingredients for this problem; it relies on traditional use.
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Modern home recipe.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
How much and when? After every main meal, daily.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For one cup)
  • Comfortable shoes
  • 10–15 minutes after the main meals
Method
  1. Start walking within half an hour of finishing the meal.
  2. Walk at a moderate pace — indoors or in a shopping mall in the heat.
  3. Make it a habit after lunch and dinner.
Note

With diabetes medicines that cause lows, carry a date or a juice with you.

🌿 From traditional medicine; most of its recipes have not been tested in large studies. Choose organic or clean local ingredients from a trusted source where you can, and if you do not improve, see a doctor.

Vinegar with oil (“the best condiment”)

A simple condiment; studies suggest vinegar with a meal may soften the rise in blood sugar afterwards.

Authentic (sahih) hadith«What an excellent condiment vinegar is” (Muslim).
How much and when? As part of food, several times a week.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For one cup)
  • Olives and olive oil: One tablespoon of olive oil
  • One tablespoon of apple or grape vinegar
  • Bread or salad
Method
  1. Mix the vinegar with the oil.
  2. Dip bread in it or pour over salad.
Note

Vinegar is acidic: never drink it neat, and avoid too much with stomach ulcers or reflux.

Sencha green tea

Japan's everyday tea, made with water below boiling.

Moderate evidenceSafe for most peopleInherited folk custom
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Green tea (Moderate).
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Inherited folk custom.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
How much and when? One to two cups a day; not in the evening.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For one cup)
  • Green tea (matcha and sencha): One teaspoon of sencha
  • A cup of water at about 75 °C
Method
  1. Boil the water and let it cool for two minutes.
  2. Steep the tea for one and a half to two minutes only.
  3. Strain; the same leaves can be steeped a second time.
Note

Contains caffeine: not in the evening, and drink between meals, not with them.

How much and when? Once a day in the morning for 4–6 weeks, then a week's break.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For one cup)
  • Fenugreek: One teaspoon of fenugreek seeds
  • A cup of water
Method
  1. Soak the seeds in the water overnight.
  2. Drink the water in the morning and eat the softened seeds.
  3. Drink enough water during the day.
Note

Avoid in pregnancy. Keep two hours apart from your medicines, as it may reduce their absorption.
