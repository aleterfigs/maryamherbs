Breastfeeding

These results are for a non-pregnant adult without chronic illness or medicines. In the app you choose your age, condition and medicines to see the blends that suit you.

The Gulf

Fenugreek with dates for new mothers

A traditional Gulf recipe to strengthen women after childbirth.

Moderate evidenceNeeds caution for some groupsInherited folk custom
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Fenugreek (Moderate).
  • Safety: Fenugreek: not suitable for some situations (pregnancy, breastfeeding, illnesses or medicines). Use «Suggest a blend» to find out whether it suits you.
  • Source: Inherited folk custom.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
How much and when? One cup a day in the first weeks after childbirth.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For one cup)
  • Fenugreek: One teaspoon of ground fenugreek
  • Dates and fresh dates (ajwa): 4 dates, stones removed
  • A cup of milk
Method
  1. Simmer the fenugreek in the milk over a low heat for 5 minutes.
  2. Add the dates and let them soften for two minutes.
  3. Mash and drink warm.
Note

After childbirth only, not during pregnancy. Diabetic mothers should count the dates.

🌿 From traditional medicine; most of its recipes have not been tested in large studies. Choose organic or clean local ingredients from a trusted source where you can, and if you do not improve, see a doctor.

Drunk traditionally to increase milk; the scientific evidence is weak.

How much and when? One to two cups a day while it helps, watching the baby's weight.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For one cup)
  • Fenugreek: One teaspoon of fenugreek
  • A cup of water
  • Honey: One teaspoon of honey
Method
  1. Boil the fenugreek for 5 minutes.
  2. Strain and add the honey.
  3. One to two cups a day.
Note

What increases milk most: frequent feeding, drinking water and rest. It may cause wind in the baby and a distinctive smell to sweat.

The Gulf

Fenugreek madeed for new mothers

A warm Gulf porridge cooked for women after childbirth.

Moderate evidenceNeeds caution for some groupsInherited folk custom
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Fenugreek (Moderate).
  • Safety: Fenugreek: not suitable for some situations (pregnancy, breastfeeding, illnesses or medicines). Use «Suggest a blend» to find out whether it suits you.
  • Source: Inherited folk custom.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
How much and when? A bowl a day in the first weeks after childbirth.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For one bowl)
  • Fenugreek: One teaspoon of ground fenugreek
  • Two tablespoons of brown wheat flour
  • One and a half cups of milk or water
  • Dates and fresh dates (ajwa): 3 dates or a spoon of date syrup
  • Cardamom: A pinch of cardamom
Method
  1. Toast the flour in a dry pot until it colours slightly.
  2. Add the milk gradually, stirring, then the fenugreek and cardamom.
  3. Cook for 10 minutes until thick and add the dates or date syrup.
Note

After childbirth only; forbidden in pregnancy.

Southeast Asia

Moringa-leaf soup (tinola)

Chicken soup with ginger and moringa leaves, cooked in the Philippines for nursing mothers and the sick.

Moderate evidenceNeeds caution for some groupsInherited folk custom
  • Evidence: Strongest ingredient for this problem: Ginger (Relatively strong).
  • Safety: Moringa: not suitable for some situations (pregnancy, breastfeeding, illnesses or medicines). Use «Suggest a blend» to find out whether it suits you.
  • Source: Inherited folk custom.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
How much and when? As part of food, several times a week.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For four people)
  • Moringa (ban, shu', yusr): A handful of fresh moringa leaves or two spoons of powder
  • Ginger: A piece of ginger, sliced
  • Chicken pieces, an onion, garlic and 5 cups of water
Method
  1. Lightly fry the onion, garlic and ginger.
  2. Add the chicken and water and cook for 40 minutes.
  3. Add the moringa leaves in the last two minutes.
Note

Pregnant women should ask their doctor. Caution with diabetes, blood-pressure and thyroid medicines.

Cabbage leaves for breast engorgement

A recipe used worldwide; small studies suggest it eases the pain of breast engorgement.

Traditional, no studiesSafe for most peopleModern home recipe
  • Evidence: There are no studies on its ingredients for this problem; it relies on traditional use.
  • Safety: No common contraindications are known for its ingredients at the stated amounts.
  • Source: Modern home recipe.
  • An estimated rating based on the blend's ingredients, since the blend itself has usually not been tested as a combination.
How much and when? 2–3 times a day, on engorged days only.
Ingredients and method
Ingredients (For one cup)
  • Green cabbage leaves, washed and chilled
Method
  1. Roll a rolling pin over the leaf veins to soften them.
  2. Place inside the bra around the breast (not over the nipple).
  3. Remove after 20 minutes or when they wilt.
Note

Keep breastfeeding or pumping. Redness with fever needs a doctor (mastitis).

See a doctor instead of herbs if:

  • The baby is not gaining weight or is passing less urine (fewer than 6 wet nappies a day after the first week)
  • A red, hot, painful breast with fever (mastitis)
  • The best help: a breastfeeding specialist
