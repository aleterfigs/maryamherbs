Strengthening immunity

These results are for a non-pregnant adult without chronic illness or medicines. In the app you choose your age, condition and medicines to see the blends that suit you.

What really strengthens immunity
  • Relatively strong Enough sleep: 7–9 hours for adults, and more for children.
  • Relatively strong Handwashing with soap and water.
  • Relatively strong A balanced diet with protein, vegetables, colourful fruit and whole grains.
  • Moderate Correcting deficiencies in vitamin D, zinc and iron after testing.
  • Moderate Moderate exercise , less stress and quitting smoking.
Herbs and colds
  • Relatively strong Honey to soothe cough.
  • Moderate Elderberry syrup may shorten flu and colds slightly.
  • Limited Echinacea: conflicting results.
  • Limited Garlic: few studies.
Myths and warnings
  • No food or herb «boosts immunity» above normal in a healthy person; the aim is for immunity to work well, not to «increase».
  • High doses of vitamin C do not prevent colds in most people, though they may shorten them slightly.
  • Immune stimulants such as echinacea, ashwagandha and elderberry may harm people with autoimmune disease or organ transplants.
